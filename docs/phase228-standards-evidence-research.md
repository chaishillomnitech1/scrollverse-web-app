# Phase 228 — Standards & Evidence Commons Research Register

> **Purpose.** This record maps public systems that use different forms of review, authorization, quality assurance, and recordkeeping. It is educational context for ScrollVerse. It does not make ScrollVerse an accreditor, regulator, licensing authority, court, medical authority, financial institution, or official validator.

| Domain | Public source pattern | What it can support | What it does not establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Education | The U.S. Department of Education provides oversight information about recognized accrediting agencies, while private accreditors perform the accreditation relationship. [1] | An institution or program’s reported status within the relevant system. | Institutional ranking, universal quality, admission, learner outcome, or a right for ScrollVerse to issue a credential. |
| Science and technology | NIST describes itself as the United States’ national metrology institute and publishes standards and measurement resources. [2] | Measurement, standards, testing, and methodology context. | Peer-review status, product security, legal compliance, or validity of every downstream claim. |
| Medicine | FDA describes regulatory requirements that can include device classification, registration, listing, premarket pathways, quality systems, labeling, and reporting. [3] | A bounded explanation of regulatory pathways and public FDA context. | Medical advice, diagnosis, a product authorization, or a claim that ScrollVerse has regulatory status. |
| Law | The ABA Council reports approval status for specified law schools and expressly distinguishes approval status from school rankings. [4] | A source-labeled illustration of professional education approval. | Legal advice, court authority, bar admission, or a decision in an individual matter. |
| Finance | The SEC’s mission addresses investor protection, fair/efficient markets, and capital formation; it distinguishes disclosure and regulated-market roles. [5] | Public regulatory-learning context and disclosure concepts. | Investment advice, a securities offering determination, token legality, or approval of a financial activity. |
| Government audit | GAO’s Yellow Book sets standards for government audits, attestation engagements, and performance audits. [6] | A model for distinguishing audit type, scope, criteria, independence, and reporting. | A ScrollVerse audit opinion, government authorization, or assurance about a private project. |
| Archives and cultural records | NARA presents federal research records and the Library of Congress provides structured digital collections. [7] [8] | Public-record discovery and collection/provenance context. | Ownership, complete provenance, rights clearance, current accuracy, or a causal claim. |

## First-Party Evidence Pattern

ScrollVerse can use the following first-party record structure: **narrow statement**, **source or record**, **date/context**, **evidence category**, **limitation**, **review need**, and **public/restricted state**. This structure helps a visitor understand the scope of a record without declaring it an official validation.

## Qualified-Review Boundary

Questions involving a regulated product, health, law, financial activity, professional licensing, rights, security, institutional authorization, or official records should be referred to the relevant qualified professional or institution. The Commons may teach the vocabulary and preserve the research trail; it does not issue the authorization.

## Route Verification

On August 14, 2026, the `/standards-evidence-commons` route rendered with all seven cross-domain cards, the source/context, readiness, and boundary labels, a visible local evidence planner, and correction/qualified-review handoffs. The rendered authority boundary states that ScrollVerse is not an accreditor, licensing body, court, government, medical regulator, legal authority, financial institution, or official validator absent independently verifiable authorization. The planner presents a copy-only browser-local note and does not upload or create an official record.
