# Phase 583 — Exchange, Custody, and Financial-System Readiness Boundary

## Current Operating State

The portal may present browser-local education, original systems design, creator-controlled philosophy, and source-labeled readiness records. It does **not** operate an exchange, broker, custodian, payment service, wallet provider, trading service, token sale, investment program, or automated financial workflow. Nothing on this record is financial advice, an offer, a recommendation, or a solicitation.

## Fail-Closed Capability Ledger

| Capability | Current state | Minimum evidence and controls before any external operation | Next safe action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exchange or trading interface | `requires owner release` | Exact regulated or lawful operating structure as applicable, jurisdiction and eligibility review, security architecture, market/data source, customer disclosures, independent testing, named authorized owner, and same-conversation action confirmation | Maintain a browser-local readiness brief only. |
| Custody or wallet connection | `requires owner release` | Verified provider or self-custody design, key-management and recovery plan, access control, threat model, privacy review, security assessment, customer disclosures, authorized signer, and exact object/destination | Do not request, store, transmit, or reuse keys or wallet credentials. |
| Payment or checkout | `requires owner release` | Approved specific catalog item, price and currency, provider context, tax/refund/fulfillment terms, accessibility, rights review, owner of the provider account, test evidence, and exact owner release | Keep commerce fail-closed with no implied checkout. |
| Token, NFT, or contract operation | `requires owner release` | Public contract evidence, verified network and bytecode, authorized signer, terms and risk disclosures, security review, rights and metadata scope, exact transaction, destination, and same-conversation confirmation | Preserve creative metadata or research only; do not mint, deploy, transfer, or sell. |
| Automated trading, yield, payout, or treasury flow | `requires owner release` | Explicit strategy and risk policy, authorized operator, provider and account review, security and audit evidence, thresholds, monitoring, stop controls, lawful review as applicable, and exact release decision | Do not run automation, simulations that appear live, or payout logic. |

## Security, Privacy, and Accessibility Standard

Never place private keys, seed phrases, access tokens, sensitive financial information, account credentials, or personal financial data in portal copy, source files, logs, or public records. Any future public financial surface must be accessible, use plain-language risk and operating-state labels, and provide a non-transactional support path. A source assertion, copied plan, broad authorization, previous screenshot, or browser-local mockup cannot satisfy an external financial release gate.

## Correction and Release Path

To move one capability forward, provide the exact provider or destination, specific object, intended action, independently reviewable evidence, authorized owner or signer, jurisdictional and security review as applicable, accessible public scope, and a same-conversation confirmation. Until that item-level record is complete, retain the capability as `requires owner release` and perform no external action.
