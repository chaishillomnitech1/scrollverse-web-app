# Phase 180 — ConsoleVerse Workflow Pattern Expansion

ConsoleVerse extends its first-party operating approach with reusable record fields, saved-view concepts, dependency lanes, and review gates. The enhancement uses public documentation as design research only; no third-party data model, account, API, automation, contact, or record is imported. [1] [2] [3] [4]

| Pattern | First-party adaptation | Operational boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Standard record fields | A reusable record template identifies record type, creator, artifact, owner, source, evidence state, dependency, next step, and last-confirmed date. | It is a local planning aid, not a CRM record, legal file, or provider submission. |
| Purpose-built views | Browser-local filters organize the template by relationship, readiness, dependency, or review lens. | A view changes only the presentation in the browser; it does not change external data or permissions. |
| Relationship map | The conceptual links between creator, artifact, source, collaborator, and provider-preparation route are made explicit. | A route link does not establish a commercial, rights, employment, or platform relationship. |
| Stage-gated review | A readable gate identifies what evidence is needed before a preparation record can move from intake to review or handoff. | A visible gate is not an approval, signature, legal review, provider acceptance, payout, or release action. |
| Exception register | A blocker or missing field is shown with an owner-controlled next step. | No automated escalation, notification, account action, or deadline commitment occurs. |

The local `/consoleverse` route rendered on August 13, 2026 with the six operation patterns, browser-local lens controls, relationship blueprint, four stage-gate template cards, visible exception patterns, and the stated external-action boundary. The controls organize only the current view and do not write, send, notify, assign, or alter records.
