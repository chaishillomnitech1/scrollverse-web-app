# Phase 451 — Art Learning & Practice Studio Standard

**Purpose:** Create a free, reader-directed ScrollVerse learning layer where visitors can explore art, philosophy, public collections, creative methods, research pathways, and preparation tools without needing to purchase, trade, join a token program, or surrender control of their work.

## Studio promise

The Art Learning & Practice Studio is a place to study, make, compare, and prepare. It gives each visitor a clear path from a public source to an original prompt, a visual practice, an archive question, or a browser-local project brief.

> **Learn freely. Make originally. Keep the source visible. Let the creator choose the next step.**

## Learning lanes

| Lane | Visitor activity | Source and release treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Art, philosophy, and visual literacy** | Compare ideas about art, imagination, ethics, beauty, craft, and public meaning | Link named public sources and distinguish a source from ScrollVerse interpretation |
| **Museum and archive discovery** | Follow objects, collections, artists, materials, periods, and conservation questions | Use custodial or public-collection records where available; do not imply a loan, affiliation, or image license |
| **Creative practice** | Use original prompts for drawing, photography, writing, collage, music, design, code, or visual world-building | The prompt is original; the visitor keeps their own work and chooses their own publishing path |
| **Research desk** | Move from a question to a source list, notes, comparison frame, and project concept | Browser-local preparation; no personal-data collection or automatic account action |
| **AI Access & Creation Station** | Reach public AI and digital-creation destinations, compare their stated access paths, and prepare prompts or project briefs | External destinations are source-labeled; provider account, plan, terms, output rights, and privacy choices remain the visitor’s decision |
| **Artist support and opportunity literacy** | Learn how to prepare a portfolio, source record, artist statement, grant-search question, or release packet | No grant, payment, reward, scholarship, mentorship, token, or opportunity is promised by the Studio |

## Youth-safe adult-supported pathway

Young artists and learners should be able to explore the free Studio with adult-supported participation guidance. The Studio does not require a young person to submit personal information, contact strangers, create an external account, purchase a tool, join an incentive program, trade a token, or upload a work publicly.

| Youth pathway | Available now | Requires separate program design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free art-history, source, and practice prompts | Yes | — |
| Browser-local creative brief or prompt preparation | Yes | — |
| Adult-supported exploration guide | Yes | — |
| Portfolio or opportunity preparation checklist | Yes | — |
| Public submissions, profiles, messaging, contests, payments, prizes, crypto rewards, wallets, or tokenized work | No | Named safeguarding, eligibility, parent/guardian, privacy, rights, and release controls |

## AI Access & Creation Station standard

The Station is a directory and preparation desk, not a claim that every AI is connected to ScrollVerse. Each tool card needs a public destination, a stated use category, an access label, and a note that the visitor should review the provider’s own account, cost, privacy, output, and rights terms.

| Access label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Available now** | A public destination is reachable for the visitor to inspect. |
| **Free exploration may be available** | The provider may offer a free path; visitor confirms the current provider terms. |
| **Provider account or plan may be required** | ScrollVerse does not create or manage the visitor’s external account. |
| **Research** | The destination is being indexed or requires a source update. |

The Station may offer original prompt templates and browser-local project notes. It does not claim a direct integration, provider endorsement, shared account, traffic payment, referral payment, analytics revenue, or access to a visitor’s work.

## Artist-support boundary

ScrollVerse can prepare an artist for opportunity through original tools: a work inventory, source trail, rights checklist, portfolio brief, artist statement, accessible description, project plan, and release packet. A later artist-support program may be designed with transparent eligibility, review, funding source, recipient verification, privacy, accessibility, and appeal/correction records.

Until those fields exist, **no crypto incentive, coin, NFT reward, grant, payment, commission, stipend, or compensation claim is active.** The Studio must not frame a young person’s participation, art, attention, data, or social reach as consideration for a digital asset or financial return.

## Source and correction standard

Every external art, philosophy, archive, AI, or tool destination will show: source organization, public URL, purpose, access label, date/context, scope note, and correction path. The Studio links and contextualizes sources; it does not copy restricted materials in full or claim affiliation with a listed organization.

## Traffic and sustainability boundary

The Studio may use the portal’s configured aggregate analytics to understand its own pages. It does not represent traffic as revenue, a paid referral, a payout, a guaranteed monetization method, or a reason to direct visitors to any external provider. Any future advertising, referral, sponsorship, traffic-revenue, creator payment, or partner agreement is a separate verified commercial record.
