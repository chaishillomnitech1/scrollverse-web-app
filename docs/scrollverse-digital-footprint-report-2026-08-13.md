# ScrollVerse Digital Footprint Research Report

**Prepared:** August 13, 2026  
**Scope:** Public ScrollVerse routes, owner-supplied creator channels and research links, public GitHub metadata, the designated ScrollScan explorer, versioned project history, and official external platform update records.

## Executive Summary

The public record shows a substantial, multi-surface ScrollVerse footprint: the live `scrollverse.co` portal; a public GitHub portfolio containing **50 distinct repository records** in the reviewed inventory; six owner-supplied creator-channel handoffs; a 28-link public research trail; a versioned portal delivery history; and official product update timelines for seven external AI and developer platforms. The result is a reviewable body of dated material—not a single undifferentiated claim. [1] [2] [3]

> **Method rule:** A public record can establish what the source visibly says or shows. Records occurring near each other establish temporal overlap. A claim that one event caused another requires a direct, independently reviewable causal link.

| Evidence class | Meaning in this report | What it does not establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| **Documented record** | A visible route, public repository field, explorer page, checkpoint, or official release note. | Private activity, account control, contract ownership, commercial outcome, or causation. |
| **Temporal overlap** | Two independently dated records appear in the same period. | That either record caused, influenced, or was coordinated with the other. |
| **Causal evidence** | A direct attribution, linked commit, signed statement, provider record, transaction, or other inspectable provenance. | It is not supplied by timing alone. |

## 7. Alignment Matrix: What the Timeline Can and Cannot Say

| Comparison | Directly documented | Temporal-overlap reading | Causal-evidence requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| GitHub commit vs. external release note | Each has a public timestamp. | The records may be ordered or contemporaneous. | A direct statement, linked ticket, shared commit, provider record, or other inspectable attribution. |
| ScrollVerse checkpoint vs. external release note | The checkpoint records the portal change and test state. | The portal and external release may appear in the same time window. | Direct acknowledgement by the external publisher or a verifiable integration record. |
| Creator channel post vs. platform update | The channel and update may each be public. | Visitors can compare dates and content. | A direct source proving contribution, influence, or a relationship. |
| Explorer activity vs. product release | Chain statistics and release records are independently public. | Both can be placed on a timeline. | Contract address, transaction, or provider record tying the activity to the claimed product event. |

The public record is appropriate for open research and independent assessment. The strongest statement supported today is that a visible portfolio, portal history, source trail, and external product timelines coexist in a dated record. It is not technically sound to turn that coexistence into a causal conclusion without the additional evidence named above.
