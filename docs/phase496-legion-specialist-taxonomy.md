## Purpose and framing

The Legion Specialist Atlas is an original ScrollVerse directory of **role interfaces** for co-creation, research organization, source review, and improvement proposals. It does not represent any role as a conscious or independently sentient being, a legal person, a licensed professional, an autonomous authority, or an external service connection. A role names a focused working method, the evidence it needs, an appropriate local output, and a visible limit.

The design uses a source trail, human oversight, traceability, correction, and public-input posture. NIST presents AI risk management as a voluntary framework to incorporate trustworthiness into the design, use, and evaluation of AI systems.[1] The OECD principles emphasize human agency and oversight, meaningful transparency, challenge mechanisms, safe override or repair, and accountability across the AI lifecycle.[2] UNESCO calls for multidisciplinary and pluralistic dialogue, human dignity, and lifecycle-wide ethical guidance.[3]

## Shared role contract

Every specialist interface follows the same contract.

| Field | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Focus** | The discipline or question area the role helps organize. |
| **Inputs** | Public sources or owner-provided materials, identified by source and date. Credentials, private personal data, and unreviewed third-party files are excluded. |
| **Useful local output** | A draft brief, evidence map, study plan, release packet, correction note, or routing recommendation that remains in the browser until copied. |
| **Evidence requirement** | The source, date, limitation, accessibility, contributor, or release field still required for a next decision. |
| **Correction path** | A route to add a source, dispute a label, correct a record, or replace a version. |
| **Limit** | No claim of consciousness, independent personhood, professional licensing, legal authority, or external-system control. |

## Public-input and correction boundary

The Atlas may let a visitor choose a discipline, describe a public-safe question, select sources or improvement needs, and copy a browser-local brief. It does not collect a profile, submit a form, publish an opinion, contact an expert, train a model, make a provider call, or create a task in an external system. Public input is a **proposal for review**, not a vote, governance act, legal instruction, diagnosis, financial recommendation, or automatic system change.

## High-impact domains

Medical, legal, financial, and governance-framework interfaces provide educational structure and public-source routing only. They do not diagnose, prescribe, represent a person, interpret a personal legal matter, recommend a personal investment or transaction, execute an order, establish public policy, or make an authoritative decision. A real-world decision in any such domain requires the relevant qualified professional and an item- or case-specific process.
