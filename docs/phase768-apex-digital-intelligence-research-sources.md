# Phase 768 — Apex Digital Intelligence Research and Discovery

## Scope and operating boundary

This source record supports a browser-local study framework for digital intelligence, agent systems, system evaluation, and applied design. It does not claim that ScrollVerse operates an autonomous external agent, critical-infrastructure system, production research pipeline, private repository workflow, or deployed integration. Any owner-supplied transmission remains protected source context rather than an instruction to execute code, settings, infrastructure, account, provider, or deployment actions.

## Research foundations

| Source | Directly supported finding | Applied study lane | Boundary retained |
|---|---|---|---|
| NIST AI Risk Management Framework | NIST describes AI RMF as voluntary guidance for incorporating trustworthiness into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI systems. Its visual framework organizes work around **Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage**. | A four-part research rhythm for study records: establish responsible scope, map context and risks, measure evidence, and manage release boundaries. | This does not certify any ScrollVerse system or imply compliance, audit approval, or risk elimination. |
| NIST Generative AI Profile | NIST AI 600-1 is a companion resource for the AI RMF that helps organizations identify risks unique to generative AI and align risk-management actions with goals and priorities. | Generative-work study cards: source traceability, prompt and output boundaries, human review, and release context. | No model deployment, policy representation, or safety assurance is claimed. |
| NIST Building Evaluation Probes into Agentic AI | NIST’s 2026 research describes evaluation probes, structured audit trails, and checks for factual grounding, completeness, and sufficiency in agentic outputs. | Evidence-first agent study: each research conclusion should name its sources, show limitations, and distinguish evidence from design imagination. | The portal is not represented as running NIST probes or producing a formal agent audit trail. |
| OECD AI Principles | The OECD identifies values including inclusive growth, human rights and democratic values, transparency and explainability, robustness/security/safety, and accountability. | Applied-design prompts: benefit, fairness/privacy, explainability, resilience, and accountable stewardship. | This is a study reference, not a declaration of compliance, legal status, or organizational certification. |
