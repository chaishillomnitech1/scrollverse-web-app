# Phase 763 — ScrollVerse × XLVIIIblocks Local Proposal Canvas

## Local-only purpose

The proposal canvas is a browser-local preparation worksheet for organizing a potential ScrollVerse × XLVIIIblocks financing and white-label partnership packet. It does not save records to a server, send an email, submit an application, contact a counterparty, or transmit user-entered data to an external service.

## Fields

| Proposal area | Local field set |
|---|---|
| Relationship | Counterparty or operating name, representative, proposed relationship, project title |
| Scope | Project scope, delivery milestones, responsibilities, intended public or customer surfaces |
| Capital | Proposed amount, currency, intended use of funds, financing form, payment or revenue-share terms |
| Governance | Decision rights, reporting cadence, escalation approach, record-retention approach |
| White-label | Brand-use scope, operating roles, reserved rights, customer-support responsibility |
| Destination | Intended proposal destination, evidence references, internal next action |

## Local actions

The canvas may provide a **copy preparation summary** and a browser-generated **JSON download**. These actions operate in the visitor’s browser. The canvas must not collect credentials, passwords, account numbers, private keys, seed phrases, identity documents, or bank-routing information.

## Boundary

> Completing a local proposal canvas does not create an agreement, request or receive funding, establish credit, bind a party, authorize a transaction, satisfy a disclosure obligation, or transmit information to a lender, counterparty, marketplace, bank, provider, or government body.
