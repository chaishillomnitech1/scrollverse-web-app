# Phase 117 — ScrollVerse Evidence & Standards

## Purpose

The ScrollVerse Evidence & Standards layer is a **first-party transparency model**. It helps visitors distinguish implemented features, active development, optional external paths, and conceptual material. It is not a legal, regulatory, medical, financial, scientific, or certification authority.

| Status | Meaning | Required public behavior |
|---|---|---|
| First-party implemented | A controlled route or workflow exists in the ScrollVerse project. | Link to the working route and state the implementation boundary. |
| In progress | A scoped project phase is defined but remains under active development. | State what has not yet been activated. |
| Optional external pathway | A provider-controlled route may be useful. | Link to an official route where available and disclose that external policy and review apply. |
| Conceptual | A creative, narrative, research, or future-design direction. | Avoid presenting it as deployed technology, proof, a credential, or real-world authority. |

## Operating Standard

Every public ScrollVerse system should identify its owner or steward, its implementation state, source basis, privacy or consent posture, and any external dependency. This improves clarity for creators, contributors, and prospective partners without treating internal records as external certification.

The model is informed by the project’s existing source-first creator provenance, provider-boundary documentation, and IP-readiness controls. It applies a plain-language version of NIST AI RMF principles around accountability, transparency, and documented risk management. [1]
