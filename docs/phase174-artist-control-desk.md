# Phase 174 — Artist Control and Specialized Assistance

## First-party artist-control inventory

| Creator need | First-party route | Available now | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private source record | `/creator-workspace` | Consent-based profile, collaboration requests, and protected provenance records. | Not a legal rights registry, payment channel, marketplace, or title determination. |
| Media facts and package preparation | `/media-readiness-lab` | Browser-local inspection, SHA-256 integrity record, preview, review state, and JSON package export. | Not a mastering tool, distribution upload, delivery service, rights certificate, or provider approval. |
| Public provider comparison | `/distribution-highways` | Source-labeled map of representative public music, video, streaming, and creator routes. | Not a provider account, partnership, onboarding authorization, placement, or payout. |
| Revenue-model learning | `/creator-revenue-learning` | Source-labeled education about platform mechanics and first-party preparation. | No income forecast, account activation, or guaranteed result. |
| Review and operations | `/revenue-ops` and `/creator-operations` | Readiness records and owner-controlled release workflow. | No agreement signing, financial execution, release, or provider action without evidence and authorization. |

## Incentive boundary

The Artist Control Desk states only one active incentive: free access to first-party learning and preparation routes. Any tokenized service, creator reward, kickback, referral, revenue share, or payout remains inactive until the specific contract, agreement, rules, eligibility, compliance review, and provider or on-chain evidence are documented.

## Guidance boundary

Specialist routes are navigation and preparation tools. They do not replace a creator’s judgment or act as a label, lawyer, accountant, distributor, financial advisor, or external-platform representative.
