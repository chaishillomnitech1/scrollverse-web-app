## Retained style qualities

The source was reviewed only for broad narration attributes, not for identity, biometric voice features, or imitation. The recorded characteristics are deliberate and measured pacing; precise, distinct articulation; a formal and declarative register; controlled, stable energy; strategic pauses; moderately dense elevated vocabulary; and a clear, resonant foreground vocal mix with restrained spatial depth against atmospheric music.

These are direction-level production qualities, not a voice identity. They can inform an original ScrollVerse narrator profile while preserving a distinct performer, wording, timing, and voice character.

## Boundary

No attempt was made to identify the speaker, extract a voiceprint, train a clone, reproduce the narrator, transcribe the full video, download or republish its audio, or infer personal traits. Any future ScrollVerse narration must use original approved script, a distinct voice direction, documented performer or synthesis rights, accessibility assets, a delivery destination, and an item-specific release record.
