## Public Research Findings Applied to the Design

FIDE describes safeguarding as an organisational responsibility that includes children and other persons at risk, with attention to safe and inclusive environments.[1] The US Chess Safe Play Hub links safe-play policy, reporting information, resources, and training for tournament directors, indicating that youth participation requires more than game mechanics alone.[2] FIDE's *Chess for Protection* programme describes level-appropriate workshops, trained facilitators, educational materials, community context, gender inclusion, and an emphasis on psychosocial well-being and social connection.[3]

The Phase 584 implementation therefore applies four practical controls. First, it makes adult-supported participation visible for any youth-facing use. Second, it avoids public chat, profiles, matchmaking, leaderboards, payment mechanics, and personal-data capture. Third, it provides learning pathways by level rather than a universal skill claim. Fourth, it distinguishes an accessible local practice guide from a professional coach, rated engine, external service, or credential pathway.

## Enhancement Brief

The Chess Arena will become the **ScrollVerse Chess Initiative**: a playable local board paired with a creator-directed learning curriculum. The curriculum will include a first-move foundation, tactical pattern practice, deep-strategy reflection, a suggested AI-practice prompt format that visitors may take to a compatible public AI of their own choice, a youth and facilitator guide, and a partnership-ready evidence checklist. The page will show direct operating labels: **available now**, **research**, and **requires owner release**.
