# Phase 261 — Quantum & Frequency Research Lab: Source Trail

## Scope

This source trail supports a free ScrollVerse research lab. It separates **documented quantum engineering and cryptography**, **reproducible signal-and-frequency study**, and **historical or cultural geography interpretation**. It does not represent a physical quantum computer, biometric system, quantum-safe product, energy system, or geographic-energy connection as active on ScrollVerse.

| Research lane | Authoritative finding | ScrollVerse study use | Operating state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quantum information science | The National Academies describes quantum information science as a field investigating how quantum behavior may be used to encode, sense, process, and transmit information. | Establish basic concepts, terminology, and open research questions. | Public learning and source study. |
| Quantum education and simulation | IBM Quantum Learning provides instructional modules on quantum computing and related learning pathways. | Link visitors to practical learning and simulation resources rather than claiming a local physical quantum machine. | External learning handoff; no account or provider connection is configured by this portal. |
| Post-quantum cryptography | NIST has released finalized post-quantum cryptography standards and frames PQC as a defense against future quantum-computing attacks on electronic information. | Study cryptographic inventory, migration planning, and evidence-based security transition questions. | Research and planning; no cryptographic product or security assurance is issued. |
| Frequency and signals | NIST’s Time and Frequency Division maintains U.S. standards for frequency and time interval, while its metrology work describes high-precision measurement of electromagnetic signals. | Support repeatable notes about signal settings, source, method, units, and observed output. | Browser-local study planning only. |
| Ley-line traditions | Historical accounts trace ley-line theory to Alfred Watkins in the 1920s; scholarly work treats it as a cultural, landscape, and folk-geography topic. | Present an interpretive study lane with source/date/context fields and visitor reflection prompts. | Cultural-geography research; no verified geographic-energy linkage is represented. |
