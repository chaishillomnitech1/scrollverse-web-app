## Batch Handling Rule

No file in this batch is copied into a public route, uploaded to storage, shared with a provider, used in a campaign, associated with a product, attached to a music release, tokenized, minted, licensed, or distributed from this intake. The batch remains local protected context until a specific asset receives its own evidence packet and owner release decision.

The newest intake includes multiple `gemini_generated_video_*.mp4` files and owner-supplied image files in JPG, WEBP, and PNG formats. They are held as protected first-party Media World source material. A file name or upload origin alone is not treated as a public-release authorization, proof of rights for every visible subject or brand, a music synchronization clearance, or a distribution instruction.

## Catalog Evidence Packet

Each potential music or media release should have a separate record with the following fields:

| Packet area | Questions to resolve | Current default |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | What is the final title, version, creator credit, and source/master reference? | Owner-supplied context only |
| Rights | Who controls the composition, recording, samples, likenesses, artwork, and contributor permissions? | Needs review |
| Master readiness | Is there an approved audio/visual master, cover/art treatment, caption/transcript plan, and technical delivery package? | Needs review |
| Provider and metadata | What provider record, identifiers, territories, dates, and terms apply? | No provider account access or claim |
| Audience and accessibility | Is there an accurate description, caption/transcript plan, age-appropriate presentation, and correction contact? | Planning only |
| Release decision | Is the destination, pricing or free status, announcement, and owner approval documented? | Fail-closed |
