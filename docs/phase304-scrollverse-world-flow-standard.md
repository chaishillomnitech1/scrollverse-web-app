## Creator-Directed World Flow

Apply the same presentation standard throughout ScrollVerse: merch and fashion, books and free knowledge, music and ScrollStorm, films and story worlds, artifacts, creator tools, community pathways, and crypto-related discovery. Present the creator’s own works, stated intent, and original world directly in first-party language. Visitor controls are optional ways to explore, organize, save, share, comment, or follow a verified destination; they do not ask visitors to determine the creator’s intent or replace creator direction.

## Factual Operating Record

Direct presentation is paired with visible facts. Each relevant item should carry its applicable source/version, rights and contributor record, accessibility material, operating state, intended destination, correction path, and owner-release decision. The approved operating states remain **available now**, **research**, and **requires owner release**.
