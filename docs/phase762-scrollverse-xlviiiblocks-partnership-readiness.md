# Phase 762 — ScrollVerse × XLVIIIblocks Financing and White-Label Partnership Readiness

## Purpose

This browser-local readiness record organizes a potential first financing and white-label partnership pathway between **ScrollVerse.co** and **XLVIIIblocks**. It is a preparation framework for a future proposal and governance record. It does not establish a lender relationship, loan, credit facility, partnership agreement, funding event, security interest, contract, white-label license, market transaction, or commitment by either party.

## Proposed readiness lanes

| Lane | Browser-local preparation | Evidence needed before external activation |
|---|---|---|
| Partnership identity | Identify the proposed relationship, owners or authorized representatives, roles, scope, and intended public names. | Verified organization and signatory records; counterparty acceptance; final agreement path. |
| Use-of-funds narrative | Describe the intended project scope, milestones, budget categories, and reporting approach in plain business language. | Actual financing request, source of funds, terms, budget support, and counterparty or lender review. |
| Governance design | Define decision rights, approvals, reporting cadence, escalation process, record retention, and brand-use review. | Agreed governance terms and executed authorization by the relevant parties. |
| White-label architecture | Identify what could be branded, which customer surfaces are involved, who operates each function, and what remains non-exclusive or reserved. | Approved brand permissions, product/service definition, technical feasibility, provider terms, and written agreement. |
| Capital and revenue readiness | Connect the proposal to the capital, bank, market, revenue, commerce, and financial readiness records already in the portal. | Named institution, terms, disclosures, provider setup, and exact owner-controlled transaction or agreement record. |

## Proposal evidence checklist

The readiness record can accept the following non-secret inputs when they become available: a counterparty legal or operating name; authorized representative; project scope; requested or proposed amount and currency; intended use of funds; proposed payment schedule; collateral or security terms if any; term sheet or agreement version; governing entity and jurisdiction; brand-use scope; operational responsibilities; delivery milestones; reporting cadence; and a specific external destination for the proposal.

> No estimate, term, credit statement, revenue outcome, financial return, approval probability, or legal arrangement is inferred by this browser-local framework.

## Activation boundary

Before any lending, financing, contract, white-label launch, account connection, provider action, payment, transfer, market action, public announcement, or customer-facing offering, the relevant lane needs the appropriate verified evidence, named institution or counterparty where applicable, agreed terms, required disclosures, and an owner-controlled release record.
