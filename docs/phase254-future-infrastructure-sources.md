# Phase 254 — Future Infrastructure Blueprint: Source Trail

## Purpose

This record supports a **free, source-labeled research blueprint** for future-facing infrastructure. It separates public research, first-party design questions, regulated operating categories, and owner-controlled activation gates. It does not represent ScrollVerse as a bank, money services business, lender, exchange, custodian, registrar, patent office, trademark office, accredited security provider, or on-chain operator.

| Research lane | Public source finding | ScrollVerse-compatible research use | Current boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Post-quantum cryptography | NIST has finalized post-quantum standards and recommends organizations identify vulnerable algorithms and plan migration. FIPS 203 defines ML-KEM and describes its security relationship to the Module Learning with Errors problem. | Study cryptographic inventory, migration planning, interoperability, and evidence records. | No cryptographic product, biometric system, zero-knowledge circuit, security claim, key management service, or production implementation is activated. |
| Money-service categories | FinCEN describes money transmission, currency exchange, check cashing, and certain stored-value activity within its MSB framework; registration and other obligations may apply depending on facts and jurisdiction. | Make regulated-category research and owner/provider activation gates visible. | No registration, money transmission, custody, exchange, card issuance, lending, payment processing, payout, or fund movement is available. |
| Consumer-finance rules | The CFPB publishes federal consumer-finance rules, policy actions, and compliance resources. | Treat consumer transparency, product characterization, privacy, dispute, and support planning as research and review lanes. | No consumer financial product is offered or approved through this portal. |
| Patents and trademarks | USPTO provides public patent and trademark search tools and explains distinctions among patent, trademark, and copyright protection. | Help creators identify which type of intellectual-property question they need to research before seeking qualified review. | No clearance opinion, application, filing, registration, ownership claim, or legal conclusion is created. |
| Domain registration | ICANN explains that registrants contract with registrars and have rights and responsibilities for management, transfer, renewal, and restoration. | Support a source-led domain research and owner-controlled management checklist. | No domain availability claim, registrar login, purchase, transfer, DNS change, or ownership claim is made. |
