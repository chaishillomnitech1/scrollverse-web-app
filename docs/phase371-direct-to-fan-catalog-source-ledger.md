The source review identifies paths that can support a future direct-to-fan catalog. It does not establish that a particular song, book, master, look, publishing right, royalty split, contract, marketplace listing, payout, or distribution account is ready for commercial activation.

## Recommended release-ledger workflow

| Sequence | Owner-controlled decision | Direct-to-fan result |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Select one item | Exact music master, book edition, visual, look, or bundle | One unambiguous catalog record |
| 2. Attach source context | Source file, owner release, master/publishing/contributor context, distribution terms, artwork, and accessibility assets | A source-labeled release packet |
| 3. Decide the offer | Free, paid download, physical good, bundle, listening handoff, or future collectible; price and fulfillment chosen by the owner | A clear visitor offer without false availability |
| 4. Test delivery | Test-mode checkout and delivery path using the real approved asset and customer-facing description | A reviewed purchase/fulfillment experience |
| 5. Activate one item | Explicit owner confirmation of the exact item, price, destination, and consequence | A production-ready direct-to-fan listing |

## Vydia reference boundary

Vydia’s public distribution page describes a platform that offers distribution to more than 200 audio and video destinations, content and metadata supply-chain services, automated royalty accounting, and analytics. [1] That general information can inform the catalog architecture. It does not replace the owner’s individual distribution agreement or establish the terms he stated; those remain first-party context pending an owner-approved source record.

## Lookbook source boundary

The master shoppable lookbook is modeled as a visual discovery and release-ledger route. It will identify each future look as a concept, approved artifact, or item-level release record. It will not display an active price, inventory count, checkout, shipping promise, third-party likeness, or sale state until an item-specific image/rights, fulfillment, and owner-release packet exists.
