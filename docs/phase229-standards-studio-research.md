## Original ScrollVerse Methodology: Proposed Minimum Record

Every voluntary Standards Studio review should begin with a **narrow question**, the **review scope**, **evidence supplied or linked**, **source quality/context**, **known limitations**, **conflict disclosure**, **reviewer role**, **public/restricted status**, **decision state**, **correction path**, and **version/date**. A decision state may be “context recorded,” “source gap,” “first-party method check,” “awaiting qualified review,” “corrected,” “superseded,” or “withdrawn.” It must never be styled as an external accreditation, license, regulatory approval, court ruling, medical determination, legal opinion, financial endorsement, or government record.

## Public Participation Boundary

Visitors may submit corrections, sources, or original contributions through the existing co-creation path. Submission creates neither a right to publication nor a professional reviewer relationship. Content that includes private information, regulated advice, sensitive claims, copyrighted material without permission, or evidence requiring a qualified reviewer remains outside the public Studio record until appropriate review and authorization occur.
