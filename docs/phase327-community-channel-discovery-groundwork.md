# Phase 327 — Community & Public-Channel Discovery Groundwork

**Operating state:** community exchange available through authenticated participant controls; public-channel discovery remains source-labeled only.

| Area | Current local control |
|---|---|
| Participant identity | Author-selected display name or pseudonym, profile-visibility selection, and author-controlled reply choice |
| Contribution | Authenticated first-person entry and reply flow; no anonymous provider access or external posting |
| Community care | Respectful-reply framing, correction path, and participant-controlled public visibility |
| Public channels | Categorize the supplied public source by topic, source URL, observed date, and context; link outward without copying videos, posts, audio, or claims into the portal |
| External boundary | No subscription, message, contact import, social post, schedule, provider connection, wallet, payment, token, trade, or transfer action |

The participant exchange preserves author ownership of a contribution, but a community feature does not establish factual truth, endorsement, professional advice, or a required conclusion. Each public-channel record remains a distinct attributed learning path with a correction route.
