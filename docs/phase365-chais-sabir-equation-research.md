# Phase 365 — Chais Sabir Equation and Pattern Study Research Record

## Research orientation

The planned public study separates three layers: **first-party record** for the owner-supplied name, birth context, family chronology, seal, and frequency language; **reproducible methods** for letter values, date arithmetic, time conversion, and chart inputs; and **free learning references** for readers who want to examine sources directly. The study does not convert a calculation into a claim about a person or the future.

## Validated public learning references

| Field | Verified source and usable lesson | Public-page use |
|---|---|---|
| Measurement and frequency | NIST’s uncertainty materials distinguish basic definitions, evaluation of components, combination of components, expanded uncertainty, and coverage factors. [1] | A frequency panel will distinguish a creative frequency label from a measured quantity that needs units, a method, an instrument, conditions, and uncertainty. |
| Interpretation | The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy defines hermeneutics as the study of interpretation and describes its role in fields that interpret human intentions, testimony, texts, and artifacts. [2] | A method panel will invite readers to record the source, calculation convention, alternative reading, and limits of an interpretation. |
| Ephemeris calculations | Swiss Ephemeris describes a high-precision ephemeris with a general manual and programmer documentation, and identifies its JPL ephemeris basis. [3] | The chart-input panel will identify the necessary reproducibility fields: calendar date, local civil time, time zone and historical daylight rules, latitude, longitude, ephemeris source, and calculation convention. |
| Historical gematria | The Jewish Encyclopedia describes gemaṭria as a cryptograph based on numerical values of words and identifies numerical and permutative variants in traditional literature. [4] | A gematria panel will require the declared transliteration or Hebrew spelling, value convention, itemized addition, and the complete arithmetic result. |
| Decimal digital roots | Cambridge’s NRICH explains that a digital root is obtained by repeatedly adding decimal digits until one digit remains and relates the method to modulo-nine arithmetic. [6] | A date and name-number panel will show each intermediate sum, so a reader can reproduce or challenge the arithmetic. |

## Candidate historical and mathematical learning paths

The following sources are queued for the public bibliography and will be presented with their historical or mathematical scope rather than personal conclusions.

| Topic | Method to make reproducible | Candidate public source |
|---|---|---|
| Hebrew gematria | Transcribe into a declared Hebrew spelling; choose and display a stated value table; sum letters; record any final-letter convention. | [Jewish Encyclopedia: Gemaṭria][4] |
| Greek isopsephy | Transcribe into a declared Greek spelling; state the Milesian numeral table; sum letters; record archaic-letter conventions if used. | [Overview of isopsephy][5] |
| English ordinal and reduction | Map A–Z to 1–26; sum values; separately show an iterative decimal digital root; retain the complete work. | [NRICH: Digital Roots][6] |
| Number theory | Use modular arithmetic to verify a digital-root result; distinguish a base-10 arithmetic result from any interpretation. | [MIT OpenCourseWare: Mathematics for Computer Science][7] |

## Source and method boundary

The first-party context for the page is: **Chais “Sabir Kajir” Hill the Great**, born **August 28, 1978 at 7:00 PM**, with **Harlem, New York** specified by the owner as the birth location. The family chronology will retain the owner-supplied records for Marie Angela Hill and the owner’s brother as remembrance material. A chart is reproducible only when the location coordinates, time-zone history, ephemeris data set, zodiac convention, and house convention are explicitly displayed. A gematria, isopsephy, ordinal, or reduction result is reproducible only when its input spelling and value table are displayed.
