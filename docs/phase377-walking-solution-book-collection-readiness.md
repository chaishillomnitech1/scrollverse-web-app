# Phase 377 — Walking Solution Book Collection Readiness Record

## Collection concept

**Working collection name:** *The Book of the Journey — Walking Solution Edition.*

The collection concept connects the existing free Book of the Journey reader, its original ScrollVerse cover art, the owner-authored identity statement **“I’m a Walking Solution,”** and a future owner-approved edition record. The free reader remains available independently of any future collection activity.

## Failure-closed activation rule

Nothing in this readiness record mints, lists, prices, sends, transfers, connects a wallet, requests a payment, creates a checkout, or claims a collection exists on a chain. A proposed edition can advance only when its source asset, rights context, metadata, approved technical destination, owner instruction, and visitor delivery/support path are complete together.

## Free-reader commitment

The Walking Solution identity layer and the Book of the Journey reader are free learning and creative record pathways. A future collection edition does not remove, gate, or retroactively restrict the free reading path. The collection is a separate owner-controlled edition concept, not a substitute for access to the underlying public learning experience.

## Correction path

The owner may add a collection-ready record one exact edition at a time. If a title, asset, contributor, source, or release instruction changes, the collection ledger will record the revised source and operating state rather than infer that an older preparation record remains active.
