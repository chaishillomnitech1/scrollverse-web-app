# ScrollVerse Creator Exchange and Bundle Readiness

> **Scope.** The Creator Exchange Navigator is a source-led planning surface for original music, media, books, art, comics, merchandise, downloads, and collectible readiness. It organizes a creator’s bundle concept before a release. It does **not** publish, price, sell, trade, fund, custody, deliver, manufacture, mint, list, or distribute anything.

| Release lane | Required preparation | Explicit activation boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct-to-fan release | Rights-cleared assets, metadata, release context, product/delivery record, approved price and terms, payment configuration. [1] [4] | A live checkout requires owner approval, provider configuration, an approved SKU, secure webhooks, and delivery controls. |
| DSP/platform delivery | Final audio, cover, metadata, copyright clearances, distributor/provider account, platform ingestion. [2] | A provider reference is not evidence of availability, profile claim, playlist consideration, payout, or royalties. |
| Music rights administration | Accurate track/release metadata, rights-holder/artist identification, registration and payout records where appropriate. [3] | Rights collection is never inferred from a title, page, or bundle draft. |
| Digital bundles | Cleared files, content/accessibility package, secure delivery rules, rights record, product and consumer terms. [4] [6] | No download entitlement or customer delivery exists until the delivery system is activated and verified. |
| Merchandise | Cleared artwork, product specs, fulfillment decision, consumer/shipping/return terms, provider account and integration. [5] | No manufacturing, stock, shipping, or order routing is represented as active without owner-authorized provider configuration. |
| Collectible/currency learning | Asset integrity, rights record, explicit purpose, contract/provider/wallet path, activation authorization. | A concept, learning route, or readiness record is not a mint, listing, wallet connection, offer, custody, trade, or currency transaction. |

## Bundle Rule

A release can connect music, a story, comics, artwork, books, downloads, and merchandise when each component has its **own** source, contributor/rights, accessibility, release, and delivery record. A bundle does not cause rights to travel automatically between components, and a creative concept does not authorize a price, payment, provider upload, collectible action, or marketplace listing.
