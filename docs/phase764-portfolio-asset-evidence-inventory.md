# Phase 764 — Portfolio Asset Evidence Inventory

## Purpose

The Portfolio Asset Evidence Inventory is a browser-local worksheet for organizing physical, material, digital, and business assets relevant to ScrollVerse and XLVIIIblocks partnership readiness. It does not verify ownership, appraise an item, determine market value, establish collateral eligibility, create a security interest, prove title, or support a credit decision.

## Inventory fields

| Field | Preparation purpose | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Asset category | Physical, material, digital, or business asset classification. | Category is owner-entered context, not a valuation conclusion. |
| Asset title and description | Plain-language identification and relevant condition or capability notes. | Description is not an ownership or quality verification. |
| Portfolio relevance | Relationship to a product, creative work, infrastructure, revenue model, or partnership scope. | Relevance is a planning statement, not a performance claim. |
| Evidence reference | Public reference, internal file title, invoice reference, serial record, provenance note, or photo reference. | The worksheet does not inspect, validate, upload, or retain the evidence file. |
| Owner-entered status | Supplied, needs review, or ready for proposal-review. | Status is a local workflow label, not a legal or financial determination. |
| Notes | Context, restrictions, condition, or next action. | Do not enter credentials, account numbers, private keys, seed phrases, identity documents, or sensitive financial details. |

## Local actions

The interface may maintain entries in the current browser session and allow a visitor to copy a preparation summary or download a JSON file. It does not save to a server or send asset information to a lender, partner, marketplace, insurer, appraiser, bank, provider, government body, or external service.

> An inventory supports a conversation; it is not an appraisal, title report, ownership verification, collateral schedule, loan package, loan approval, or financial advice.
