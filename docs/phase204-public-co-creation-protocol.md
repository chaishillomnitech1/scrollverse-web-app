# ScrollVerse Public Co-Creation Protocol

> **Purpose.** ScrollVerse invites visitors to help improve the public knowledge space through original work, public sources, corrections, accessibility improvements, translation proposals, and route suggestions. The protocol makes the review state explicit so a contribution is never misrepresented as an immediate public fact, commercial agreement, or release.

| Stage | Visitor action | Public meaning | Review boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | A visitor prepares a proposal in the browser. | The material remains a local proposal. | No record, account, publication, or ownership transfer is created. |
| Protected handoff | A contributor elects to continue through the separate protected workflow. | The contributor has requested review. | Authentication, data handling, and owner review remain separate. |
| Review | Source, rights, privacy, safety, accessibility, and relevance are assessed. | The item is under review, not accepted as fact. | Missing evidence is documented; no public release is implied. |
| Source-labeled update | A cleared item can be presented with its source, date, limitation, and correction path. | The item is a bounded public record. | It is not legal, financial, medical, security, or commercial advice or authority. |

## Participation Scope

The public navigator accepts four kinds of proposals: source or correction, original creative work, accessibility or translation, and route improvement. A proposal should state its topic or route, provide relevant context, identify any rights or usage limits, and avoid claiming that a result has been deployed, published, approved, or externally verified unless linked evidence supports that claim.

## Material That Must Not Be Submitted

Private keys, wallet recovery phrases, passwords, confidential records, personal financial information, doxxing material, security-sensitive operational directions, and third-party work without permission are outside the public co-creation lane. Young creators should participate with a parent, guardian, educator, or other trusted adult. This route does not provide a transaction, employment, investment, legal, medical, or financial service.

## Existing Public Gaps

Recovery Materials, Books and Media Readiness, and Evidence Commons are linked from the Co-Creation Navigator as public, source-labeled invitations. They invite context and corrections without exposing raw recovery exports, private files, or unreviewed assets.
