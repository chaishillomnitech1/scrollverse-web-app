# Phase 525 — Privacy-Aware Analytics & Visitor Experience Readiness

**Evidence basis:** owner-shared 24-hour dashboard observations recorded in Phase 524.  
**Operating state:** aggregate engagement interpretation and browser-local route improvement planning; no personal visitor tracking, conversion claim, revenue claim, or provider action.

The observed dashboard values show repeatable aggregate engagement signals: approximately 99–100 visits from 61 displayed visitors, 353–356 page views, and displayed average duration around eight minutes. The dashboard’s period-over-period indicators are positive for page views, visits, visitors, and duration, while bounce rate is shown lower than its comparison value. This supports prioritizing clarity and continuity in the portal experience, not identifying people or inferring commercial outcomes.

| Aggregate observation | Privacy-aware interpretation | Browser-local priority |
|---|---|---|
| Page views exceed displayed visits | Some visitors explore more than one route | Keep cross-route discovery and clear return paths visible |
| Displayed duration is about eight minutes | Visitors may spend time reading or exploring | Preserve readable hierarchy, source trails, accessibility, and optional deeper paths |
| Bounce rate remains visible | A route-quality signal, not a statement about an individual visitor | Improve first-screen context, direct operating state, and destination clarity |
| Dashboard presents only aggregates | No person, device, identity, or private behavior should be exposed | Use aggregate route-quality review; do not add personal tracking or public visitor profiles |

The current improvement priorities are: concise first-screen purpose statements; source and operating-state labels; accessible route controls; linked continuation paths; and correction routes. Analytics observations remain evidence for portal experience review only. They do not create a financial claim, influence valuation, sale, product, payment, provider access, profile, score, or external result.
