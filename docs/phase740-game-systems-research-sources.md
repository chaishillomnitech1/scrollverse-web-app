# Phase 740 — Game Systems & Research Blueprint Sources

## Source-labeled study lanes

| Lane | Direct source finding | Blueprint use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dead Sea Scrolls | The Israel Antiquities Authority’s digital library classifies the corpus into biblical, non-biblical, documents, letters, scribal exercises, unidentified texts, and multiple compositions. It notes that scholarly categories can be debated and are navigation aids. | A **manuscript interpretation game loop**: compare source category, fragment condition, competing classification, and confidence before unlocking an interpretation card. | This is a study path, not a religious authority, historical reconstruction engine, or claim about any individual belief. |
| Solmization and Solfeggio claims | The requested modern 396–852 Hz association set is treated in the blueprint as a **modern claim set requiring source labels**, distinct from historical solmization. | A **sound-design sandbox** may let visitors compare rhythm, pitch, spectral visualization, and self-authored mood notes without health promises. | No medical, therapeutic, DNA, fear-release, intuition, or spiritual-outcome claim is made. |
| Emerald Tablet / Thoth materials | The blueprint treats “Thoth Emerald Tablets” language as a comparative-text and provenance study lane; modern attribution claims are not treated as verified ancient Egyptian provenance. | A **text transmission puzzle** may separate source date, attribution tradition, translation, commentary, and original fiction. | No assertion of ancient origin, supernatural effect, or historical certainty is made without a cited source. |

## References

[1] [Israel Antiquities Authority, *The Dead Sea Scrolls: Scrolls Content*](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/learn-about-the-scrolls/scrolls-content?locale=en_US)

[2] [Encyclopaedia Britannica, *Guido d’Arezzo*](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Guido-dArezzo-Italian-musician) describes Guido’s 11th-century staff-notation and solmization work, including the `ut re mi fa sol la` syllables tied to a hexachord. The blueprint therefore treats fixed-Hertz Solfeggio lists as a separate modern-claim lane rather than as a direct continuation of Guido’s documented teaching method.

[3] [Encyclopaedia Britannica, *Emerald Tablet*](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Emerald-Tablet) describes the earliest known version as appearing in the Arabic *Book of the Secret of Creation*, thought by some to date to the 9th century CE, and notes that reliable evidence does not support an origin earlier than the medieval Arabic period. The blueprint uses this as a provenance-and-transmission study lane, while treating later Thoth-associated traditions as attribution context rather than verified ancient Egyptian authorship.
