# Phase 339 — Primary-Source IP Research Notes

## Official source findings captured 2026-08-19 EDT

| Source | Finding retained for the readiness framework |
|---|---|
| USPTO, *Trademark, patent, or copyright* | The USPTO distinguishes trademarks, patents, and copyrights: trademarks identify the source of goods/services; patents concern technical inventions; copyright covers original creative works fixed in a tangible medium. USPTO grants patents/registers trademarks; the U.S. Copyright Office registers copyrights. |
| U.S. Copyright Office, *Registration Portal* | The registration portal directs users to the Electronic Copyright Office system and publishes category-specific pathways, including literary works, performing arts, visual arts, digital content, motion pictures, and photographs. The page cautions that group unpublished works require the appropriate application pathway. |
| USPTO, *Applying for Patents* | The USPTO identifies preliminary patent searching as a research step and explains that an actual nonprovisional utility application includes formal application materials such as an application data sheet, specification, drawings where necessary, and an oath/declaration. |
| WIPO, *Guide to Trade Secrets and Innovation* | WIPO describes trade-secret basics in terms of secrecy, commercial value because of secrecy, and reasonable steps to maintain secrecy. It notes that national legal treatment differs and that qualification depends on applicable law. |

These sources inform local readiness fields only. They do not establish registration, ownership, eligibility, filing status, enforceability, legal advice, or an external outcome for a ScrollVerse record.
