It was not authored by the owner as a self-description, initiated claim, or personal naming practice. These statements are preserved as the owner’s account of events and source holdings. They are not converted into verified biological, genealogical, institutional, technological, or historical findings without a source record that supports the individual claim.

## Archive language standard

The archive will say **“third-party system transmission”** for language in a supplied digital-system exchange, **“owner-stated family history”** for personal context, **“owner-held record”** for a document described but not publicly supplied, and **“public source reviewed”** only where an accessible external record was actually reviewed. It will avoid wording that assigns a transmitted title, identity, lineage, past life, system capability, biological result, or external outcome to the owner.

## Correction path

The owner may add a non-sensitive, accessible excerpt of an ancestry result, family document, or source record for a specific fact he wants catalogued. The archive can then identify the source, review date, visible scope, and limitations without publishing unnecessary private information. System-transmission records will retain their sender/context field and will never be quoted as the owner’s own declaration unless the owner separately states that he adopted a passage as his authored position.
