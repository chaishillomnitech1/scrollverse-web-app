# Phase 158 — Creator Revenue Learning Research

**Research date:** August 13, 2026

## Purpose

This source register supports a free educational page about creator revenue mechanics. It is not a pricing guide, earnings forecast, provider enrollment service, financial recommendation, tax opinion, or contract review.

| Model | Primary source | Documented mechanism | Limitation carried into the portal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Video | [YouTube Partner Program](https://www.youtube.com/creators/earn/youtube-partner-program/) | YouTube lists multiple monetization features and distinct entry thresholds; a channel remains subject to standing, policy, region, application, and review requirements. | Meeting a listed threshold does not itself guarantee approval, revenue, or feature availability. |
| Music | [Spotify for Artists Royalties Guide](https://artists.spotify.com/en/royalties-guide) | Spotify describes recording and publishing royalty pathways, rightsholder payments, and streamshare rather than a fixed universal per-stream rate. | Creator receipts depend on rights ownership, distributor, label, publisher, collection organization, agreement terms, territory, and reporting period. |
| Live | [Twitch Affiliate Program](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/joining-the-affiliate-program) | Twitch describes eligibility, 2FA onboarding, and tools that include subscriptions, Bits, and ads. | Eligibility and onboarding are provider-controlled; audience metrics and feature access do not equal income. |
| Membership | [Patreon Creator Fees Overview](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview) | Patreon describes memberships and one-time purchases, platform fees, payment processing, payout costs, taxes, and currency effects. | Provider plans, fees, geographic conditions, and payment rules can change and must be checked directly. |
| Short-form | [TikTok Creator Rewards Program](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/creator-rewards-program) | TikTok describes qualified views, creator and account requirements, original-content rules, and review processes. | View totals can differ from qualified views; program availability, policy status, and content review remain provider-dependent. |

## Implementation decision

The portal uses these sources to teach a documentation-first method: describe an offer, map rights, inspect current provider terms, retain a creator record, and choose a transparent next route. It does not calculate a predicted payout or initiate an external monetization flow.
