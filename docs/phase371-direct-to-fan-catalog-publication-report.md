# Phase 371 — Direct-to-Fan Catalog and Lookbook Publication Report

## Delivered catalog pathway

The local ScrollVerse portal now includes `/direct-fan-catalog`, a creator-controlled music, books, masters, and master-lookbook front door. The route is connected from the Home navigation and the Music page, while retaining the existing Music, Books, Fashion Artifact Atlas, Creator Collection Records, and release-readiness pathways.

The reader-facing experience has four connected functions. It presents the twelve existing local music catalog records as discovery/release-readiness records; exposes free reading paths for the Book of the Journey, VaultBook XV, Quantum Practice, and Books & Media Readiness; offers a master-lookbook pathway with item-level release requirements; and provides a browser-local release brief for a selected catalog record.

## Fail-closed commercial boundary

The direct-to-fan route does not create an offer from a catalog title alone. It requires an item-level release record with the exact version, source asset, owner instruction, master/publishing/contributor context, distribution limitation, approved price/package, and fulfillment/support path before any offer can advance. The browser-local release brief can be copied by the owner but does not persist a server record, start a checkout, issue a download, connect a wallet, mint a token, or communicate with a provider.

| Catalog lane | Current treatment |
|---|---|
| Music catalog | Discovery and release-readiness records; direct offer requires an item-level packet. |
| Free books | Direct reader routes are available where linked. |
| Paid book or edition concept | Release-gated until source, rights, price, delivery, and owner instruction are recorded. |
| Master lookbook | Visual discovery and evidence path; each future look needs image/rights, product, fulfillment, accessibility, and owner-release context. |
| Token or collectible concept | Requires a distinct metadata, contract, destination, and owner-release record; no minting or wallet action exists here. |

## Source and distribution record

The catalog preserves the owner’s stated catalog, publishing, master, and Vydia distribution context as first-party release context. It also links the general public Vydia distribution overview as background. Vydia’s public page describes distribution, supply-chain, royalty-accounting, and analytics capabilities; it does not establish an individual account, agreement, split, catalog delivery, royalty balance, or platform placement. ## Exact activation requirements

To activate any one direct offer, the owner must identify its exact title/version, source master or edition, owner release instruction, rightsholder and contributor context, distribution limitations, approved price/package, fulfillment/support method, and any optional token record. After that source packet is in place, the next stage is a separately confirmed test-mode checkout and delivery review. Production payment activation requires confirmation of the exact item, price, destination, and consequence.

## Smooth exchange and commerce ethics update

The Direct-to-Fan Catalog now also presents three creator-directed visitor lanes—**Products**, **Services**, and **Capabilities**—so a visitor can clearly distinguish work they can explore, preparation paths that are available now, and connected creative/research tools. A new **Smooth Exchange Standard** adds four readable practices: complete offer details before commitment, voluntary choice without hidden activation, private-information protection through a future approved processor-hosted flow, and clear delivery/support before an item is activated.

The guidance is based on public materials concerning online offer disclosures, payment-data separation, and accessible web interaction. It is a design and operational guide, not a claim that an item has been activated for sale or a substitute for item-specific legal, tax, or payment-provider review. [4] [5] [6]

| Smooth-exchange layer | Current catalog state |
|---|---|
| Offer clarity | Required in the item-level release brief before an offer can be considered for activation. |
| Visitor choice | Available now through free readers, discovery routes, and non-transactional catalog exploration. |
| Payment data protection | No payment form, card-data field, wallet credential field, or processor action is active in the catalog. |
| Fulfillment and support | Required in the future item-level release record; no delivery claim exists until then. |
