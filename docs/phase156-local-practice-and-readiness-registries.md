# Phase 156 — Local Practice and Readiness Registries

## Purpose

ScrollVerse now includes two optional browser-local preparation tools that help a visitor organize the work before an external handoff. They are deliberately **not** a hidden data collection or transaction layer.

| Surface | Local tool | Storage | What it supports | What it does not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Manus Hero Codex | Hero practice registry | Browser local storage | Clarifies the original-work, evidence, route, and decision sequence | Create a release record, determine rights, submit work, or share data |
| Signalworks | Readiness inspection | Browser local storage | Prompts source, permissions, route, and expectation checks | Submit work, verify a license, change a provider account, or promise an outcome |

## Transparency principle

The checklists visibly state that they retain data locally in the active browser. They do not send checklist answers to ScrollVerse or an external platform. A completed check is a visitor’s own preparation marker, not an approval, acceptance, rights decision, transaction, listing, or partnership.

## Automation boundary

No new maintenance schedule is created by these tools. Any future continuity schedule must be explicitly selected and remain limited to non-transactional, non-posting, first-party review work.
