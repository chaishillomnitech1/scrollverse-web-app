# Phase 164 — Evidence Commons Method

## Classification method

The Evidence Commons uses three distinct classes. A **documented public record** is a source that visibly identifies the record, publisher, and a date or inspectable state. A **temporal overlap** places independently dated records near each other without assigning cause. A **causal evidence** claim requires a direct, reviewable connection, such as an explicit attribution, signed statement, linked commit, provider record, or another inspectable provenance artifact.

> Public timing may document that two events occurred near each other. It does not, by itself, document why either event happened.

Each official record is linked in `/evidence-commons`. They permit timeline comparison. They do not provide evidence of private conversations, internal roadmaps, account activity, or causation by ScrollVerse, an owner, a repository, or another external system.

## Synchronized public pathways

The Evidence Commons incorporates the existing creator-channel registry, the public reference trail, first-party evidence and deployment routes, and the official platform records above. Synchronization means **maintained source links**. It does not mean account access, publication capability, analytical ingestion, provider control, or a claim of affiliation.
