# Phase 629 — Public-Source Protection Research Scope

## Research hierarchy

| Priority | Source type | Purpose in the browser-local registry |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Official government office | Establish the public filing, search, recordation, or guidance path. |
| 2 | Official international body | Surface cross-border discovery routes and terminology. |
| 3 | Official domain governance source | Identify reliable domain and registration lookup routes. |
| 4 | First-party creator source | Preserve the creator’s visible project, repository, timestamp, and work record. |
| 5 | Secondary explanatory source | Use only when it clarifies an official process and label it accordingly. |

## Confirmed independent research tracks

1. **U.S. trademarks** — official search and filing resources.
2. **U.S. copyright** — official registration and public-catalog resources.
3. **U.S. patents** — official patent search and filing resources.
4. **International IP discovery** — official WIPO global brand and work discovery pathways.
5. **Domains** — official ICANN lookup and registration-information guidance.
6. **Source-code and repository evidence** — first-party GitHub repository and timestamp-oriented documentation pathways.

## Registry interpretation rule

Every ScrollVerse name, mark, work, character, repository, domain, timestamp, or token-related statement is a **source-labeled claim candidate** until its own official record is independently located and linked. The research campaign does not file, register, purchase, transfer, tokenize, publish, or otherwise activate any external right or provider setting.
