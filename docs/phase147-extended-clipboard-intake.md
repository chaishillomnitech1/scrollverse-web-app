# Phase 147 — Extended Clipboard Intake Classification

## Evidence outcome

Eight additional clipboard attachments were classified passively. Four are UTF-8 text captures and four are Apple binary property-list containers. The text captures did not contain a visible cleared app icon, authenticated owner session, administrator assignment, provenance record, reviewer record, or release-bundle evidence. The property-list containers are retained as opaque private material; they were not decoded, extracted, executed, converted, or published.

## Handling decision

The attachments remain **unverified private reference material**, not release evidence. They cannot establish ownership, rights clearance, provider configuration, account control, administrator identity, commercial activation, or deployment state.

## Additional batches

Three subsequent clipboard pairs and one eight-file batch followed the initial review. Each pair followed the same observable pattern: UTF-8 narrative text alongside an Apple binary property-list container. A passive scan of the supplied text material did not surface direct app-icon, administrator, provenance, reviewer, private-record, cleared-asset, or release-bundle evidence. ## Required next input

The protected workflow still requires an authenticated owner in Creator Operations, a cleared app-icon asset, an explicit administrator assignment, and a linked provenance record. A smaller, clear asset or record export can be reviewed when it includes source and intended-use information.
