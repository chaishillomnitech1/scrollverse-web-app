# Phase 378 — Living Journey Experience Route Map

## Experience flow

The Living Journey Experience will use a cinematic six-part flow that routes visitors into existing, source-labeled ScrollVerse pages. The centerpiece does not replace the individual records; it provides a readable entry into them.

| Experience movement | Primary route | Supporting routes | Visible operating state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Read | `/book-of-the-journey` | `/books`, `/open-record-continuum`, `/open-knowledge-verse` | Free visual reader available locally; private materials retain their own release lanes |
| Hear | `/music` | `/direct-fan-catalog`, `/music-creator-release-readiness`, `/creator-operations-intelligence` | Public listening and discovery available; commercial activation remains fail-closed |
| See | `/junior-movies` | `/media-hub`, `/scroll-tv`, `/digital-hollywood-studio`, `/sovereign-cinema` | Route discovery available; original concepts retain creator-rights, consent, and release review |
| Study | `/sovereign-methodology` | `/synchronization-research`, `/chais-sabir-equation`, `/quantum-research` | Free creator-authored methodology and public learning pathways available locally |
| Build | `/sovereign-contribution-exchange` | `/creator-services-navigator`, `/journey-co-creation-archive` | Browser-local planning and source-labeled collaboration pathways available |
| Continue Together | `/chosen-ones-exchange` | `/governance`, `/assistive-governance`, `/direct-fan-catalog` | Community reflection and governance-learning routes available; exchange activation requires its own record |

## Main-page centerpiece plan

The Home page will receive a section that begins with the Walking Solution anchor and reveals six chapter cards as a visual sequence. Each card will name its content type, explain the visitor action, state the operating condition, and link to the corresponding route. The result is an experiential bridge from the Home hero into books, music, visuals, study, contribution, and community.

## Accessibility actions

The experience will use real links and buttons, visible focus styles, short descriptive labels, and source/rights operating states. The Book reader may later add ARIA live announcements for chapter switching. Music filters, media selectors, video embeds, and planner fields retain their own page-level accessibility work; the experience page will not mask those obligations with a decorative landing layer.

## Rights and release application

The route map includes only existing internal paths. It does not embed third-party copyrighted worlds, claim a media asset is cleared, or make an offer from a route link. Each destination retains its own rights, source, privacy, and release requirement.
