## Integration Decisions

The public navigator at `/recovery-materials` exposes only the classification, limitation, and existing first-party learning-route handoff for each record. Its cards do not expose raw recovery text, screenshots, code, corporate data, credentials, financial identifiers, private records, or unverified claims.

The following route mappings are safe at the concept level: technical workflow patterns map to `/operational-safeguards` and `/consoleverse`; contextual and retrieval patterns map to `/knowledge-context` and `/operating-pattern-navigator`; blueprint and archive language maps to `/artifact-evidence-blueprint`, `/crypto-systems-atlas`, `/institutional-systems-atlas`, and `/lawful-operating-navigator`; imaginative index material maps to `/original-systems-hub` and `/scroll-library`.

## Owner Review Requirements

Before any future public release of a recovery-derived artifact, the owner must confirm the exact source file, redactions, rights and contributor status, intended public claim, accessible release package, and final destination. Any external provider upload, listing, contract interaction, payment, collectible release, security deployment, financial product, legal filing, or account action remains a separate authenticated and authorized operation.
