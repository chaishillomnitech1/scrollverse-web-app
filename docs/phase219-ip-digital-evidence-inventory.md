# Phase 219 — IP and Digital Evidence Inventory

**Reference date:** August 14, 2026. This inventory maps evidence classes for a future public navigator. It does not determine ownership, registration, enforceability, valuation, title, infringement, patentability, trademark availability, or legal remedy.

| Evidence lane | Current source state | What it can support | What it cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public GitHub profile and repositories | Dated public-source snapshot in `/github-showcase`, with repository names, languages, licenses, and update dates. | Public code/artifact discovery, visible repository metadata, and a starting point for commit/history review. | Legal title, individual authorship, exclusive rights, commercial rights, security, deployment, provenance for an outside asset, or a patent/trademark/copyright registration. |
| Public portal pages | First-party public pages, routes, release-readiness records, and visible source labels. | What the portal stated or displayed at a particular version/date. | Independent proof of external facts, rights clearance, commercial availability, payment, or asset ownership. |
| Dated project materials | Owner-provided materials or project documents with a creation/recovery timestamp where available. | A context and intake trail when source, version, and access state are documented. | Independent authorship, authenticity, legal priority, ownership, or a public release decision. |
| Blockchain/explorer records | No specific verified address/transaction/contract record is attached to this Phase 219 inventory. | A future public explorer lookup can show the visible state of a supplied valid address, transaction, or verified contract. | Legal title to an asset, rights in off-chain media, a token’s value, or a right to sell/offer a product. |
| Domain records | No registrar, WHOIS, or DNS record is attached to this Phase 219 inventory. | A future public domain/registrar record may support technical registration/context observations. | Brand rights, trademark rights, control, website provenance, or business ownership by itself. |
| Copyright, trademark, and patent records | No official registration, application number, or search result is attached to this Phase 219 inventory. | Official agency records can be linked after an exact registration, application, mark, invention, or owner search is documented. | Any claimed registration, pending application, protection scope, or availability until the relevant official record is found and reviewed. |
| Owner-supplied business, valuation, and property assertions | Owner assertions require independent documentation before publication as verified fact. | A source-labeled perspective/context field only. | Net worth, asset ownership, commercial revenue, real-estate title, financial capacity, product availability, or legal claim. |

## Public Navigator Rule

The future public dossier will use four labels: **publicly observable record**, **owner-supplied context**, **independently verified official record**, and **missing/needs review**. It will not provide a legal conclusion, expose private files, accept sensitive records, or ask visitors to submit credentials, personal addresses, financial information, private keys, or confidential deal materials.

## Protected Review Rule

Public source links can guide discovery; exact filings, registrations, title materials, contracts, and sensitive creation files should move through a protected owner/qualified-professional review process. Before publishing any intellectual-property assertion, the project must identify the exact asset/mark/work, source identifier, date, relevant jurisdiction, rights holder, and permitted public wording.

## Initial Official Finding

The U.S. Copyright Office states that copyright protects original works of authorship once fixed in a tangible medium, while registration provides a public record of the claim and carries additional U.S. enforcement consequences. This supports a public route that distinguishes an original work, a dated source artifact, a registration/record number, and an ownership/legal conclusion rather than treating any one field as proof of every other field.
