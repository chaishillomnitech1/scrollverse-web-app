# Phase 399 — Creator-Directed Visitor Choice and Financial Highways Audit

**Record date:** 2026-08-17 EDT  
**Scope:** `/direct-fan-catalog`, `/sovereign-contribution-exchange`, and the registered Support Highways destination record.

## Owner Direction

The portal presents owner-provided work and lets each visitor choose a free learning path, a future item-specific purchase path, a contribution path, an exchange path, or no transaction at all. The portal does not require visitors to purchase access to the free knowledge routes.

## Security and Privacy Boundary

The live visitor experience uses public destinations only. It does not request passwords, one-time codes, account numbers, card data, wallet seed phrases, recovery codes, payout details, or other private credentials. Browser-local release briefs remain preparation records and do not create a product, payment, transfer, checkout, wallet action, or delivery obligation.

## Correction Path

To activate an additional public support lane, the owner supplies the exact public-facing destination URL and its intended purpose. To activate a direct offer, the owner supplies the corresponding item-level release packet. This record does not modify provider settings, payout settings, or any financial configuration.
