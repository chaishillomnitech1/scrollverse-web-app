# Phase 339 — Authoritative IP Evidence & Readiness Framework

**Purpose:** local evidence preparation for ScrollVerse-created works. **Operating state:** research and readiness; no registration, clearance, filing, enforcement, license, or legal outcome asserted.

The U.S. Patent and Trademark Office distinguishes trademark, patent, and copyright categories: source-identifying marks, technical inventions, and original creative works fixed in a tangible medium, respectively.[1] The U.S. Copyright Office registration portal supplies category-specific pathways, while the USPTO presents formal patent-application requirements beyond preliminary research.[2] [3] Trade-secret treatment requires special care because protection standards depend on applicable law; WIPO describes secrecy, commercial value from secrecy, and reasonable protective steps as common fundamentals.[4]

| Work or asset | Local evidence packet | Readiness boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Creative work | Version/date, creator, contributor roles, source files, accessible description, intended use | Registration pathway and any license decision remain separate |
| Brand identifier | Exact mark, goods/services context, first-use/source record, search date | Search and application outcome are not inferred |
| Technical method | Inventor/contributor chronology, disclosure boundary, technical description, prior-art question | Preliminary search is not a patent application or eligibility conclusion |
| Confidential method | Custodian, access need, confidentiality classification, permitted recipients, protective-control record | No trade-secret qualification or enforcement conclusion is made |
| Licensed material | Licensor/source, scope, purpose, territory/duration if known, attribution and access condition | No permission is presumed from a reference or link |

The readiness record should preserve a date, source, limitation, correction route, and item-specific release decision. It does not replace qualified legal review, official filing procedures, or a public-record search.
