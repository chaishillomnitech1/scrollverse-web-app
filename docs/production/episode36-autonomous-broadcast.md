# Episode 36 — The Autonomous Broadcast

> **Working production state:** Research. This is an original browser-local script prepared from owner-supplied production reference. It does not represent an active broadcast, infrastructure deployment, automated distribution service, or live royalty system.

## Logline

On the night a citywide power fluctuation threatens to turn every signal into noise, Curtis Bordley and the Sector 48 team choose a different measure of readiness: preserve the story, document each handoff, and let the audience decide when the transmission is ready to leave the room.

## Character frame

| Character | Episode function | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Curtis Bordley | Holds the final human release decision. | Grounded, deliberate, present. |
| Aria Nova | Reads the room, the archive, and the audience pathway. | Clear, attentive, exact. |
| Jaxson Vance | Reconciles the physical studio with the browser-local record. | Practical, patient, observant. |
| S.O.P.H.I.A. | A studio interface that names what is present and what is still missing. | Calm, concise, never overclaims. |

## Scene 1 — Sector 48, pre-dawn

*A circular worktable glows beneath a ceiling grid. One wall carries a map of completed story records. Another shows a single empty broadcast panel, labelled “RELEASED ONLY BY RECORD.”*

**S.O.P.H.I.A.**

The room is ready to prepare. The room is not ready to pretend.

**JAXSON**

The archive is here. The visual routes are here. The audio master is still an open space.

**ARIA**

Then that is what we say. A clear record travels farther than a loud assumption.

*Curtis places an old field recorder at the center of the table. Its screen reads: “Episode 36 — working slate.”*

**CURTIS**

We do not build a signal to escape the people who hear it. We build a door, a map, and a place to return to.

## Scene 2 — The archive wall

*Aria raises a hand. Story titles travel across the wall: collections, visual worlds, study routes, and unfinished transmissions. None carries a false “live” badge.*

**ARIA**

Every signal begins as a source. Who brought it? When did it arrive? What does it actually say? Those questions make the route strong.

**S.O.P.H.I.A.**

Source status: available in the room. Release status: requires an owner record.

**JAXSON**

That is not a delay. That is the way a story stays itself after it leaves us.

## Scene 3 — The royalty record

*A small, silent ledger panel appears. Its columns are empty: reference ID, submitted transaction reference, declared amount, recorded time, review state. There are no wallet controls, no send buttons, and no price ticker.*

**CURTIS**

No one in this room moves a thing that is not theirs to move.

**ARIA**

But if a record arrives, it deserves a place where it can be read without being rewritten.

**S.O.P.H.I.A.**

Local record design: ready. Incoming rows: none.

**JAXSON**

That is the honest beginning. An empty ledger is better than an invented balance.

## Scene 4 — The choice

*The building’s outer lights soften. Beyond the glass, dawn moves through the street. The team stands around the empty broadcast panel.*

**ARIA**

What do we call it when the whole system is ready except the one decision that should remain human?

**CURTIS**

Respect.

**S.O.P.H.I.A.**

Release prompt prepared. No external channel connected.

**JAXSON**

Then the broadcast is not an order. It is an invitation.

## Scene 5 — The autonomous broadcast

*Curtis turns the field recorder toward the audience. The wall transforms into a familiar browser-local route: stories, visuals, study, and a source record connected by clear lines.*

**CURTIS**

This is the broadcast: the work is here, the source is here, and the next step is yours to choose.

**ARIA**

Not endless noise. A living library with doors.

**S.O.P.H.I.A.**

Episode record complete. Broadcast state: narrative preparation.

*The field recorder clicks off. The city has become bright enough to see every label on the wall.*
