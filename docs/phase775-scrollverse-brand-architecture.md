# Phase 775 — ScrollVerse Brand Architecture

## Purpose

This browser-local brand architecture organizes the ScrollVerse as a connected creative and learning system. It uses visible pathways rather than broad status claims so visitors can understand what is available, what is under research, and what requires an owner-controlled release.

## Architecture fields

| Field | Role in the browser-local architecture | State boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source and origin | Connected source registry, protected records, chronology, and visual evidence. | A source record preserves context; it does not independently establish rights, ownership, legal status, or operational capability. |
| Creative systems | Music, media, visual work, fashion, catalog, story worlds, and Digital Mirror pathways. | A browser-local creative record is not a public release, listing, production order, or marketplace action. |
| Research and learning | Apex Research, systems blueprint records, Game Worlds research, and learning pathways. | A study is not a certification, professional advice, external integration, or deployed system. |
| Portfolio and readiness | Portfolio evidence, capital and partnership readiness, local proposal, and asset evidence records. | A readiness record is not an appraisal, valuation, offer, funding, contract, financial product, or transaction. |
| Partnership highways | Cross-discipline contribution, evidence, participation state, and release condition. | A highway does not recruit, employ, appoint, contract, compensate, or create an external partnership. |
| External release gates | Exact named actions that require a source, destination, and owner-controlled decision. | No provider, account, publishing, commerce, finance, marketplace, or infrastructure activity is activated by an architecture page. |

## Current-state taxonomy

* **Available now**: A browser-local route, source record, research record, or preparation tool is present.
* **Research**: A documented study or design question is being explored without a claim of deployed capability.
* **Requires owner release**: A pathway has a defined external next step but cannot proceed without its exact evidence, destination, and owner-controlled decision.

## Brand rhythm

**Source → Create → Study → Organize → Collaborate → Release.** Each stage has a browser-local record and a visible transition to the next appropriate route.
