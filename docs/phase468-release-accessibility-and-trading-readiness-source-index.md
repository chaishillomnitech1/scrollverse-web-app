# Phase 468 — Release Accessibility and Trading Readiness Source Index

**Status:** Initial public-source research record. This index supports route design and review only; it does not configure a storefront, certify accessibility, recommend an investment, connect a provider, or activate an external action.

## Release-accessibility findings

| Source | Public guidance carried into the release record | Applied route boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, *Introduction to Web Accessibility* [1] | Accessible web experiences are designed so people with disabilities can perceive, understand, navigate, interact with, and contribute to the web. | Each release record should preserve an accessible visitor description, understandable navigation, and a correction path; the route does not claim formal conformance. |
| Shopify developer documentation, *Accessibility best practices for Shopify apps* [2] | Shopify frames accessibility around WCAG principles and calls out keyboard control, semantic page structure, descriptive product-image alt text, product-price/availability communication, clear links, and labeled fields. | Product-release preparation should include accessible copy, alt-text review, keyboard-aware interaction review, and clear customer-facing labels before any item-specific provider release. |

## Source-handling note

The sources are used as public design references. They do not establish that a particular ScrollVerse page, Shopify store, theme, product, checkout, or payment flow is compliant, configured, or live. A future release still needs item-specific accessibility review, source/version context, rights/contributor review, customer terms, and a verified destination.

## Trading-readiness findings

| Source | Public guidance carried into the readiness record | Applied route boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission, *Learning Resources* [3] | The CFTC’s public resources emphasize researching trading risks, recognizing fraud, and approaching digital-finance and AI-enabled deception with caution. | A source-labeled readiness record should separate research from execution, retain a correction path, and never treat a copied status or unverified system as a trading instruction. |
| U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, Investor.gov, *Introduction to Investing* [4] | Investor.gov states that investments involve risk and that markets fluctuate; its material frames risk management and diversification as educational concepts. | ScrollVerse’s control architecture preserves explicit limits, stop conditions, provider/account scope, and a no-outcome/no-live-order boundary. It does not give personalized advice or endorse a strategy. |

## Trading-source handling note

These public regulator sources are used solely to support transparent risk-education and readiness design. They do not validate a strategy, assess an individual’s circumstances, create a portfolio, provide tax or legal advice, choose an asset, or authorize a provider connection, custody workflow, funding action, or order.
