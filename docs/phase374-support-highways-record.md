## Privacy and exchange boundary

No visitor should enter a password, one-time code, bank account or routing number, debit/credit card data, wallet seed phrase, private payment link, government ID, customer data, or private contract material into ScrollVerse. The support section is an outbound discovery interface, not a payment processor, wallet, bank, escrow, tax service, or transaction record.

When a visitor chooses an external destination, the payment provider’s own interface and terms control that exchange. A future item-level support or purchase offer must still name the exact work or purpose, source state, approved price/package if any, delivery/support path, and owner release record. The portal will not claim a payment is complete, reserve a benefit, or promise delivery from an external-transfer click.

## Correction path

The owner can activate a named future lane by supplying the exact public destination URL or handle, its intended purpose, and confirmation it is appropriate for public display. The portal will then record the supplied source and add a visible external-link label. Private account numbers and credentials will not be published.
