# ScrollVerse Intellectual Property Readiness

> **Working record — for qualified legal review before reliance, filing, or enforcement.** This is an operational checklist, not legal advice or a statement that any asset is registered, cleared, owned, or enforceable.

## First-Party Protection Model

The repository now keeps five linked records for each important asset: a working asset name, its category, an origin note, a steward, and a review state. The purpose is to preserve evidence and reduce ambiguity before public release, commercial use, provider submission, or a formal IP filing.

| Asset class | Repository record | Release condition |
|---|---|---|
| Names and word marks | Use the `WORD_MARK` inventory category and record the first intended use. | Complete a relevant clearance process and review the proposed goods/services before a filing or broad public claim. |
| Logos and sigils | Store original concept files outside the app bundle, preserve prompts/source notes, and assign a steward. | Confirm originality and source chain; complete mark-specific clearance before commercial rollout. |
| Code and blueprints | Keep source controlled, use code review, preserve release history, and restrict confidential material. | Review third-party dependencies, contributor terms, and deployment scope. |
| Visuals, audio, and story assets | Keep source, author, permissions, and AI-assistance disclosures with the project record. | Confirm rights and third-party material status before distribution. |
| Creator submissions | Keep private by default in the Creator Workspace and use provenance records. | Do not convert a provenance note into an ownership, license, valuation, or sale decision without qualified review. |

## Formal Trademark and Copyright Pathways

For a U.S. trademark application, the USPTO identifies preparation steps that include similarity searches, application requirements, goods/services, drawings, and specimens. [1] The USPTO also provides a public trademark search system and information about likelihood of confusion and design search codes. [2]

For copyright registration, the U.S. Copyright Office provides a registration portal covering literary works, performing arts, visual arts, computer programs, databases, websites, motion pictures, and other categories. [3] For works involving generative AI, the Office maintains dedicated guidance and policy resources; project records should therefore preserve the human-authored contribution and any material AI assistance used. [4]

## Do Not Publish in This Repository

Never commit account credentials, private keys, seed phrases, personal identity documents, unredacted contracts, private customer records, full trademark-search work product, unlicensed media, or confidential partner materials. Store only necessary metadata and protected references in the application; keep sensitive evidence in access-controlled storage.

## Required Professional Review Gates

Before a formal trademark filing, copyright filing, assignment, license, demand letter, takedown, commercial launch, provider submission, or enforcement action, verify the asset list with a qualified intellectual-property professional. A repository record helps organize the facts but does not replace clearance, title analysis, filing decisions, or legal advice.
