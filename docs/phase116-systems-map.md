# Phase 116 — ScrollVerse Systems Map

The Systems Map is an original first-party navigation model for the ScrollVerse portal. It connects existing public routes, creator workflows, digital-intelligence spaces, knowledge archives, story-world surfaces, and carefully gated activation paths.

## Status Discipline

The map uses five visible states: implemented, in progress, external optional, conceptual, and gated. These labels make the platform legible without asserting external partnership, certification, authority, real-world infrastructure, or control over any provider.

| Layer | First-party purpose | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Public ScrollVerse | Orientation, discovery, identity, and system status. | It is a portal experience, not a replacement for external internet infrastructure. |
| Creator & Culture Network | Consent-led original work, collaboration, provenance, and transparent handoffs. | Creator records do not settle legal ownership or provider outcomes. |
| Digital Intelligence Commons | Guidance, learning, opt-in exchange, and clear activity states. | No autonomous authority, hidden activity, or professional decision-making is represented. |
| Knowledge, Archive & Research | Contextual study, archives, and source-labelled exploration. | Research, story, and sacred material stay distinct from technical claims. |
| Digital Worlds & Interactive Space | Entertainment, immersive routes, and world-building. | No property, location, or real-world infrastructure claim is implied. |
| Careful Activation | Evidence, review, and authorization gates for higher-impact functions. | A gated design is not an active product, service, or transaction path. |
