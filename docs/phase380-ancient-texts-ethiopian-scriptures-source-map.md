# Phase 380 — Ancient Texts, Ethiopian Manuscripts, and Archive Source Map

## Purpose and operating state

This source map extends the ScrollVerse Knowledge Library with **research** shelves for ancient texts, manuscript archives, historical scientific collections, strategy, financial literacy, cosmic science, and art history. The shelf preserves a clear distinction between primary archives, catalog records, scholarly editions, public research collections, and later interpretation.

It does not call every historic work “lost,” endorse a particular theological conclusion, or translate an institutional catalog into a ScrollVerse ownership, release, or affiliation claim. Each card opens the named organization’s own site and remains subject to that organization’s access and rights terms.

## Ethiopian biblical and ancient-scripture research

The Ethiopian research shelf is framed as a study of **surviving Geʽez manuscript traditions and institutional descriptions**. It explicitly distinguishes manuscript evidence, catalog metadata, translations and scholarly editions, canon traditions, incomplete evidence, and modern interpretation.

> Cambridge’s Ethiopian Manuscripts collection describes a digitized sample of Ethiopian works and includes named Geʽez codices, including an Old Testament manuscript, Gospel books, and texts connected to the Book of Enoch and patriarchal testaments. The collection therefore appears in the portal as a source-labeled manuscript path—not as a universal claim about all materials or a replacement for scholarly study. [2]

| Ethiopian research pathway | Source type | Use in the library |

| Cambridge Digital Library Ethiopian Manuscripts | Primary manuscript archive and catalog | Explore digitized Geʽez manuscript witnesses and collection descriptions. [2] |

| Beta maṣāḥǝft | Scholarly research collection | Follow a research environment for Ethiopian and Eritrean manuscript codicology and textual transmission. [4] |
| Princeton Digital PUL Ethiopic Manuscripts | Institutional catalog | Access structured records for Ethiopic codices and related collections. [15] |
| Bodleian Ethiopian and Eritrean collections | Primary manuscript archive | Review digitized historical Ethiopic manuscript holdings. [16] |
