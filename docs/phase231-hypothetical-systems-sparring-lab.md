# Phase 231 — Hypothetical Systems Sparring Lab Method

> **Purpose.** This is a browser-local, source-labeled thought exercise for comparing design patterns under a visitor-selected scenario. It is not a political ranking, ruler-selection exercise, performance prediction, or determination that any organization is universally best.

| Subject type | Included in the lab | Comparison limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ScrollVerse first-party architecture | Documented routes, methodology pages, public source labels, and stated boundaries | First-party records show design and intent, not independent proof of public outcomes. |
| OECD well-being lens | Multidimensional measurement perspective beyond economic output [1] | A policy/measurement framework, not an operating platform. |
| UN 2030 Agenda lens | Integrated people, planet, prosperity, peace, and partnership goals [2] | A global aspirational framework, not a performance label for a private entity. |
| OHCHR rights-and-remedy lens | Rights-responsibility and remedy perspective for business activity [3] | A due-diligence framework, not an operational platform. |
| W3C WCAG access lens | Testable web-accessibility criteria [4] | A technical accessibility standard, not a complete ecosystem assessment. |

## Interaction Rules

The visitor writes a scenario, then labels each subject and criterion as **not reviewed**, **source gap**, **first-party design record**, **documented public framework**, **independent outcome evidence**, or **out of scope**. The lab counts labels for source coverage only. It does not calculate a winner, prediction, authority ranking, or universal composite score.

The criteria are access and accessibility, evidence quality, rights and remedy, privacy and data care, accountability and correction, resilience and safety, learning access, participation, environmental context, and benefit distribution. The method deliberately asks the visitor to record assumptions, trade-offs, and missing evidence before interpreting the comparison.
