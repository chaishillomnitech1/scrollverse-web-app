# Phase 106 — Public Product-Pattern Research

## Purpose and Boundary

This research identifies transferable interaction patterns for an **original** ScrollVerse convergence world. It does not authorize copying source code, visual layout, trademarks, recommendation models, creator-payout systems, identity graphs, chat infrastructure, or virtual-currency systems from any referenced platform.

## ScrollVerse Design Principles Derived from the Research

The first convergence layer should use **intent before engagement**. People should be able to choose whether they are entering to learn, create, collaborate, share media, build a collection, or join a community. Recommendations should be explainable, consent-based, and reversible. Original creator and IP records should preserve source, reviewer, rights, and approval status rather than treating discovery as proof of ownership.

The Legion can provide assistive governance: explain rules, surface conflicts, route requests, and preserve consent. It must not present itself as a court, bank, exchange, legal authority, or autonomous decision-maker. High-impact decisions require accountable human review and a documented policy.

## Initial Implementation Priorities

1. Create an original **Convergence Commons** route for creator, project, idea, and IP discovery.
2. Use explicit topic preferences and transparent reason labels rather than a hidden engagement-ranking model.
3. Add source-attributed project records, collaboration invitations, and protected asset or IP review handoffs.
4. Keep marketplace, financial exchange, token, banking, payment, and real-estate functions disabled until legal, security, custody, identity, and operational controls are separately designed and reviewed.
