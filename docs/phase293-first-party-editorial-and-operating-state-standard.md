# Phase 293 — First-Party Editorial and Direct Operating-State Standard

## Editorial Position

ScrollVerse presents the owner’s creative intent, original identity, worldbuilding, research interests, and release vision in a confident first-party voice. The work is not diluted by retreat-style framing. Its strength comes from being direct about **what the owner presents**, what source/version record is available, and what state the asset is actually in.

> **Public presentation rule:** State the work, its source, its date or version, and its current operating state. Invite visitors to explore, contribute corrections, or decide whether the work resonates with them.

## Direct Operating States

| State | Use when | Required visible information |
|---|---|---|
| **Available now** | The specific asset or page has a completed source/version record, accessibility package appropriate to its format, release manifest, and owner release for its named destination | Title, source/date or version, access path, credits where applicable, accessibility route, correction path |
| **Research** | The work is a first-party idea, draft, source record, story/score/design concept, or investigation still in development | Intended use, source/context, known limits, next evidence or creation step, correction route |
| **Requires owner release** | An asset, public interaction, provider, contract, publication, distribution, product, or participant contribution needs an explicit final decision | Exact missing record or decision, owner-controlled gate, and non-active state |

## Evidence That Strengthens First-Party Work

| Record | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Owner statement | Preserves the creator’s direct expression of authorship, intent, and meaning |
| Source/version record | Connects a public item to a named original, date, version, repository entry, or verified local source |
| Contributor and character/brand record | Makes roles, credit, permission, name/likeness/mark questions, and scope visible |
| Accessibility record | Gives visual, video, music, book, and learning work an alt-text, caption, transcript, description, or youth-aware access path |
| Release manifest | Identifies final title, master/version, destination, access choice, owner approval, date, and correction route |

## Story-Universe Application

Digital Ships and Manus Hero are presented as original ScrollVerse browser-local story experiences. Their page-level claims are limited to what exists today: a story compass, local prompts, selection tools, voice-ready cues, accessible quiet/reduced-motion paths, and release-readiness links. Future episodes, public channels, visitor submissions, animation, music, products, and tokenization are not presented as active until their individual source, rights, accessibility, contributor, and owner-release records are complete.

## Reference-Claim Boundary

The supplied Mirror-Match and dashboard material offers reusable design language for transparent labels, calm dashboards, safe reference intake, and visible audit concepts. It is not presented as proof of an active Mirror-Match engine, self-verification score, immutable ledger, repository seal, provider isolation, security control, test count, current runtime, or synchronized deployment. Those statements require evidence from the actual approved project controls before ScrollVerse may state them as operating facts.

## Correction Standard

Every source-labeled public record should provide a route to correct a title, credit, date, source link, accessibility note, or release state. A correction strengthens the archive because it keeps a versioned history while making room for new evidence.
