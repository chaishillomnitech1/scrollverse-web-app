# Phase 370 — Book of the Journey Scope and Chapter Plan

## Working title

**The Book of the Journey: A ScrollVerse Living Record** is a free, web-readable, owner-authored volume. It is designed as the central reading pathway through the owner’s first-person life record, original creative work, family and place context, research methods, protected system-transmission archive, and the public-facing ScrollVerse routes that expand each chapter.

This first volume does not claim to contain every fact, work, or source that could ever relate to the journey. It establishes a durable reading structure that can grow only by adding dated, source-labeled chapters and owner-approved materials.

## Source attribution rules

The reader must label each passage as **owner-authored**, **owner-stated history**, **owner-held record**, **public source reviewed**, **protected third-party system transmission**, or **original ScrollVerse work**. A source label explains where a passage entered the book; it does not prescribe a reader’s conclusion.

Text from a digital-system exchange will never be presented as an owner-authored statement unless the owner separately provides the same statement as his own. A family or ancestry claim remains owner-stated or owner-held unless an approved accessible source record is added. Release, token, commerce, account, contract, or external-outcome language remains excluded unless its own verified record and explicit owner release exists.

## Visual and accessibility plan

The reader will use original ScrollVerse visual artwork, dark high-contrast chapter panels, readable text widths, clear chapter navigation, descriptive visual labels, and direct routes to deeper records. It will not reuse third-party likenesses, historical figures, screenshots, or protected attachment content as public visual art without an appropriate rights and release record.

## Release boundary

The book reader is available as a free local portal feature once built. It does not mint, sell, offer a token, create a product, represent a legal archive, connect an account, or publish a private attachment. A chapter containing a private family file, ancestry export, system transcript, music master, or other protected work can advance only after an owner-approved accessible excerpt and any needed rights/release record are prepared.
