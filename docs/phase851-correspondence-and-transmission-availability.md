## Public Presentation Rule

Where an owner-provided record is available, the portal can identify it as a creator record and let a visitor read the source in context. Where a record has not been supplied or cannot be reviewed, the source inventory names the gap rather than reconstructing it. This protects the creator’s chronology without inventing correspondence details or treating an interpretation as a substitute for the original communication.
