# Phase 672 — Non-Posting Campaign Cadence Planner

The Campaign Activation Center now provides two browser-local planning modes. **Story rhythm** sequences a source-led brief, visual or route handoff, and review of copy, accessibility, and destination. **Event rhythm** sequences an event/release name, approved asset pairing, and a recorded destination, visibility, and exact release decision.

## Browser Verification

The browser-local `/campaign-activation-center` route was opened after implementation. It rendered the non-posting cadence section, Story and Event controls, the selected Story rhythm steps, and the provider launch gate. The visible copy confirms that no recurring job, content post, outreach, or provider contact is created from the local planner.

## Boundary

This planner is a local review aid, not a scheduled task or publication mechanism. An external launch still needs a named destination, intended-use scope, visibility, exact asset, and final owner release decision.
