### `scrollverse-4j2bciqz.manus.space` — Archive & Connections

Observed 2026-08-14. The site presents a restrained archival reading-room pattern: a single primary claim, contextual navigation grouped into **Music**, **Media**, **Code**, and **Sources**, then a layered flow into creator listening links, destination records, repositories, and conversation references. Its most compatible first-party pattern is the repeated status vocabulary—such as public destination, public source, source record, and open repository—which helps visitors distinguish a link category before leaving the site.

The ScrollVerse portal can adopt this as a source-labeled discovery pattern: one pathway for evidence and provenance, another for creative media, with clear link labels and an internal route-health check.

### `scrollverse-jqwuy5bt.manus.space` — ScrollVerse V2 Command Center

Observed 2026-08-14. The site organizes many concept lanes in a persistent command-center navigation: music intelligence, NFT collections, treasury readiness, AI workspace, gateways, governance, connector inventory, discovery, composition, and manifests. Its strongest reusable pattern is a consistent **preview-first** posture: every lane exposes a status and describes the type of next action while clearly stating that broadcast, purchase, mint, and external writes are disabled from the surface.
