# Creator Services Research Record

> **Boundary.** This record supports evidence preparation, not financial advice, legal advice, a recovery guarantee, accounting, collections, representation, or a promise of payment.

## Key Source-Backed Patterns

The UK Intellectual Property Office states that accurate, complete music metadata is essential to crediting and paying music creators, and that incomplete metadata can delay payment or leave creators unpaid. [1] Spotify’s Loud & Clear site describes the streaming royalty process at a market level, but it is not an artist-specific accounting or payout statement. [2] A creator-service brief should therefore preserve the source, date, reporting period, identifier, and factual question before any authorized provider or professional follow-up.

Spotify for Authors describes audiobook author profiles, audience insights, and direct publishing pathways as provider-specific capabilities. [3] Amazon KDP and Apple Books each set their own metadata, cover, language, territory, file, and publication requirements. [4] [5] W3C EPUB Accessibility provides an accessibility standard for digital publications. [6] A free first-party route and a paid or external-platform edition must therefore retain distinct source, rights, accessibility, metadata, delivery, and owner/provider-release records.
