# Phase 106 — ScrollVerse Convergence World Roadmap

## Thesis

ScrollVerse should be a **creator-first world**, not a copied social network or a financial platform presented before its controls exist. Its first responsibility is to help people discover a path, express an original perspective, attribute the source of an idea or asset, find suitable collaborators, and move into the right existing ScrollVerse space. The implementation should favor intent, provenance, and consent over opaque ranking or attention extraction.

## Original Architecture

| Layer | Purpose | First ScrollVerse expression | Safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Journey Layer** | Help people choose what they are here to do. | `/journey` intent zones and authenticated progress. | Explicit preference and analytics consent. |
| **Convergence Commons** | Connect creators, thinkers, designers, and builders around an original idea. | `/commons` discovery paths, project stages, and handoffs to Think Space. | No automatic ownership, payment, or partnership claim. |
| **Personal Signature System** | Preserve the creative DNA of an independent brand or project. | Brand signature cards: origin, regional anchor, palette, material language, and rights state. | Source and rights review before public asset use. |
| **IP and Asset Review** | Record the source and review state of submitted work. | Protected fashion-review console and archive-intake records. | Admin-only review, evidence trail, and fail-closed approval gates. |
| **Assistive Governance** | Explain policies and route decisions fairly. | Legion guidance, escalation links, and a documented human-review boundary. | No autonomous legal authority or final adjudication. |
| **Exchange-Ready Future Layer** | Prepare future interoperable commerce only after safeguards exist. | Clearly labelled readiness architecture, not activation. | Banking, payment, exchange, token, real-estate, and custody functions remain disabled pending qualified review. |

## Personal Signature Design System

Every independent collection can carry a portable signature record without depending on an outside brand. The record contains an **origin statement**, **regional or cultural anchor chosen by the creator**, **color language**, **material intent**, **symbol vocabulary**, **story arc**, and **rights-review state**. It makes room for unlimited original colorways and silhouettes while keeping collaboration separate from ownership.

> A fusion is an optional documented collaboration. It does not replace the identity, authorship, or commercial readiness of an independent ScrollVerse brand.

## Phased Path

The first phase is discovery and provenance. It links people to Think Space, the Brand Studio, the Legion, the Journey map, and protected review paths. The second phase may add consented creator profiles, collaboration requests, and project records. The third phase may add independently reviewed marketplace primitives. Regulated financial, exchange, payment, real-estate, and token functions require separate security, identity, consumer-protection, tax, and legal work before activation.

## Success Criteria

The public experience should make it easy to answer four questions: **What can I create? Who can I collaborate with? How do I preserve my original signature? What is the responsible next step?** It must not imply that a creator owns another person’s work, that a partnership exists without documentation, or that a financial or legal result is guaranteed.
