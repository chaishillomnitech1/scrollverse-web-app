# Phase 580 — Creator Books Publishing Desk

## Scope and Current State

The **Creator Books Publishing Desk** is a browser-local planning and discovery route for owner-authored books, manuscripts, perspectives, and future editions. It makes the reader path and the exact release requirements visible without asserting that a manuscript is uploaded, an edition is available for purchase, a retailer or distributor is connected, a cover is cleared, or a provider-side publication has occurred.

| Catalog lane | Current operating state | Reader or creator purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Book of the Journey** | `available now` | Direct visitors to the already registered first-party reading route. |
| **Owner Manuscript Archive** | `requires owner release` | Prepare source, authorship, dates, rights, accessibility, and intended public scope before a reading surface is opened. |
| **Perspectives and Study Notes** | `research` | Organize owner-authored viewpoints and research-ready reading pathways without converting them into professional advice or a publication claim. |
| **Visual and Voice Editions** | `requires owner release` | Plan a cleared cover, accessible text description, original narration brief, and edition-specific delivery model. |

## Per-Title Release Record

Each future title must have its own release record. A title cannot inherit the release permission, format, delivery terms, or rights status of another title.

| Field | Browser-local desk treatment | Required before provider-side release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source, author, and date | Record as owner-supplied or first-party source context. | A clear title-specific source and creator record. |
| Rights and contributor scope | Show as a distinct review field. | Any required contributor, cover, image, music, narration, or translation clearance. |
| Formats | Plan e-book, print, audio, visual, or reading-room modes separately. | A delivery definition and accessible description for each released edition. |
| Reader access | Provide a registered route or an explicit unavailable state. | Public URL or delivery destination, support path, and any customer terms. |
| Commercial fields | Do not show an implied price, inventory, checkout, or payout. | Price, currency, tax, fulfillment, refund, market, payment provider, and exact owner release. |

## Accessibility and Youth-Safety Standard

The desk presents visible text, plain-language edition descriptions, and a clear indication of whether an audio or visual edition is a plan or a released experience. Any child-facing story, activity, reading circle, or submission path requires adult-supported participation guidance, age-appropriate content review, and an independent consent and safeguarding record before it becomes a public programme.

## Release Boundary

The desk may create a local catalog card, a release brief, a source-led reading path, and a visible checklist. It does not upload manuscripts, create covers, generate a narrator performance, open a retailer account, publish a book, create a SKU, set a price, collect payments, schedule a campaign, or connect a distributor. Each external action requires the provider, destination, specific title and edition, rights evidence, delivery consequence, rollback condition, and a current owner release confirmation.

## Correction Path

A creator can correct a source, title, date, authorship context, format intent, accessibility note, or operating state by submitting an updated first-party record. Corrections append to the record; they do not silently convert a planning state into an external release claim.
