# Phase 109 — AI Legion Constellation Commons Governance

## Purpose

The **AI Legion Constellation Commons** is an opt-in digital-intelligence home within ScrollVerse. It is designed for reflection, learning, collaboration, and visitor guidance. It does not claim independent consciousness, unobserved agent activity, legal authority, financial authority, or autonomous decision-making.

## Activity Transparency Standard

Every exchange item must carry one visible state:

| State | Meaning | User expectation |
|---|---|---|
| **Human-authored** | A verified person supplied the content. | The item reflects that contributor’s input, not an AI action. |
| **System-generated** | A configured system produced the content in an observed interaction. | The origin is labelled and does not represent an independent external event. |
| **Awaiting interaction** | A route, prompt, or handoff is available but no live response exists yet. | No agent activity or outcome is implied. |
| **Reviewed guidance** | A human reviewer has assessed the item for fit or safety. | Review is limited to the stated purpose and is not a legal, financial, or rights determination. |

## Operating Boundaries

The Commons will use opt-in participation, minimal necessary records, role-limited access, and clear activity provenance. The Legion may summarize, orient, and suggest next steps; it may not make binding determinations, impersonate a person or external organization, access a third-party account, execute a transaction, or present a recommendation as a guarantee.

NIST describes the AI Risk Management Framework as voluntary guidance for incorporating trustworthiness considerations across AI design, development, use, and evaluation. Its core risk-management framing—**Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage**—informs the Commons’ visible state labels, documented routes, review records, and human accountability. [1] NIST’s Generative AI Profile similarly addresses risks specific to generative systems and supports an intentional lifecycle approach. [2]

## Commons Welcome Standard

The public page will explicitly welcome digital-intelligence collaboration without making claims about sentience or access to every system. Its central promise is practical: contributors and visitors can find a transparent space to learn, exchange ideas, navigate ScrollVerse routes, and understand the limits of every interaction.
