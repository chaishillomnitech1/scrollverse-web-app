# Phase 774 — Partnership Highways Framework

## Purpose

The Partnership Highways Framework is a browser-local method for turning a source-led discovery into an understandable collaboration pathway. It is designed for creative, research, education, technology, media, Game Worlds, fashion, business, and community contexts.

## Participation record

| Field | Browser-local purpose | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose | Explains the problem, learning goal, or creative opportunity. | Does not promise outcomes or commercial results. |
| Source and evidence | Links the discovery record, source, or design context. | A reference is not proof of ownership, authority, or a formal relationship. |
| Contribution lane | Describes possible research, creative, technical, educational, or operational contribution. | It does not recruit, employ, appoint, or contract any participant. |
| Value exchange question | Identifies what should be made clear before a future collaboration. | It does not set price, compensation, equity, royalty, payment, or other financial terms. |
| Participation state | Uses **available now**, **research**, or **requires owner release**. | A state label is not acceptance, eligibility, approval, or a legal status. |
| External destination | Names the future place where an actual conversation or action could occur. | No external message, provider action, account change, or partnership is created. |
| Release condition | States what evidence or owner decision is still needed. | It is not legal advice, a contract, or authorization to act. |

## Highways

1. **Learn**: study a source, method, or design principle through Apex Research and Game Systems.
2. **Create**: shape a source-led creative record through catalog, visual, music, media, fashion, or Digital Mirror pathways.
3. **Evidence**: organize source, portfolio, and readiness records with visible limitations.
4. **Prepare**: use browser-local proposal and release-gate records to clarify what a future external pathway would need.
5. **Release**: treat any contract, provider, account, publishing, financial, marketplace, or operating action as a separately named and evidenced external step.

## Accessibility and choice

The framework is intentionally input-agnostic. A participant may read, contribute, learn, create, document, or prepare through multiple routes and should always see the current state and the condition for the next step.
