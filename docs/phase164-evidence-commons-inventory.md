## Evidence Commons scope

The public space will synchronize **links and records**, not private accounts. It will support open reading and discussion through source categories, direct routes, dates where known, and clear evidence classes. It will not claim that public platform changes were caused by a ScrollVerse conversation or project artifact without direct, independently reviewable causal evidence.
