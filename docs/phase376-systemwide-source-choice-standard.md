# Phase 376 — ScrollVerse Source, Choice, Privacy, Release, and Correction Standard

## Purpose

This standard gives every ScrollVerse pathway a shared operating language. It is designed so a visitor can understand what they are seeing, what choices are available, what information remains private, what has actually been released, and how a record can be corrected. It applies to books, research, media, community exchange, creator services, direct-to-fan catalog routes, contribution planning, and support destinations.

## The five-part standard

| Standard element | Visitor-facing question | Portal requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Where did this record, statement, asset, or method come from? | Label the item as owner-authored, owner-stated, owner-held, public source reviewed, original ScrollVerse work, community first-person material, or third-party system transmission. |
| Choice | What can I do here without pressure or ambiguity? | State whether the visitor can read, listen, learn, plan locally, join a community route, explore an approved catalog, or prepare a future proposal. |
| Privacy | What should stay out of this page and who can see a future contribution? | Do not request credentials, payment data, account details, private agreements, sensitive personal data, or unapproved proprietary material in public/browser-local tools. |
| Release | What is available now, what is research, and what needs owner release? | Use direct operating-state labels and item-level release records before a product, payment, service, token, delivery, or external action is activated. |
| Correction | If a record changes, where is the path to update it? | Retain a source date or version when possible and identify the owner-approved correction/review path. |

## Financial-highway application

Financial and support routes must add two safeguards. First, they must identify the exact purpose of a future exchange—such as an item, service, support purpose, or delivery promise—before any activation. Second, they must remain fail-closed until the owner provides an approved source, destination, price/package if relevant, fulfillment/support approach, privacy boundary, and release instruction.

The Support Highways section may link to an owner-stated public destination while making clear that an external provider controls its own payment interface and that ScrollVerse does not process, confirm, or guarantee the exchange. A browser-local plan, a catalog title, a community idea, or an external-link click is never treated as a transfer, contract, vote, payment, or completed contribution.

## Systemwide route application

| Pathway type | Primary standard emphasis |
|---|---|
| Free books and learning | Source, choice, and correction: readers can learn freely and follow references. |
| Research and synchronization records | Source, choice, and correction: first-person accounts and public materials remain distinct. |
| Community exchange | Choice, privacy, and correction: participation is voluntary and governed by the community route’s own controls. |
| Creator services and co-creation | Choice, privacy, and release: a brief is not an agreement and no rights transfer is inferred. |
| Direct-to-fan catalog | Source, release, and correction: an item needs its own owner-approved record before purchase or delivery. |
| Support highways | Privacy, release, and choice: public destinations are optional links, not in-portal payment processing. |

## Durability and correction

The standard becomes durable through dated source records, regression tests, documented local validation, and managed checkpoints when the project history is available. It does not claim that an individual page is irreversible, that a source cannot change, or that a local record is published until the managed checkpoint succeeds.
