# Open Record — Continuum Manifesto

## Tonight’s purpose

This record preserves the core working principles expressed in tonight’s conversation as a creator-directed ScrollVerse continuity document. Its purpose is not to demand agreement or substitute interpretation for evidence. Its purpose is to keep the work visible: what is stated by the owner, what is locally documented, what is publicly available, what remains research, and what requires an actual owner-controlled release.

> **Continuum statement:** Build with agency and care. Share learning that can help people grow. Protect private material and unreleased work. Keep source, date, operating state, and correction path visible enough for visitors to inspect the work in their own way.

## Continuum principles

| Principle | Working commitment | Portal expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Open record** | Let the work, its source path, and its boundaries be seen. | Source-labeled research records, public-source indexes, and browser-local preparation tools. |
| **Creator agency** | The creator makes decisions about original work while respecting the rights, safety, and consent of others. | Evidence readiness, IP stewardship, rights-aware release packets, and documented permission context. |
| **Care with capacity** | Do not reproduce the harm that a new system is meant to move beyond. | Assistive governance, correction paths, accessible language, human review, and voluntary participation. |
| **Open learning** | Share methods and knowledge that can be shared; let more minds improve what is useful. | Free learning routes, public research sources, and the private MIT evidence archive’s documented boundary. |
| **Distinct records** | First-party perspective, local results, public sources, and external outcomes carry different kinds of meaning. | Direct operating-state labels: **available now**, **research**, and **requires owner release**. |

## What remains intentionally separate

The record does not convert a conversation, a source theme, a personal interpretation, or a local prototype into an external outcome. The following require their own exact evidence and owner-controlled release: account action, private Gmail/YouTube information, public media publication, provider activation, filing, contract, payment, wallet action, token action, transaction, identity claim, or institutional outcome.
