# Original Narrative and Media Readiness Research

> **Purpose.** This research record supports original ScrollVerse comic, animation, and educational-media preparation. It does not authorize use of another company’s characters, stories, visual identity, footage, scripts, or other protected expression.

| Production concern | Source-led pattern | ScrollVerse application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original expression | The U.S. Copyright Office describes original works as independently created by a human author with at least a minimal degree of creativity. [1] | Use original characters, settings, scripts, dialogue, story structures, art direction, and sound assets; record source and contributor context. |
| Preexisting material | U.S. copyright law defines a derivative work as a work based upon one or more preexisting works. [2] | Inspiration research does not create permission to reuse protected characters, names, designs, footage, storylines, or other expression. Keep rights questions visible before release. |
| Captions | W3C explains that captions are a text version of speech and non-speech audio information. [3] | Plan reviewed captions for narrative dialogue, relevant music/audio cues, and educational segments before a public video release. |
| Synchronized media | Section 508 describes captions as enabling fuller participation for people who are deaf or hard of hearing. [4] | Pair captions with audio description or other accessible alternatives where the work’s visual content is material to understanding. |
| Youth participation | ScrollVerse’s standing youth-safety rule requires adult-supported participation guidance for child-facing content. | Keep creator submissions, character ideas, feedback, and any public release pathway age-aware and adult-supported where children participate. |
