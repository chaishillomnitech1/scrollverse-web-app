# Phase 454 — Physical Artwork Rights, Artist Recognition & Compensation Boundary

**Purpose:** Define how the ScrollVerse Art Museum can honor the owner-held physical objects, visibly name documented artist information, and prepare an optional artist-recognition or compensation structure without assuming reproduction rights, payment instructions, artist affiliation, or an active token program.

## Core distinction

The Art Museum records the two works as **owner-held physical collection assets**. That record is distinct from copyright and public-reproduction rights. The U.S. Copyright Office lists reproduction, derivative-work preparation, distribution, and public display among the exclusive rights of a copyright owner for pictorial, graphic, and sculptural works.[1] The Thomas Kinkade Studios copyright page states that artwork shown on its website is protected by the Thomas Kinkade Estate’s copyright.[2]

This is general source context, not legal advice. Any public artwork image, derivative, token media, collector license, commercial use, or artist compensation agreement needs an exact rights and terms review for the selected object.

## Artist-recognition band — design only

The **Artist Recognition Band** is a proposed metadata and agreement layer for a future rights-cleared representation. It is not a coin, NFT, payment, royalty, grant, contract, or smart contract deployment.

| Field | Required meaning before activation |
| --- | --- |
| Accession ID and object identity | The exact physical artwork record and evidence packet the band refers to |
| Confirmed creator or rights-holder | A documented name and authority record, not a guess from a plaque or image |
| Recognition statement | The exact approved credit wording and display context |
| Representation right | Specific written permission for any public image, derivative, token media, catalog entry, or collector license |
| Compensation model | Fixed honorarium, royalty, donation, revenue share, or no-payment acknowledgement—named precisely, with scope and duration |
| Recipient and payment rail | Verified recipient and lawful, approved payment method; no wallet, account, or personal information stored in the Museum page |
| Review and correction | Record of approval date, correction path, termination or revision terms, and public-display state |

## Compensation and digital-asset boundary

An artist-support or compensation concept should never be inferred from physical possession, visitor traffic, a source card, a museum image, an interest in the artwork, or a future token idea. It may be considered only after the recipient, authority, use scope, payment terms, rights, and written approval are all identified. Young artists require an additional adult-supported safeguarding, eligibility, privacy, rights, and non-financial-participation design before any program is considered.

No compensation, royalty, payout, grant, incentive, token, coin, NFT, listing, sale, collection contract, payment method, or wallet is activated by this document.

## Source and correction path

For *Bridge of Faith*, retain the official Thomas Kinkade copyright page as a source context and use a rights-holder contact or license process only if a proposed representation calls for it. For the Cosmic Knowledge Artwork, first obtain a clearer full signature/copyright line or other documentary record before attributing or contacting a potential creator or rights holder. All corrections should be appended to the relevant accession record with date, source, and revised operating state.
