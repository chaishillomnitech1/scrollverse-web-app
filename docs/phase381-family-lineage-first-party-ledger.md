# Phase 381 — Family Legacy and Lineage Ledger

## Record identity

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Record type | Owner-authored family and lineage statement |
| Current operating state | **Research** |
| Public presentation | First-party account, with source lane shown beside each statement |
| Private material | Family-held documents, genealogy-account data, DNA reports, certificates, correspondence, and unshared research remain protected until an owner-approved excerpt is prepared |
| Correction path | Add a dated correction note that identifies whether the change is to the owner statement, a family document summary, a genealogy finding, or an external archival reference |

## Owner-stated lineage record

The following is preserved as the owner’s first-person family and lineage account. It is not an independent historical, genealogical, or genetic finding by the portal.

> The owner states that his family history includes connections he understands through King Solomon, Mansa Musa, War Sechum, Cuffee, Cherokee, Shinnecock, Kofi Slocum, Ghana, Mali, Ethiopia, Nigeria, and a wider family story that joins African, Indigenous, and diasporic memory. The owner states that Kofi Slocum married a Cuffee ancestor and that the family line is fused across both sides.

The record honors the importance of that account without merging the owner’s statement with a claim that an institution, archive, genealogical service, nation, community, or historical figure has independently confirmed it. It will grow source by source as owner-approved documentation is added.

## Source lanes

| Lane | What belongs here | Public state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner-stated history | The owner’s memories, family narratives, lineage understanding, and named connections | **Available now** when presented as an owner statement |
| Family-held records | Certificates, letters, photographs, family trees, oral-history recordings, and documents held by relatives | **Requires owner release** until an accessible approved excerpt is cleared for the portal |
| Genealogy research | Research notes, database results, genetic genealogy summaries, and comparison work | **Research** until a dated source summary and provenance are added |
| Third-party archives | Libraries, government records, newspapers, university archives, museum collections, and historical registers | **Research** until a direct citation, collection identifier, date, and relation path are documented |
| Community and heritage records | Community context, local history, tribal or nation-specific records, and cultural materials | **Research**; neither identity nor affiliation is inferred from a general historical source |

## Public portal wording

The Journey, Book of the Journey, and family legacy routes may use the following source-labeled statement:

> **Family Legacy · Owner-stated history.** The owner shares a family lineage account spanning African, Indigenous, and diasporic memory, including named connections to Ghana, Mali, Ethiopia, Nigeria, Cherokee, Shinnecock, and Cuffee family history. The family record is kept as a living source ledger: the owner’s statement is visible now; private documents and external archival confirmations are added only through their own dated, owner-approved source lanes.

## Intake and release checklist

| Before a new lineage item becomes public | Required record |
| --- | --- |
| Family document excerpt | Owner approval, source holder, creation date if known, privacy review, and an accessible transcription or description |
| Genealogy result | Provider or researcher name, retrieval date, method description, limitations, and an owner-approved public summary |
| Historical archive citation | Institution, collection or catalog identifier, stable URL when available, date, relation path, and a note distinguishing source content from interpretation |
| Community affiliation reference | Direct owner-approved source and care not to infer membership, enrollment, identity, or community recognition from a general history source |

## Release boundary

This ledger does not publish private DNA, legal identity documents, account credentials, unreviewed family information, or a relative’s sensitive details. It also does not place a public verification stamp on an item merely because it is meaningful to the owner. The portal preserves the owner’s statement directly and then lets each supporting source stand visibly in its own lane.
