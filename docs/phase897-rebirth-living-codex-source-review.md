## Compatible planning framework

The public route may present three creator-led planning lanes: **Tree**, **Water**, and **Knowledge**. Each lane connects a creator-authored theme to an external research reference and a human-governed planning prompt. The route may offer browser-local export only, plus first-party pathways to the Seven-Episode Series, Episode Brief Builder, and Special Repository.

> **Human-governance principle:** AI can assist research and synthesis; qualified people, locally informed partners, appropriate scientific expertise, and accountable governance retain consequential decisions.

The research supports a careful approach. FAO describes assisted natural regeneration as a context-dependent method that depends on ecological and social conditions, including existing regenerants, disturbance prevention, community support, land tenure, and local capacity. [1] UNEP frames restoration across multiple ecosystem types and emphasizes themes such as reducing pollution, managing water demand, restoring vegetation, and community participation. [2] IUCN describes restoration as a varied set of approaches across terrestrial, aquatic, and marine systems, and emphasizes scientific support and monitoring. [3]
