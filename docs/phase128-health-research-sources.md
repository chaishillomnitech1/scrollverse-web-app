# Phase 128 — Health Research Desk Source Register

## Purpose

This register defines the initial external sources for a ScrollVerse Health Research Desk. The product is an educational navigation layer: it must show a source URL and a visible date or current-status note for every factual item. It does not diagnose, prescribe, rank clinicians, establish a clinician-patient relationship, or provide emergency response.

| Source | Intended use | Boundary | URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| MedlinePlus Health Topics | Plain-language health-information and appointment-question orientation. | General information only; not personal medical advice. | https://medlineplus.gov/healthtopics.html |
| MedlinePlus Directories | Starting point for public directories of health professionals, services, and facilities. | ScrollVerse does not verify, rank, credential, endorse, or recommend a listed provider. | https://medlineplus.gov/directories/ |
| NIH News in Health | Dated public research and health-information stories. | Research news is not individual treatment guidance. | https://newsinhealth.nih.gov/ |
| NIH Health Information | Science-based public health information and discussion-preparation material. | Content does not replace a clinician’s assessment. | https://www.nih.gov/health-information |
| CDC Newsroom | Public-health announcements and dated public updates. | News must retain its source date and must not be rephrased as a diagnosis or personalized risk statement. | https://www.cdc.gov/media/index.html |
| CDC Health, United States Data Finder | Official trend tables and charts for U.S. health statistics. | Statistics require methodology and date context; they do not determine an individual’s condition. | https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/hus/data-finder.htm |
| CDC Emergency Preparedness | Public emergency-preparedness material. | For a life-threatening emergency in the U.S., users should call 911 instead of relying on the portal. | https://www.cdc.gov/Emergency/index.html |
| 988 Lifeline | U.S. crisis-support information. | Available support pathway, not a ScrollVerse-operated emergency service. | https://988lifeline.org/ |

## Publication Standard

Every Health Research Desk item must display its **publisher**, **source URL**, **publication or access date**, **content type** (news, statistic, directory, or education), and a succinct **limitations label**. Clinician discovery stays a directory handoff with geography and specialty filters; it never converts source listings into a ScrollVerse doctor ranking.
