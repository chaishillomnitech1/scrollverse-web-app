# ScrollVerse × OVO-Inspired Partnership Concept

## Status and Purpose

This is an **independent, non-binding creative proposal** prepared from submitted concept material. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or produced for October’s Very Own, OVO, Drake, or any related party. It does not grant rights to use third-party logos, trade dress, artist likenesses, imagery, product names, or proprietary campaign material.

The purpose is to prepare a respectful first conversation with an authorized partnership contact. It presents three original ScrollVerse styling directions and an evidence-led operating path. It is not a live product catalog, product endorsement, price list, commercial offer, or purchase flow.

## Proposal Conversation Points

The proposed conversation is about whether an authorized partner would be open to evaluating a creator-led capsule, editorial direction, or community-impact collaboration. ScrollVerse can offer original design exploration, creator provenance records, a source-to-review product readiness process, and evidence-led campaign operations. No part of the proposal assumes a partnership exists.

The submitted giveback structure—creator participation, community support, and a resale-oriented component—should be framed as a discussion design, not a payment promise. Before any funds, products, token incentives, or royalty mechanism can activate, the project requires documented recipient eligibility, rights and governance terms, accounting treatment, approved provider relationships, and separate technical/security review.
