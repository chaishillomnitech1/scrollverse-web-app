## Remaining Owner-Controlled Gates

The portal deliberately leaves several actions outside the completed route catalog. These gates are not internal defects: they require an authenticated owner session, verified source material, a cleared administrator/provenance record, a fresh securely stored credential where applicable, provider terms, qualified review, or an explicit transaction-time confirmation.

> Documentation, source records, browser-local planning, and route discovery are live first-party capabilities. External publication, authentication, deployment, financial operation, and irreversible on-chain actions are separate readiness states.
