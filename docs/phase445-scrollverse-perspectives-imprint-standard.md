# Phase 445 — ScrollVerse Perspectives Imprint Standard

**Imprint:** **ScrollVerse Perspectives**  
**Creator-directed identity:** Chais “Sabir Kajir” Hill the Great / ScrollVerse  
**Purpose:** Create original books, readers, lessons, and study guides that help visitors encounter source material, understand the limits of a record, and form their own conclusions.

## Imprint promise

ScrollVerse Perspectives is an original-writing imprint, not a copying machine. It turns source trails into readable learning experiences by clearly separating the source itself, the author’s original analysis, historical context, questions for the reader, and revision history.

> **The source stays visible. The perspective is named. The visitor remains free to decide.**

## Original production standard

| Component | Required original contribution | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source card | A concise, linked description of the record, edition, translation, access state, and limitation | Do not reproduce restricted text, scans, images, or translations in full without a documented right to do so. |
| Perspective essay | Original authorial analysis, thematic connection, historical framing, or reflective question | Label interpretation as interpretation; do not turn a source into a universal conclusion. |
| Comparison panel | Original comparison of named editions, ideas, records, or historical contexts | Show the source and scope of comparison; do not erase differences among traditions or communities. |
| Learning lesson | Original study prompts, definitions, visual structure, activities, or discussion questions | Do not present historical or financial material as personal professional advice. |
| Revision log | Date, source correction, changed claim, and current operating state | Preserve the correction path instead of silently replacing the earlier record. |
| Accessibility pack | Semantic reading structure, alt text, plain-language source summary, and optional audio or visual treatment plan | Audio, video, illustrations, and translations require their own rights and contributor records. |

## Content families

| Family | Reader experience | Source treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open Scroll Readers** | Short, free web-first readers that introduce a source trail and question | Link-first; public-domain or open material only for full-text inclusion |
| **Perspective Books** | Original long-form books that connect source records, lived experience, analysis, and reflection | Each chapter has named sources, interpretation labels, and a correction section |
| **Study Guides** | Guided reading routes, comparison prompts, glossary, and accessible learning modules | Cite each source and distinguish a translation from an original-language witness |
| **Great Minds & Institutions** | Source-led profiles of thinkers, inventors, movements, organizations, and historical records | Separate archival evidence, scholarly context, and ScrollVerse commentary |
| **Money, Systems & History** | Financial-literacy and institutional-history readers | Historical education only; no trading, payment, token, investment, or credit direction |
| **Journey Volumes** | Owner-authored first-person records with linked evidence and creative works | Clearly identify owner statements and source materials; do not state unverified external outcomes as fact |

## Reading and revenue pathways

| Pathway | Visitor choice | Required before use | Current state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free web reader** | Read original ScrollVerse material and follow external sources | Final original manuscript, source notes, accessibility review, public destination, and owner release | Requires owner release |
| **Free study guide** | Download or use an original learning route | Same as web reader plus file format and access review | Requires owner release |
| **Paid digital edition** | Choose a defined digital book or bundle | Final manuscript, rights record, price, terms, delivery method, support route, payment destination, and owner release | Requires owner release |
| **Print or collector edition** | Choose a specific physical or collector-format work | Production proof, source/rights record, fulfillment, edition terms, price, support, destination, and owner release | Requires owner release |
| **Institutional or classroom license** | Request a scoped learning or library use | Exact license, audience, territory, duration, accessibility, price, invoice/payment terms, and owner release | Requires owner release |

No pathway claims an active listing, published book, purchase destination, price, order fulfillment, payment method, customer, revenue, subscription, or commercial result until the item’s own release packet is complete.

## Per-book release packet

Every book, guide, or lesson should carry a packet with the following records:

| Record | Required decision |
| --- | --- |
| Scope and manuscript | Final title, author/creator credit, chapter list, original text, and version |
| Source and rights | Links, editions/translations, quotation and image rights, contributor releases, and license treatment |
| Accessibility | Reading structure, alt text, captions or transcripts where needed, and available formats |
| Editorial and correction | Fact/source check, interpretation label, limitations, and correction contact/path |
| Public release | Free or paid state, public URL, reader terms, support contact, and owner release |
| Commercial terms | Exact format, price/currency, delivery, refund/return position where relevant, and payment destination |

## Authorship and public voice

The author’s perspective can be direct, original, and unmistakable. It becomes stronger when it names the source, labels the interpretation, shows the revision path, and does not claim permission to reproduce someone else’s full work or to guarantee a reader’s financial, religious, historical, or personal conclusion.
