# ScrollVerse Education System Audit and Learning Design Record

> **Scope.** This record inventories first-party learning surfaces that are present in the active route map and sets a practical design direction for a unified learning navigator. Route presence does not itself establish accreditation, a credential, professional instruction, course availability, a paid enrollment, a learner record, or an outcome.

## Design Principles for the Unified Navigator

W3C describes accessibility guidance that applies to dynamic content, multimedia, mobile, and AI interfaces, and identifies WCAG, ARIA, captions/timed text, authoring tools, and adaptive presentation as relevant categories. [1] CAST’s Universal Design for Learning guidelines organize design around multiple means of engagement, representation, action/expression, and learner agency. [2] UNESCO defines OER as materials in the public domain or released under an open license that grants stated reuse permissions; a resource should not be called open merely because it is free to view. [3]

The first-party navigator will therefore provide clear course state, local study notes, completion checks, learning-goal prompts, source/reuse labels, visual and text pathways, keyboard-compatible controls, and learner support language. Youth-facing or participatory material will direct learners toward adult-supported participation where relevant. The navigator will not issue credentials, collect learner data, create an official transcript, or state that a route qualifies a learner for employment, academic credit, professional practice, or financial results.
