# Phase 102 — Draft Incentive Governance Framework

> **Draft for business, legal, tax, and compliance review before adoption.** This framework does not authorize a payment, grant, token issuance, revenue share, partnership, employment relationship, or investment offer.

## Purpose

The program is a reviewable framework for future partner, employee, and creator recognition. It is designed to make contribution criteria explicit, preserve a traceable decision record, and prevent automatic compensation or token-linked rewards from being represented as guaranteed.

| Control | Required before any future award |
|---|---|
| Eligibility | Written role, contribution scope, and conflict disclosure |
| Compensation | Signed terms, currency and tax classification, payment authority, and cap |
| Token or digital asset | Separate legal and compliance review, recipient agreement, wallet verification, and risk disclosure |
| Revenue share | Executed commercial agreement, accounting definition, payment cadence, and audit rights |
| Partner benefit | Counterparty approval, brand-use authorization, and delivery obligations |
| Recordkeeping | Reviewer decision, dated rationale, recipient acknowledgement, and amendment history |

## Operating Rules

Awards are never triggered solely by traffic, public statements, uploaded content, token balances, or association with a brand. The review owner must document the contribution, the approved agreement, the measurement period, and the applicable cap before an award can move from proposal to payable status. Any public description must avoid claims of guaranteed appreciation, yield, profit, or perpetual income.

## Review Cadence

The evidence-expiry digest and review console can surface documents that need attention. They do not calculate compensation, select recipients, create payment obligations, or release assets. Any future compensation module should require a distinct approval workflow and a separate security review.
