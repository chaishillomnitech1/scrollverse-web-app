## Standard

The Continuous Improvement Standard adds six evidence-backed review lenses to the portal: Route Integrity, Accessible Experience, Evidence Quality, Security Boundaries, Release-State Accuracy, and Recovery & Correction. Each lens states a review question, evidence to inspect, and a correction path so that a quality improvement can be explained without converting it into an unsupported claim of automatic change.

> Recovery-ready means detect, review, correct, verify, and preserve the record. It does not mean silent changes to a provider, account, payment, contract, or external system.
