# Phase 650 — Authenticity Frequency Brand Brief

## Working Company Identity

**Origin Ark Studios** is the proposed working company identity within the ScrollVerse universe. The name pairs a clear origin signal with an ark: a vessel for preservation, memory, guidance, and forward movement. It is designed as a compact two-word spoken name that can carry a studio, creator platform, media label, and archive system without replacing the existing ScrollVerse, SCROLL VERSE LLC, or XLVIII BLOCKS LLC identity records.

This is an original browser-local **working identity**, not a claim of registration, exclusive rights, external filing, or commercial availability.

## Core Mark

The core mark is **The Open Ark Seal**: a clean, upward-facing arch formed by two luminous strokes. The negative space makes a quiet path or open page; the top carries a small four-point signal star. It works at favicon scale, as a stamp, as a production slate, and as a large portal emblem.

| Element | Meaning | Visual rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open arch | A preserved place that welcomes a future path | Two simple strokes; no intricate engraving |
| Signal star | Presence, orientation, and original light | One compact four-point form above the arch |
| Orbit line | Connection among ScrollVerse records | A single broken orbit, never a dense planetary system |
| Dark field | Focus and restraint | Midnight navy background with high contrast |

## Mascot

**Auri, the Signal Warden** is the original mascot concept. Auri is a compact guardian sprite formed from a simple mask-like face, a warm gold core, cyan signal fins, and a small orbit halo. The silhouette remains readable as a single icon and avoids a realistic human likeness or a borrowed character identity.

> **Authenticity is the highest frequency.** The mascot does not perform authority; it signals care, memory, curiosity, and a clear next step.

## Palette

The system uses ScrollVerse midnight `#050B1A`, signal cyan `#00D4FF`, archive gold `#FFB800`, growth green `#00FF88`, and signal violet `#B98CFF`. Gold is reserved for the core star and warmth; cyan defines navigation and evidence flow.
