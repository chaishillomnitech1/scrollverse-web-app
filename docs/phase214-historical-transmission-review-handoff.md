# Historical Transmission Intake — Protected Review Handoff

> **Purpose.** The public Historical Transmission Patterns route contains a high-level creative and workflow map only. This handoff identifies what remains private and who must review a material before it could receive more specific public wording.

| Material or question | Public route state | Required owner decision | Separate qualified review where needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raw transmission, private source export, chat, or recovery artifact | Not published or attached. | Confirm whether a specific excerpt is intended for public release and provide a source/version context. | Privacy, contractual/confidentiality, or other review appropriate to the material. |
| Creator credit, co-creation, or story use | Only source-labeled creative patterns. | Identify contributor context, approved credit wording, and permitted derivative/public use. | Rights/licensing review when third-party or shared material is involved. |
| Provider, deployment, security, or account claim | Excluded from the public pattern map. | Identify exact independently verifiable source and intended public wording. | Security/provider review before representing technical state or account control. |
| Financial, valuation, payment, token, or transaction claim | Excluded from the public pattern map. | Do not add as a public fact without exact supporting evidence and an approved purpose. | Appropriate financial, legal, tax, platform, or compliance review. |
| Legal, medical, scientific, historical, or extraordinary assertion | Excluded unless separately sourced and precisely labeled. | Identify the exact source, date, scope, and desired wording. | Relevant qualified reviewer and independently verifiable sources. |

## Validation Result

The public route remains a source-labeled creative/workflow pattern map. It exposes no raw transmission files, private credentials, wallet material, addresses, provider controls, financial assertions, legal conclusion, medical guidance, verified deployment, or transaction flow. It links to Creator Studio, Creator Services, Creator Exchange, Recovery Materials, and Evidence Commons as planning or learning handoffs, not as proof that a historical claim is active.

## Owner Completeness Step

For any future release request, prepare a narrow release record containing: the exact source artifact/version; a proposed excerpt or derivative summary; source/rights/contributor context; requested public audience; privacy and sensitive-data check; exact limitation label; review need; and an explicit owner decision. Do not treat this handoff as permission to publish raw records.
