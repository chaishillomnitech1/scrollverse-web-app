## Assistive Governance Translation

The local Assistive Governance Desk should make five facts visible for every sensitive task: **who is accountable**, **what exact action is proposed**, **what evidence supports it**, **what authorization is needed**, and **what outcome was actually recorded**. An AI may prepare, explain, validate local data consistency, and surface a checklist. It is not the legal person, bank, fiduciary, provider, court, or independent external witness for the task.
