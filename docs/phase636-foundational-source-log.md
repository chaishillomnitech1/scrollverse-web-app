# Phase 636 — Foundational Source Log

The following public sources were verified for the initial browser-local ScrollVerse Research Atlas. They are discovery anchors, not exhaustive coverage or endorsements of any creator claim.

| District | Source | Institution | Verified finding | Scope limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Physics | [About CERN](https://home.cern/about/) | CERN | CERN describes using particle accelerators and detectors to investigate fundamental particles and their interactions. | This is an anchor for particle physics, not all branches of physics. |
| Mathematics | [Millennium Prize Problems](https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/) | Clay Mathematics Institute | The page describes seven major mathematical problems selected to emphasize open research questions. | This is a problem-focused entry point, not a general mathematics survey. |
| Science | [About NASA Science](https://science.nasa.gov/about-us/) | NASA Science Mission Directorate | NASA describes interdisciplinary work across Earth, planetary, astrophysical, heliophysics, and related scientific research. | This focuses on NASA-supported sciences rather than all scientific disciplines. |
| Philosophy | [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/) | Stanford University | SEP describes a scholarly, regularly maintained reference work across philosophy and related disciplines. | Entries are editorial works that change over time and should be read in their revision context. |
| Design methods | [The Double Diamond](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/the-double-diamond/) | Design Council | The framework presents discover, define, develop, and deliver as an adaptable model for design and innovation work. | It is a framework rather than an instruction manual and must be adapted to a specific project. |
| Creator practice | [Practice as Research](https://www.tate.org.uk/research/research-centres/tate-research-centre-learning/practice-as-research) | Tate | Tate frames rigorous reflective practice, knowledge sharing, and practitioner-research as research-led creative activity. | The page describes museum-learning practice and does not cover every independent studio or commercial context. |

## Correction path

Visitors can use the Research Atlas correction record to identify a source, state the concern, and point to a more relevant public source. A correction record changes only the browser-local research entry until a future reviewed update is published.
