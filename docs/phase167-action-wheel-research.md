# Phase 167 — Respectful Main Page Action Wheel

## Source and presentation model

The Action Wheel connects six first-party pathways: 99 Names learning, Gulf cultural learning, creator collection records, source-first historical research, creator-workspace development, and evidence review. The wheel is a navigation and education surface; it does not require a visitor to accept a claim, purchase anything, or disclose personal information.

| Wheel path | Sources | Presentation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 99 Names learning | [Islamic Relief][1]; [Hadith Answers][2] | Devotional, voluntary, non-commercial study. It is not gamified and does not claim religious authority. |
| Gulf cultural learning | [Dubai Culture][3]; [SMCCU][4]; [Saudi Ministry of Culture][5]; [Ithra][6] | Public learning links only. No government, ministry, cultural-institution, or tourism affiliation is implied. |
| Historic research | [Britannica historiography][7]; [NEH][8]; [UCLA primary/secondary source guide][9] | Visitors compare evidence and interpretations; no single historical interpretation is mandated. |
| Nipsey Blue and Cereal Killer records | Owner-supplied collection names and first-party creator routes | Presented as creator-submitted records. No estate, trademark, licensing, production, listing, token, checkout, or commercial status is asserted. |
