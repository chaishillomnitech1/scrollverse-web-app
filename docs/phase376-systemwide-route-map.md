# Phase 376 — Systemwide Source and Choice Standard Route Map

## Mapping rule

This map applies the Source, Choice, Privacy, Release, and Correction Standard to existing ScrollVerse route families using their documented current state. It does not introduce wallets, cryptographic credentials, consensus thresholds, membership tiers, smart contracts, automated governance, autonomous release actions, encrypted peer channels, or immutable publication claims that are not present in the local portal record.

## Connected pathways

| Route family | Source | Choice | Privacy | Release | Correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Book of the Journey | Chapter source labels distinguish owner-authored, owner-stated, public, protected, and original-work records | Read chapters and follow connected sources freely | Protected source files and private archive material remain outside the reader | The reader is available locally; private excerpts need owner approval | Source shelf and chapter record can be updated with dated corrections |
| Sovereign Methodology and Open Record Continuum | Owner-authored methodology and public learning sources are labeled separately | Read, use the browser-local reflection board, or follow connected routes | The reflection board is browser-local; no credentials or sensitive material should be entered | Learning tools are available locally; no external action is triggered | The owner can amend methodology records and source paths |

| Sovereign Contribution and Co-Creation Exchange | Contribution lanes and planning resources identify source and purpose | Learn, create a browser-local brief, or visit connected community/governance routes | Browser-local briefs should not contain credentials, sensitive data, or unapproved proprietary material | No submission, vote, trade, payment, contract, token, or delivery is activated | Future intake processes must define review, response, correction, and withdrawal paths |

| Synchronization Research and Chais Sabir Equation | First-person records, public metadata, historical methods, and public references are kept distinct | Explore sources, calculation panels, and linked research routes | Browser-local calculation and readiness notes remain local; private source material is not assumed public | Research pages are available locally; external claims or releases retain their own evidence path | Findings can be updated with source dates and correction records |

## Cross-route standard language

Every connected route should display or link to the same operating vocabulary: **available now**, **research**, and **requires owner release**. When an offer, contributor pathway, support destination, or source changes, the correction path should identify the record owner and the revised operating state rather than imply an automatic or irreversible outcome.
