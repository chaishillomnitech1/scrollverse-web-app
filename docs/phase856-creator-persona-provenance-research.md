# Phase 856 — Creator Persona + Provenance Research

## Research Synthesis

The Chais Sabir Allah Mirror can be a useful first-party persona when it is designed as a **creator-controlled guide with explicit scope**. NIST’s AI Risk Management Framework describes a voluntary approach to incorporating trustworthiness into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI systems; its core functions are Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage.[1] C2PA describes provenance as labeled assertions made by actors about how an asset was originated or transformed, and supports separate manifest repositories when embedding is not suitable.[2] The W3C DID specification describes identifiers that a controller can manage, including verification methods and interaction services, while allowing identity and privacy scope to stay distinct.[3]

| Research pattern | First-party implementation | Not implied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Named creator controller and visible scope | The Mirror profile names its creator-authored role, guidance areas, source trails, and available ScrollVerse paths | Sentience, legal personhood, external account access, or real-world delegated authority |
| Source trail and correction path | Persona claims point to a ScrollVerse creator record, Digital DNA context, or source registry | That every record is independently verified or irreversible |
| Separate identity and action planes | The profile distinguishes identity, narrative, evidence, guidance, and future external-action pathways | Automatic wallet, payment, contract, domain, hardware, provider, or physical-world control |
| Visitor-led collaboration | Visitors can begin in the existing AI Twin conversation, Companion Hub, or AI Legion directory | Unsupervised action on behalf of a visitor or the creator |
| Future-ready provenance | A roadmap can explain C2PA/DID-inspired preservation without claiming current credential or ledger deployment | A minted credential, registered DID, or signed provenance manifest without the underlying implementation and verification record |
