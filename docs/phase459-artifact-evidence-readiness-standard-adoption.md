## Shared operating requirements

All new asset records should retain a stable ID, a source or owner context, a rights state, a public-display state, a visitor-choice path, and a correction path. Each item moves only through its own release conditions. A source card, owner-held record, research route, draft metadata file, or local preparation control does not become a public license, payment option, product listing, token, or external delivery action by implication.
