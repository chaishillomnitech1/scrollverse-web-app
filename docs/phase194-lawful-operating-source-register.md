# Phase 194 — Lawful and Auditable Operating Navigator

> **Working analysis, not formal legal advice.** The navigator is a first-party operating and disclosure tool. A qualified attorney should review any specific contract, trademark, copyright, consumer-facing claim, regulatory filing, business structure, or transaction before reliance.

The navigator converts official public guidance into a transparent project-practice map. It supports original, lawful systems design; it does not advise on a particular legal matter or turn creative planning into concealment, deceptive presentation, evasion, or a workaround for an obligation.

| Principle | Public context | ScrollVerse route application | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Truthful disclosure | FTC business guidance describes truthful, non-misleading, appropriately supported advertising claims. | Evidence Standards and Evidence Commons use source, date, evidence state, and limitation labels. | This is not a legal determination or a certification of compliance. |
| Rights record | U.S. Copyright Office materials distinguish original expression from facts, ideas, systems, names, and titles. | Artifact Evidence Blueprint and Creator Operations preserve source, rights context, and uncertainty before a release is described as cleared. | A record is not a license, registration, ownership judgment, or clearance. |
| Permission | CFPB personal-data guidance provides public context for authorized access and data-sharing scope. | Release Readiness requires action scope, owner authority, dependency, and evidence before any owner-controlled step. | A checklist does not create consent, a provider relationship, or a signature. |
| Privacy | NIST Privacy Framework offers a voluntary approach to managing privacy risk. | Privacy Summary and protected routes separate public learning, browser-local tools, and private records. | This does not certify compliance or assure privacy outcomes. |
| Correction | First-party versioning and evidence practices preserve what changed and why. | Evidence Commons and source registers preserve updates and limits. | A project record is not an audit, legal remedy, or dispute decision. |
| Clear exchange | Consumer-information resources support informed choice and understandable public information. | Financial Systems Capability Map explains external-action boundaries and transparent handoffs. | ScrollVerse does not act as the transaction authority or financial provider. |
