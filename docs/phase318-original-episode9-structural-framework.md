# Phase 318 — Original Episode 9 Structural Framework

**Working production:** *The Black Beacon Chronicles*  
**Working episode:** *Episode 9 — The Thread Between Lanterns*  
**Operating state:** **research**

## Story Premise

When a citywide archive begins linking stories without their original context, Imani, Juno, and Sol must discover whether the new connections are mistakes or invitations. The Beacon Archive answers only when each story carries a source, a human voice, and a path for correction. The episode treats care, evidence, and imagination as parts of the same creative craft.

## Six-Sequence Framework

| Sequence | Original visual/action | Voice and sound direction | Accessibility and evidence gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Lantern Weather | A rain of small archive lights crosses the neighborhood skyline, forming unfamiliar paths. | Warm Storyteller, with spacious natural pauses; soft rain and room textures only from approved sources. | Visual description and sound-source log begin with the first storyboard panel. |
| 2. The Wrong Connection | Juno notices two stories linked by a similar phrase but belonging to different creators. | Clear Guide, direct and caring. | Source trail visible on screen; no creative ownership is inferred from similarity. |
| 3. Sol’s Listening Window | Sol turns down the signal field so individual voices can be heard without crowding each other. | Cool Host, gentle humor and grounded technical clarity. | Comfortable listening level review, captions, transcript, and mute/stop route. |
| 4. The Quiet Correction | Imani edits the archive link and leaves the original record visible with a correction note. | Original archive-guide voice, non-authoritative and supportive. | Keyboard-accessible correction interaction; reduced-motion version of the animation. |
| 5. Lantern Exchange | Contributors offer new original story threads, choosing what to share and how to describe it. | Playful Spark, warm and never caricatured. | Contributor field, accessibility description, participation guidance, and moderation path. |
| 6. The Thread Home | The restored map becomes a constellation that points to the next original archive door. | Warm Storyteller transitions to a Cool Host invitation. | Final packet: script/storyboard, art/sound sources, voice record, captions/transcript, destination, and owner release. |

## Production Packet Before Release

The final Episode 9 packet must include a final original title/version, script and storyboard, character/world sheet, art/sound sources, contributor and rights record, voice-consent record where a specific person is involved, caption/transcript/audio description, youth suitability review, intended destination, owner-release decision, and a post-release correction path. Until those records exist, the episode remains **research**.

## Originality Boundary

This framework uses original working characters, title, setting, and scenes. It does not incorporate third-party character names, logos, protected story elements, or copied dialogue from the supplied reference material.
