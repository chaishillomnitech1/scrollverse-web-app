# Phase 230 — Ecosystem Outcomes Scorecard Research Register

> **Non-authority boundary.** This Scorecard is a first-party comparison method for documented design patterns and evidence states. It does not select leaders, determine legitimacy, judge a person’s worth, predict societal outcomes, or declare a universal winner.

| Criterion family | Source-led rationale | Safe Scorecard use | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-dimensional well-being | OECD frames social progress beyond economic output by considering material conditions, quality of life, inequalities, and resources for future well-being. [1] | Let visitors consider access, learning, social connection, resilience, and environmental context alongside any economic design feature. | Do not turn a framework into a claim that a specific ecosystem improves well-being without independent outcome evidence. |
| People, planet, prosperity, peace, partnership | The UN 2030 Agenda presents integrated economic, social, and environmental goals, with inclusion and collaboration as cross-cutting themes. [2] | Use public-interest lenses for inclusion, sustainability, participation, and shared benefit. | These are global aspirations and targets, not a certification or score automatically applicable to a private platform. |
| Rights and remedy | OHCHR describes the expectation that businesses avoid infringing rights and address adverse impacts; remedy mechanisms matter where harms occur. [3] | Include rights context, grievance/correction path, and evidence of remediation or source gap. | A first-party record cannot investigate, adjudicate, or remedy alleged harm in place of competent legal, regulatory, or community processes. |
| Accessible participation | WCAG 2 defines testable accessibility success criteria organized around perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust content. [4] | Show an accessibility design/state label, such as documented target, tested feature, source gap, or qualified assessment needed. | A self-assessment or accessibility feature list is not a legal accessibility certification. |
| Evidence and interpretation | The Standards Studio research record applies scope, source, conflict disclosure, limitation, correction, and version fields to every first-party note. [5] | Distinguish documented design feature, independently measured outcome, owner-supplied context, source gap, and visitor interpretation. | No aggregate “best ecosystem” score is produced where inputs lack comparable independent evidence. |

## Proposed Scorecard Method

The Scorecard will use **evidence bands rather than winner rankings**. Each criterion receives a visible state: **documented feature**, **independent outcome evidence**, **owner-supplied context**, **source gap**, or **qualified review needed**. A visitor may set a personal priority weight for public-interest criteria locally in the browser; the page will show that the result reflects those chosen priorities and source coverage, not an objective conclusion about who should govern or which ecosystem is superior.

The public criteria will include access and accessibility, evidence practice, rights and remedy pathway, privacy/data care, accountable governance, correction and transparency, learning access, creator/community control, resilience/security posture, environmental context, and benefit distribution. Each comparison record must disclose source/date, scope, limitation, and missing evidence before a visitor interprets it.

## Route Verification

The `/ecosystem-outcomes-scorecard` route was browser-verified on August 14, 2026. The rendered page exposed scenario and comparison-profile fields, ten public-interest criterion rows, local priority selectors, separate evidence-state selectors for each comparison profile, coverage summaries, a local-copy control, and explicit non-winner/non-impact boundaries. Changing the first profile’s access-and-accessibility evidence state to **Documented public framework** immediately changed its local coverage summary from `0/10` to `1/10`, confirming that the planner control is functional and browser-local. The route does not upload the scenario, profile labels, or evidence state.
