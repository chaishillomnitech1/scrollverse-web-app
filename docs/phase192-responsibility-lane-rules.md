# Phase 192 — Responsibility-Lane Rules

ScrollVerse connects first-party learning and preparation routes through a common evidence model while keeping responsibilities distinct. The approach is derived from the Phase 191 source register and the existing Institutional Systems Atlas. It is not a claim to operate the external systems used as research references.

| Lane | May do | May not do without a separate reviewed, authorized action |
|---|---|---|
| Education | Present sources, dates, terms, limitations, and free learning routes. | Recommend an investment, accept funds, value assets, or promise an outcome. |
| Private records | Hold authenticated project/creator workflow context where the product already supports it. | Expose private material, credentials, sensitive locations, or owner identity publicly. |
| Authorization | Show a checklist and record an owner-controlled decision state. | Treat a draft, checkbox, or route visit as provider consent, wallet consent, contract authority, or an executed action. |
| External providers | Link to official public destinations and show preparation requirements. | Create an account, submit assets, sign terms, send data, or activate a provider on the visitor’s behalf. |
| Wallet/on-chain evidence | Explain safety, show verified public evidence, and preserve non-broadcast readiness. | Request a secret, connect a wallet, request a signature, mint, transfer, deploy, or execute a transaction. |
| Security/identity | Explain scoped access, safe defaults, and privacy controls. | Claim assurance, certification, proofing, KYC, compliance, or breach protection without separate evidence. |

The public map is therefore connected through visible navigation and shared record vocabulary, while each high-impact action remains a discrete, evidence-gated handoff.
