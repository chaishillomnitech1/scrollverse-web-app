# Phase 321 — Transparent Teaching & Creator Protection Standard

**Operating state:** public learning and browser-local review; no external result, provider action, or financial action inferred.

| Required field | Standard |
|---|---|
| Source and date | Identify the source route or record and its observed date/version where available. |
| Operating state | Separate available-now local functionality, research, owner release, and independently verified external results. |
| Limitation | State what the route does not establish, execute, or authorize. |
| Correction | Offer a visible correction or source-update pathway. |
| Teaching access | Keep public learning routes readable and available without implying that study is a product, endorsement, license, or rights determination. |

Creator protection requires source, contribution, likeness, rights, accessibility, release, and exact-action evidence before a work is represented as released. The standard preserves direct first-party presentation while keeping third-party sources, unverified outcomes, and provider-dependent actions separately labeled.
