## Verified Activation Sequence

1. Start with ordinary creator-commerce products only after their exact title, rights, price, delivery, refund, tax, customer-support, and owner release record are approved.
2. Use a verified live payment-provider account, live credentials stored only in secure project settings, and a tested webhook that records success, failure, refunds, disputes, and fulfillment without overstating delivery or asset issuance.
3. Treat payment-card issuance, payouts, custody, lending, exchange, investment, money transmission, and on-chain functionality as separate provider/regulatory operating lanes—not as a consequence of a checkout page existing.
4. For any irreversible payment, payout, wallet signature, transfer, or broadcast, obtain transaction-specific owner confirmation in the active conversation and retain evidence of the exact target, amount, network, asset, and consequence.
