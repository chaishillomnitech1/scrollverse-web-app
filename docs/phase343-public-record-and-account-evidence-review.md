## Exact private-evidence requirement

To review a private account record, the owner would need to connect and authorize the exact intended Google/YouTube account through an approved account-specific flow that exposes the relevant service and read scope. A valid export or screenshot of the account-status notice and a channel’s own YouTube Studio export could also supply a source-labeled record. Even with authorized records, a suspension notice or subscription history would document platform actions and account activity only; it would not by itself establish motive, personal targeting, government involvement, or external coordination.
