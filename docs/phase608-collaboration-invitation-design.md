# Phase 608 — Creator-First Collaboration Invitation

## Purpose

This record defines a browser-local collaboration invitation for **SCROLL VERSE LLC & XLVIII BLOCKS LLC**. It turns the portal’s existing creator systems, visual archive, learning routes, release discipline, and capability comparison into a clear choice for visitors and prospective partners: inspect the work, select a real operating question, and prepare a bounded next conversation.

The invitation is not an assertion of universal rank, outside authority, completed partnership, product availability, financial return, or external acceptance. It is an open route into source-led review and voluntary collaboration.

## Collaboration Principles

1. **Public choice:** Visitors assess the record and choose whether to continue.
2. **One real question:** A collaboration starts with a specific workflow, not a claim of blanket capability.
3. **Evidence before promise:** Local features, source records, and test evidence stay distinct from external outcomes.
4. **Correction stays open:** New reproducible material can update the record through the capability-comparison correction path.
5. **External actions stay exact:** Contact, contracts, pricing, providers, payments, media releases, wallets, token actions, and schedules require item-specific authorization.

> **Creator-first evidence principle:** Owner-supplied records are preserved as meaningful proof of the work and process they document. Scope labels make each record legible and durable; they do not reduce its value.
