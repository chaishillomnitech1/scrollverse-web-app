# Phase 460 — Art Museum & Creation Station Support Governance

**Status:** Local operating architecture only. No grant, artist payment, reward, wallet, token, coin, NFT, account, provider integration, schedule, or public campaign is active.

## Purpose

The Art Museum & Creation Station can combine free learning, artifact archive, creative practice, and artist-support preparation without treating vulnerable visitors, young artists, or original works as financial instruments. The system keeps learning open and gives adult artists a route to prepare a portfolio, archive record, rights packet, and opportunity-ready project brief.

## Participation pathways

| Pathway | Available local experience | Financial and token boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Young artists and learners** | Free art discovery, philosophy, public-source study, browser-local prompts, and adult-supported participation guidance | No wallet, digital asset, crypto reward, payment, purchase, trade, prize, or public-upload requirement |
| **Adult emerging artists** | Portfolio prep, work inventory, source trail, rights checklist, original project brief, and opportunity-readiness record | No grant, commission, reward, funding, or payment promise until a separate program exists |
| **Established artists and rights holders** | Optional artifact record, visible credit pathway, provenance preparation, and rights-aware representation planning | No compensation, representation, license, collector token, sale, or payout without a named agreement and release record |
| **Visitors and supporters** | Free reading, museum discovery, learning tools, and transparent research pathways | No solicitation, contribution, payment, or financial claim is created by this architecture |

## Adult artist-support program design

Any future artist-support program must have an exact name, purpose, eligibility, review process, funding source, recipient verification, privacy statement, rights treatment, support terms, correction path, and owner release. It may support a concrete non-financial outcome such as an archive packet, portfolio review, accessible artist statement, rights inventory, or project plan before any funding or commercial component is considered.

## Digital-asset and compensation separation

Digital assets and compensation are not prerequisites for learning, visibility, or artist participation. A future adult-only program can evaluate a digital representation or compensation pathway only for a documented participant and only when all of the following exist: a verified adult recipient, source and rights record, explicit benefit/term description, funding or fee source, privacy handling, payout recipient, applicable approval, and a specific release action. The Art Museum does not establish those records by itself.

## Browser-local operations

The Creation Station may prepare an **Artist Support Brief** locally with a project title, adult creator context, source links, rights state, requested preparation help, accessibility needs, and next evidence. It does not submit the brief to a database, contact a funder, create a profile, collect youth data, send a message, connect a wallet, or create a payment request.

## Evolving museum rule

Every new physical or digital work can enter through the Asset Registry intake desk. The Art Museum then becomes the object-focused lens and the Creation Station the learning and preparation lens. A work progresses only when its own evidence and release fields are ready; adding an object to the registry does not cause public display, financial activity, or a token action.
