# Phase 631 — Cover Art Storyworlds Records

## Catalog treatment principle

Each cover enters the Storyworlds catalog as a **source-led visual record**. A record can have a browser-local story direction before an external release exists. The story direction does not generate, publish, distribute, list, mint, price, transfer, or otherwise activate the underlying release.

## Per-cover release-preparation fields

Every Storyworlds record carries its own **cover source**, **release title**, **creator/publishing context**, **visual direction**, **accessibility description**, **intended destination**, and **owner-release decision** fields. An NFT, mint, marketplace, token, product, distribution, stream, or audio source remains a separate exact record for that cover; no storyworld card changes its external operating state.
