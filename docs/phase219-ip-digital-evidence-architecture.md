# Phase 219 — IP and Digital Evidence Navigator Architecture

The public navigator uses one evidence-first vocabulary: **publicly observable record**, **owner-supplied context**, **independently verified official record**, and **missing or needs review**. It helps a visitor identify the exact source, observation date, scope, limitation, and next qualified-review question without making a legal conclusion.

| Evidence lane | Public route may show | Separate review still required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original creative work | Public-safe title/context, source date, public link, and source label. | Rights holder, registration, chain of title, licenses, enforcement, and release rights. |
| Mark/name | Exact reference link and dated search/record context. | Clearance, geographic scope, goods/services, registration, use, and enforceability. |
| Patent/invention | Exact public patent/publication or assignment-search reference. | Patentability, current ownership, scope, infringement, validity, and commercialization. |
| Domain | Dated RDAP/technical registration context. | Trademark/business ownership, authority, web-content provenance, and control. |
| Code/project record | Public repository/release/commit context. | Author identity, external dependencies, security, deployment state, commercial rights, and IP registration. |
| Owner assertion | Transparent source-labeled context. | Independent support before verified public wording. |

The browser-local record builder deliberately excludes sensitive material. It neither uploads, stores, signs, hashes, registers, submits, nor files a record. It directs an owner toward protected review rather than creating public sensitive data.
