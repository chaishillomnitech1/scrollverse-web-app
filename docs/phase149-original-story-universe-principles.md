# Phase 149 — Original Story Universe Principles

## Purpose

The ScrollVerse story universe builds **creator-owned** characters, worlds, episodes, comics, and audience development paths. External work can inform general craft such as legible visual identity, episodic momentum, emotional stakes, and accessible navigation, but it is not copied into ScrollVerse characters, names, settings, images, story beats, logos, or releases.

## Original-world standard

Each concept lane must retain a distinct character name, premise, setting, visual motif, source record, and development stage. Public references are treated as learning sources only; they do not establish any relationship, license, publisher access, or approval.

## Participation standard

The Story Seed Lab remains private and adult-supported. It does not create public child profiles, direct private messaging, automatic publication, financial offers, or automatic incorporation of submitted ideas. A clear adult consent, moderation, rights-review, and final release decision is required before a contribution can be shared or used.

This follows widely recognized youth-media guidance emphasizing meaningful youth participation alongside privacy, safety, and transparent governance [1]. Childnet’s practical safeguarding guidance identifies conduct, content, contact, and commercial risks in youth-facing online activities, and recommends clear policies, organizational accounts, moderation, and consent-aware practices [2].
