## Record-Reading Method

The inventory preserves the original source path or supplied URL first, then separates **what the record directly says** from a visitor’s interpretation of it. Creator-authored identity, frequency, spiritual framework, fictional character, visual-development, and narrative material may be expressed in the Digital DNA route with attribution. Assertions about external products, accounts, payments, wallets, companies, security, legal rights, deployments, systems, or other organizations stay in the source record unless an appropriate primary record establishes the asserted outcome.
