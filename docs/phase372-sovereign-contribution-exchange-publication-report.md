# Phase 372 — Sovereign Contribution and Co-Creation Exchange Publication Report

## Delivered visitor pathway

The local ScrollVerse portal now includes `/sovereign-contribution-exchange`, a free choice pathway for visitors who want to learn, shape an idea, prepare a co-creation brief, join community reflection, study governance routes, explore approved creator support, or record a future exchange concept without triggering a transaction.

The reader presents five visible lanes: **Learn**, **Idea**, **Co-create**, **Community**, and **Future exchange**. Each lane has its own operating-state label and links to the connected ScrollVerse route. This keeps free learning, browser-local planning, community dialogue, creator preparation, direct-to-fan discovery, and future exchange concepts distinct.

## Browser-local contribution planner

The new browser-local planner allows a visitor to select a contribution lane, title an idea, describe an intended outcome, add optional context, view local brief cards, and copy their local brief. It does not submit material to a server, contact a collaborator, post a community item, create an agreement, tally a vote, accept funds, activate a wallet, or deliver an asset.

| Planner capability | Current behavior |
|---|---|
| Draft an idea or contribution brief | Available in the current browser session only. |
| Copy a local brief | Available after a brief is created; the visitor reviews the copy before choosing any later route. |
| Community conversation | Linked through the existing Chosen Ones Experience Exchange. |
| Governance learning | Linked through Assistive Governance and Governance reading paths. |
| Creator support and future exchange | Linked through the Direct-to-Fan Catalog and Creator Services Navigator. |
| Submission, vote, trade, payment, contract, delivery, token, or wallet action | Not activated. Requires a separate source, privacy, rights, consent, and owner-release record. |

## Source and protected intake boundary

The page uses transparent participation, co-creation, and contribution practices from public learning sources. These sources support clear opportunities, inclusive participation, multiple types of contributions, accountable response paths, and meaningful decision context. [1] [2] [3]

The owner-supplied Autonomous Contribution Engine registry and Episode 22 screenplay remain protected reference material. The page retained only compatible structural lessons—distinct lanes, source labels, local planning, contribution clarity, and a correction path. It did not import the attachment’s test totals, addresses, provider methods, funding routes, token/staking language, frequency outcome claims, external-validation language, script status, or deployment assertions as active portal state.

## Activation requirements

A future contribution intake, project match, proposal workflow, community ballot, barter/trade offer, financial support option, or product/service exchange must be designed as a distinct flow. At minimum it needs a purpose, visible operating state, participant role, data/privacy boundary, rights/permission context, review and response process, correction/withdrawal path, and explicit owner release. Any payment, wallet, token, contract, delivery, or transfer action also needs its own approved item record and separately confirmed test-mode review before a production action.
