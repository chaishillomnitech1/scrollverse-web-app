## Visual Language

Use navy and near-black space as a field of reflection; cyan as a signal of inquiry; gold as a limited archival accent; and green as a marker for consent, care, and completed review. Use star geometry, tessellated pattern, directional light, and expansive negative space without distorting sacred text into ornament.

The public Manus Hero Codex should keep devotional content separate from its operational method. It can state that the visual language is guided by reverence, unity, and stewardship; it should not portray the guide as a religious authority, a spiritual being, or an object of devotion.

Before publishing any page that includes Arabic scripture, translations, or the Shahādah, perform a dedicated content review for spelling, layout, reading order, source attribution, page context, and respectful presentation. Keep sacred material outside monetization, token gating, memberships, gamification, and promotional calls to action.
