## Owner-authored methodology

> The owner’s methodology is to document and scribe his life, art, music, fashion, observations, and research as they happen; examine words and sayings for how they can shape assumptions or barriers; study established disciplines deeply; and build original methods that let the work, dates, sources, actions, and outcomes remain visible for others to assess.

The record is not a demand that a reader adopt the owner’s interpretation. Its purpose is an accessible archive where a personal account, original work, public source, historical method, and later correction can appear together without collapsing into one another.

## Evidence boundary

The existence of a written record demonstrates that the owner made a statement, stored a source, or created a work at a documented point in time. It does not independently establish the accuracy, legal status, scientific result, or universal meaning of every interpretation attached to the record. Where external facts are included, the page should cite the source and capture date. Where an observation is personal, the page should identify it as first-person context.

## Correction path

Each future methodology entry should identify its source type, date, version, intended use, linked materials, current operating state, and correction path. A public source may be updated; a personal account may be clarified by the owner; an original work may advance from research to owner release only through its own rights and release record.
