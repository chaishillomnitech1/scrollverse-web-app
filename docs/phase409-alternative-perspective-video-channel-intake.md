Each source is presented as a source of perspective, not as an authority that resolves the topic it discusses. The first two videos use identity, ancestry, and cosmic-transformation framing. ScrollVerse will keep those descriptions attributed to the sources and will not represent them as biographical, scientific, historical, or metaphysical fact.
