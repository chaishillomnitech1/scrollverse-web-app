# Phase 222 — Brand Feature, Collaboration, and Incentive Research

**Reference date:** August 14, 2026. This record provides public guidance context for future feature concepts. It is not legal advice, a promotion rule set, a brand approval, a sponsorship agreement, or permission to use any product, mark, image, endorsement, incentive, or prize.

| Topic | Primary finding | Studio rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand features and cross-promotion | FTC guidance says endorsements must be honest and not misleading. When an endorser/marketer relationship that could affect audience evaluation is not expected, it should be disclosed clearly and conspicuously. The same context applies to paid, free-product, incentive, family/employee, affiliate, and other material connections. [1] | Feature concepts remain visibly labeled `owner-supplied idea — brand confirmation required` unless an approved relationship record exists. If a future material connection exists, make an appropriate clear disclosure in the relevant content. |
| Brand visuals and suggested looks | The FTC notes that a picture or video of a product can convey endorsement, even without words, when it communicates positive approval. [1] | Do not use a third-party product visual or implied product recommendation as an official feature. Keep current media private/source-labeled until rights, context, and release decisions are documented. |
| Prizes and sweepstakes | FTC consumer guidance states that real sweepstakes are free and by chance; it is illegal to require payment or purchase to enter or improve odds. It also highlights accurate winner/prize communication and privacy concerns. [2] | The initial studio has no prize, contest, random draw, paid-entry, NFT/token incentive, or winner-selection function. Any future promotion needs an exact concept, qualified review, eligibility/official-rules approach, privacy plan, and a no-purchase/claim process suitable to the relevant jurisdiction. |
| Brand alerts and posts | A message to a named company or public campaign can imply a relationship and create public-facing representation risk. | No alert, email, social post, or outreach is sent from the studio. Create only a browser-local collaboration brief pending an approved contact, terms, disclosure, and explicit owner decision. |
