## Profile experience

The profile experience expands the Legion Specialist Atlas with five interactive roles: Science Research Guide, Medical Research Navigator, Legal Source Navigator, Financial Literacy Navigator, and Governance Framework Guide. Each profile carries its purpose, evidence needs, and a role-specific safeguard so visitors can inspect the limits before preparing a local improvement question.

| High-impact role | Visible safeguard |
|---|---|
| Medical Research Navigator | No diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, care decision, or personal-health record |
| Legal Source Navigator | No legal representation, personal legal conclusion, filing, or attorney-client relationship |
| Financial Literacy Navigator | No personal investment recommendation, account action, trade, transfer, wallet action, or execution |
| Governance Framework Guide | No autonomous policy, vote, binding decision, or authority |

## Public-input pathway

Visitors may select a profile, add a public-safe improvement question, acknowledge the browser-local boundary, and copy a local proposal. The route does not send, store, vote on, submit, apply, or otherwise act on the text.
