# Phase 239 — Open Research Source Register

## Evidence Categories

The Open Research Navigator will distinguish **established scientific data**, **official public records**, **historical/archive records**, **cultural or religious interpretation**, **unresolved questions**, and **speculation**. A record of an unresolved observation is not evidence for a proposed extraordinary explanation.

## Source Lanes

| Lane | Public source and finding | Use in ScrollVerse |
|---|---|---|
| Astronomy and physics | NASA’s research archives describe public access to science data and technical information across Earth science, heliophysics, planetary science, and astrophysics. NASA notes that its science archives are organized by scientific discipline or theme. [4] | Offer an archive-literacy path focused on provenance, policy context, missing records, and the difference between an official file and a verified event claim. |

## Research Guardrails

Frequency labels, cosmic-technology claims, teleportation proposals, religious interpretations, and extraordinary explanations can be listed as **questions or cultural study prompts** only when the navigator also makes the evidence state and limitations visible. The route will not present speculative links as scientific proof, medical treatment, engineering feasibility, government confirmation, or commercial opportunity.
