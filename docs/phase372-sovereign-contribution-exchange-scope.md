# Phase 372 — Sovereign Contribution and Co-Creation Exchange Scope

## Purpose

The Sovereign Contribution and Co-Creation Exchange is a free ScrollVerse visitor pathway for people who want to learn, contribute, collaborate, reflect, participate in community dialogue, or prepare an approved future exchange. It is built around voluntary participation and clear operating states. No visitor is required to contribute money, personal data, labor, ideas, or a public statement in order to access free knowledge.

## Co-creation principles

> A contribution is an offer of participation, not a transfer of ownership. A collaboration brief is a starting point for conversation, not an agreement. A community vote or reflection is input, not a substitute for the authority, consent, rights, and review required for a specific action.

The Exchange will respect the distinction between an original idea, an owner-approved contribution, a public source, a protected private record, and a completed external outcome. When a visitor writes a brief in the browser, it remains local to that browser unless the visitor independently chooses a connected community route that has its own participation terms.

## Exchange and support boundary

The page may route a visitor to an approved direct-to-fan catalog, creator-service preparation route, community exchange, or governance reading path. It will not create a payment, trade, donation, membership, contract, account, vote, wallet action, asset transfer, or delivery obligation from a visitor click. Any future item involving funds, goods, a service, intellectual property, personal data, or a token must receive a separate approved item record and clear participant terms.

## Accessibility and correction path

Every lane will be presented with a plain-language title, operating-state label, visible purpose, and direct route. The future planner will have labelled inputs, keyboard controls, copy/export behavior, and a warning not to enter passwords, account numbers, payment data, private contract terms, or personal sensitive information. The owner may add an approved intake procedure to a lane only after identifying what is accepted, how it will be reviewed, who can see it, and how a participant can correct or withdraw it.
