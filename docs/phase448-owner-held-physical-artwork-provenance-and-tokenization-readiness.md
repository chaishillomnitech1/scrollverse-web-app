## Exact release gate

Before any irreversible public action, the owner must confirm the exact collection record, selected pathway, image/public-display decision, chain and network, minting service or contract, public custody destination, edition or supply, fee ceiling, and any price/listing decision. No private key, seed phrase, recovery code, or sensitive custody credential belongs in the archive record.

## Collection care note

For long-term physical preservation, retain the original frame and labels, avoid direct sunlight and damp storage, do not attach labels or adhesives to the artwork surface, and document any handling or restoration. A qualified conservator or appraiser can advise on condition, medium, and valuation if the owner later needs an external assessment.
