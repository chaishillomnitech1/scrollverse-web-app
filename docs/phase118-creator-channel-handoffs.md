## Display rules

Each handoff opens in a new tab with `rel="noreferrer"`. Cards should name the destination and state that the link was provided by the creator. The interface must not use platform trademarks as ScrollVerse branding, imply that a platform has endorsed ScrollVerse, or state that the link is a verified business account where a public session cannot establish that.

## Future review

The owner can update display labels, substitute a direct creator-controlled landing page, or provide public screenshots and release copy through the protected Creator Operations Workspace. A title, channel attribution, or campaign context for the supplied YouTube video should be added only after direct confirmation.
