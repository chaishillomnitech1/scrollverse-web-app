# Phase 173 — Distribution Highways Source Register

The Distribution Highways map provides a representative, source-labeled public route set for creators. It is not an exhaustive provider directory and does not represent a commercial affiliation, account, delivery channel, acceptance decision, or payout relationship.

| Lane | Destination | Official public route | Public onboarding or delivery boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Music distribution | Vydia | [Vydia][1] | Application/request-for-invite pathway; public information does not establish membership. |
| Music distribution | UnitedMasters | [UnitedMasters][2] | Plan/account and provider-controlled access. |
| Music distribution | EMPIRE | [EMPIRE][3] | Direct, selective relationship; no public self-service artist submission surfaced. |
| Music distribution | Sony Music Entertainment | [Sony Music][4] | No unsolicited creative-material route. |
| Music distribution | The Orchard | [The Orchard][5] | Selective/direct qualification rather than public self-service delivery. |
| Video creator | YouTube Studio | [YouTube Creators][6] | Authenticated account and policy-controlled uploads. |
| Music profile | Spotify for Artists | [Spotify for Artists][7] | Music is delivered through an approved distributor or label before profile access. |
| Music profile | Apple Music for Artists | [Apple Music for Artists][8] | Qualifying released role and platform verification required to claim. |
| Premium streaming | Netflix Partner Help Center | [Netflix Partner Help][9] | Invitation, approval, or authorized fulfillment-partner relationship. |
| Premium streaming | Hulu Content Portal | [Hulu Content][10] | Authorized partner/distributor status required. |
| Premium streaming | Tubi Partner | [Tubi Partner][11] | Approval and licensing terms required. |

The map is paired with `/media-readiness-lab`, which performs browser-local inspection and package export only. It does not publish media or deliver a package to a provider.
