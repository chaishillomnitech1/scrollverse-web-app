# Phase 108 — Official Distribution and Creator Pathways

## Working Studio Identity

**ScrollVerse Signalworks** is the working identity for the public entertainment and creator-distribution navigator. It is an original ScrollVerse program name, **not a claim of trademark registration, provider affiliation, distribution authority, or placement guarantee**. The public navigator will function as a transparent routing layer: it helps creators understand official next steps and then directs them to the provider’s own portal.

## Verified Public Handoffs

| Ecosystem | Official creator or delivery path | Navigator boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Netflix | Content pitches follow Netflix’s established industry submission channel. [1] | Netflix does not accept unsolicited submissions; ScrollVerse will not represent itself as an agent, producer, legal representative, or Netflix partner. |
| Tubi | Content partners can use Tubi’s official partner portal. [2] | Registration and submission do not guarantee acquisition, distribution, or placement. |
| Prime Video Slate | Filmmakers can review the self-publishing workflow through Prime Video Slate. [3] | Review, policy compliance, and publication decisions remain with Amazon. |
| Filmhub | Filmmakers may submit finished titles through Filmhub’s distribution workflow. [4] | Delivery or quality-control passage does not guarantee channel licensing or release. |
| Kick | Streamers can configure a channel and broadcast workflow through Kick’s creator guidance. [5] | Creator access is not a partnership, endorsement, or revenue guarantee. |
| YouTube Studio | Creators manage channels, uploads, and analytics through YouTube Studio. [6] | Publishing access does not confer monetization, sponsorship, or platform endorsement. |
| Spotify for Artists | Artists can claim or access artist tools through Spotify for Artists. [7] | Artist access is distinct from distribution and does not promise editorial placement. |
| Apple Music for Artists | Artists can request access to existing catalog tools through Apple Music for Artists. [8] | An existing release path and provider eligibility may be required; access is not an endorsement. |
| CapCut | Eligible creators can review the Template Creator pathway on CapCut. [9] | Program participation is subject to regional rules and review. |
| Meta | Creators can use Meta Business Suite for eligible Facebook and Instagram accounts. [10] | Access remains subject to platform terms and eligibility and does not imply an official relationship. |

## Operating Model

Signalworks will make each handoff **source-labelled, voluntary, and explainable**. A creator chooses a lane—film, streaming, music, live, social, or production tools—then sees the official entry point, a concise readiness prompt, and a boundary statement. No account is connected, material submitted, contract executed, content licensed, or money moved by the navigator.

## Verification Notes

On August 11, 2026, Netflix’s first-party help center was reviewed directly. It states that Netflix does not accept or review materials it has not specifically requested, so Signalworks must never offer a direct Netflix submission action. Tubi’s official partner portal was identified but could not be opened in the current research browser due to a platform access restriction; its navigator card will remain source-labelled and will not assert eligibility, access, or a relationship beyond the official destination link.

Prime Video Slate’s official documentation route was also identified but unavailable in the current research browser because of platform access restrictions. YouTube Studio redirected to the standard Google sign-in screen, confirming that its workspace requires a creator’s own Google account session. Signalworks will provide both links as optional handoffs only; it will not collect credentials, submit content, or claim account access on a creator’s behalf.
