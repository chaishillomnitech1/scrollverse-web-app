# Phase 175 — Inclusive Opportunity Navigator

The Opportunity Navigator combines free first-party preparation with transparent official public opportunities. It does not bypass provider, employer, platform, grant, licensing, or other eligibility rules.

| Path type | Creator benefit | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Free first-party routes | Preparation record, media readiness, public provider comparison, and revenue-model education. | No guarantee of opportunity, income, admission, selection, or placement. |
| Official public opportunities | Direct source links to published grant, resource, and creator routes. | The external organization controls eligibility, deadlines, review, acceptance, and awards. |
| Creator control | Private creator records remain selected and controlled by the creator. | ScrollVerse does not submit applications, decide eligibility, or make provider decisions. |
