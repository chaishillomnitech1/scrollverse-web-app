The public route `/scroll-network-context` may present chain-level learning context and direct source links with dates and limitations. It must not incorporate current dashboard figures as permanent status, list a third-party valuation as a ScrollVerse valuation, or convert an outside project’s marketing, social commentary, or research into a claim of ownership, endorsement, commercial relationship, or financial performance.
