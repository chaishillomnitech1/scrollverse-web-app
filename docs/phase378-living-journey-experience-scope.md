# Phase 378 — Living Journey Experience Scope

## Purpose

The **Living Journey Experience** is a free ScrollVerse pathway where a visitor can move through the owner’s books, original music, visual worlds, Junior Movies routes, comics, research, source records, and contribution pathways as connected chapters rather than disconnected pages. The experience is designed as a reader-led journey: visitors may read, listen, watch, explore, or continue into another route without a payment, login, or transaction being required to access the learning layer.

## Free-reader commitment

The Living Journey Experience is a free reading, listening, learning, and navigation layer. A visitor should not need to pay, enter a payment method, connect a wallet, provide credentials, or create an account merely to move through its book and discovery chapters. A future commercial edition, direct offer, ticket, service, collection, token, or supporter route remains separate and must identify its own exact item, source state, visitor promise, privacy boundary, and owner release.

## Original-media and rights boundary

The experience can feature original ScrollVerse work and approved creator-controlled routes. A third-party title, character, visual universe, soundtrack, clip, or likeness—including **Dragon Ball Z**—will not be copied, embedded, reproduced, marketed as a ScrollVerse asset, or used as an identity substitute unless an appropriate documented right and purpose-specific authorization exists. A public, permitted reference link can be identified as an external reference; an original visual or story world should remain clearly original and source-labeled.

## Accessibility commitment

Every experience card will state its type—book, music, visual, research, community, or contribution—and use a readable label, visible operating state, keyboard-reachable control, and direct route. Visual art will receive an accessible descriptive label; audio and video routes will retain their own captions, transcripts, or release-readiness requirements as applicable.

## Correction path

The experience will show only routes and materials that are currently registered in the portal. Each connected work keeps its own source, rights, release, and correction record. The experience page itself will be updated whenever an original media asset, free book, or source route is added, corrected, or withdrawn.
