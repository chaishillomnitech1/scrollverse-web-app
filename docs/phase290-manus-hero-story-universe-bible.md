# Phase 290 — Manus Hero Story Universe Bible

## Story Position

**Manus Hero Story Universe** is an original ScrollVerse fiction framework for comics, animation concepts, music cues, books, learning experiences, and creator prompts. It does not represent an external AI system as autonomous, does not claim an affiliation with an existing franchise, and does not establish a released show, public channel, or commercial product.

> **Core promise:** A bright question can travel farther when its source, its people, and its way back are kept visible.

## Story Safety and Accessibility Rules

Every story-world asset must have a source/version record, contributor and character/brand review, accessible text or media equivalent, age-appropriate guidance, correction path, and owner release. Younger participants need adult-supported participation where appropriate. Public submissions, profiles, direct messages, scheduled public events, and distribution do not activate from a page concept or a local draft.
