# Phase 352 — Contributor-Led First-Person Experience Model

**Operating state:** authenticated participant contribution controls available in the Chosen Ones Experience Exchange.

| Participant control | Experience model |
|---|---|
| Consent | A participant chooses to open the contribution composer and submits their own first-person text after authentication |
| Identity | The participant chooses a display name or pseudonym for entries and replies |
| Visibility | Profile settings support public, community, or pseudonymous visibility; individual entries use the selected contribution state |
| Discussion | Authors select whether respectful replies are enabled for an entry |
| Reflection | Authenticated participants may react or save a reflection; those actions do not alter authorship |
| Community care | Theme filters, readable entry/reply controls, correction path, and author-owned first-person framing support calm participation |

The exchange presents a participant’s own words rather than assigning a required conclusion. A contribution, profile, reaction, reply, or saved reflection does not establish external identity, endorsement, professional guidance, factual verification, or provider action.
