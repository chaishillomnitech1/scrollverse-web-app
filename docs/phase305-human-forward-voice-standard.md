# Phase 305 — Human-Forward Voice Standard

**Author:** Manus AI  
**Date:** August 15, 2026  
**Current state:** **available now** for browser-local device-voice direction; **requires owner release** for any custom or person-specific voice.

## Purpose

ScrollVerse voice experiences should sound warm, clear, expressive, and easy to hear rather than rigid or monotone. The standard is designed for books, media, story worlds, community guidance, and learning experiences. It defines original delivery qualities, not an imitation of a specific person.

| Delivery profile | Intended experience | Browser-local direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm Storyteller | Inviting, grounded, cinematic | Slower pacing with natural pauses for books and family stories. |
| Cool Host | Smooth, confident, professional | Conversational pacing for media, interviews, and creator introductions. |
| Clear Guide | Accessible, precise, reassuring | Deliberate pace for instruction and learning routes. |
| Playful Spark | Bright, kind, expressive | Youth-safe humor and creative prompts without harshness or mockery. |

## Production Rules

Write for the ear: use short natural sentences, punctuation that creates room to breathe, and scripts whose emotional direction matches their words. Let warmth, humor, a smile, a natural pause, or an occasional purposeful non-speech sound come from the performance direction rather than from an attempt to reproduce a public figure or private individual. Keep the accompanying text visible, provide captions or a transcript, make playback opt-in, and preserve mute/stop control.

## Specific-Person Voice Gate

No identifiable person’s voice, laugh, or emotional signature should be copied from public clips or used because it is publicly available. A person-specific voice project needs the person’s clear recorded consent, an approved clean source sample, provider eligibility, final script/use context, transcript/caption plan, revocation path, and explicit owner release. Until then, use the original delivery profiles above.

## Correction Path

To refine a voice experience, record which phrase, pace, profile, language, device voice, or accessibility issue needs improvement. For a person-specific project, add the consent and source records before any provider activation or generation request.
