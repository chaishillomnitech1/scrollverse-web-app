# Phase 591 — Connected Creator Atlas Design

## Purpose

The **Creator Atlas** is a browser-local orientation route. It presents the owner-authored body of work as connected systems rather than isolated pages, helping a visitor enter at their own level of familiarity and move between visual work, learning, books, co-creation context, and creator readiness pathways.

## Visitor Experience

The route starts with a one-sentence orientation and two selectable entry modes: **Start here** for a new visitor and **Go deeper** for a returning visitor. Each group uses concise source-aware copy, a distinct color treatment, an operating label, and direct links only to registered browser-local routes. The page should not imply a transaction, provider account, marketplace, token, or external publication.

## Public-Choice and Correction Standard

The Atlas presents owner-authored and co-creation context directly for public choice. Every group retains a visible operating state and links visitors toward the collection, tutorial, book, archive, or readiness route that supplies more detail. A visitor can explore the body of work without being asked to infer ownership, external rights, market value, or provider status beyond what each destination record supports.
