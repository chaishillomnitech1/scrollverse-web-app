# Phase 375 — Ultimate Choice Framework Learning Intake

## Scope and operating state

This record preserves the owner-supplied **Ultimate Choice Framework** for free learning on **2026-08-16 EDT**. The framework presents four response patterns: **change it**, **accept it**, **remove yourself**, and **suffer through**. It also distinguishes micro choices, macro choices, and internal filters. Its operating state is **research and reflection**.

The framework is presented as a reflective learning tool, not a universal law, clinical model, mental-health treatment, moral test, or instruction for a particular high-stakes situation. A reader can use it to name options and explore sources, then make their own decision.

## Reader-facing language standard

> The four pathways are a way to pause and name a response: alter the condition, accept the condition, step away from the condition, or notice ongoing resistance. The framework does not decide for the reader; it opens a place to think.

The future book entry will identify the framework as owner-supplied, show its reflection questions, link to reviewed public materials, and note that the numerical decision-point statistic remains unverified. The page will not use the framework to tell a visitor what they must do about health, safety, family, employment, money, legal matters, or any other consequential personal decision.
