# ScrollVerse Spiral Signature — Creator-Owned Concept Brief

## Purpose

The **ScrollVerse Spiral Signature** is an original first-party identity device for Chais "Sabir Kajir" Hill the Great and the ScrollVerse world. It is intended for voluntary use across the Manus Hero Codex, Creator Operations, identity pages, app-icon exploration, and creator-owned release materials. The signature is free to access within ScrollVerse and is not dependent on a marketplace, an external platform, or a third-party partnership.

## Visual Logic

The mark is **informed by but not drawn as a standard Fibonacci spiral**. Its core gesture is an asymmetric, clockwise, open-ended scroll spiral with five deliberate resonance breaks. Those breaks symbolize continuity, creative direction, provenance, collaboration, and public discovery. The form opens upward-right to suggest a living scroll, rather than a closed loop or a borrowed mathematical diagram.

The palette remains first-party ScrollVerse: deep midnight `#050B1A`, electric cyan `#00D4FF`, sovereign gold `#FFB800`, and resonance green `#00FF88`. The companion lockup is `EST. 2026 · CTG`, rendered outside the mark in production typography rather than embedded as generated image text. The design standard is **imagination in form, wisdom in decisions, care in access, and understanding for creators**.

## QR Identity Path

The companion QR signature will point to the public Manus Hero Codex route. It must never encode private keys, credentials, backend endpoints, session data, or hidden software instructions. The QR is a public identity and provenance doorway—not a security control or trademark-registration claim.
