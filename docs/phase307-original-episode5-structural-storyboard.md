# Phase 307 — Original Episode 5 Structural Storyboard

**Working production:** *The Black Beacon Chronicles*  
**Working episode title:** *Episode 5 — The Cartographer’s Ember*  
**Operating state:** **research**  
**Production form:** Original youth-aware audio-visual episode and interactive-comic storyboard framework.

## Creator-Directed Story Statement

This original ScrollVerse production follows a neighborhood archive that learns to preserve a future without locking people out of their own stories. It presents a creator-controlled world of imagination, care, craft, and open knowledge. The working title, characters, story beats, and visual language below are original production planning material; they are not a finished episode, casting record, public release, or representation of an external franchise.

## Original Story World

| Element | Working definition |
| --- | --- |
| The Beacon Archive | A living public memory house powered by stories that community members choose to share. |
| Imani Vale | A map-maker who turns overlooked memories into paths for young creators. |
| Sol Nadir | A sound architect who hears rhythm in ordinary city life and translates it into safe, expressive soundscapes. |
| Juno Reed | A young repair-maker who finds a hidden route between paper archives and digital scrolls. |
| CODA | An original archive guide that offers questions, source prompts, and navigation—not decisions on behalf of people. |
| The Ember Map | A visual record that lights only when a story has a source, an accessible description, and a chosen sharing boundary. |

## Human-Forward Voice Direction

Use original delivery directions from the ScrollVerse Voice & Language Studio: **Warm Storyteller**, **Cool Host**, **Clear Guide**, and **Playful Spark**. Scripts should use natural punctuation, short sentences, meaningful pauses, and emotion that arises from the scene. Do not imitate an identifiable performer, public figure, or individual’s laugh, vocal signature, or emotional mannerism without that person’s recorded consent and approved source record.

## Visual and Interactive Direction

The visual language uses ScrollVerse deep-space blue, cyan wayfinding, gold archive light, emerald contribution signals, and violet night transitions. Every interactive step must work with keyboard navigation, visible focus, descriptive labels, a non-audio route, and reduced-motion support. The comic should never require a visitor to make a choice about the creator’s intent; any choice is a voluntary way to explore the story map.

## Release-Readiness Gate

Before any release, complete a single episode packet covering the final title, scripts, storyboard, character/world materials, contributor agreements, art and sound sources, voice consents, captions/transcript/audio description, age suitability, intended platform, and an explicit owner-release decision. Until then, the episode remains **research**.

## Correction Path

Record an issue by sequence and asset—for example: “Sequence 3, sound description,” “Sequence 4, source prompt,” or “Sequence 6, caption timing.” Update the production packet without altering the original intake record.
