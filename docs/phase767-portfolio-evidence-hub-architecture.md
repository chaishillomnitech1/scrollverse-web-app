# Phase 767 — ScrollVerse Portfolio Evidence Hub Architecture

## Purpose

The Portfolio Evidence Hub consolidates the browser-local source and readiness routes that describe creative, visual, automotive, digital, business, and partnership context. It provides discovery and preparation—not valuation, ownership verification, collateral classification, financing, or financial operations.

## Portfolio route map

| Evidence lane | Primary route | Current state | Boundary retained |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portfolio worksheet | `/portfolio-asset-evidence-inventory` | Local-only evidence preparation | No appraisal, ownership verification, title, collateral, or funding claim. |
| Automotive source context | `/autoempire-source-intake` | Reviewed source record | No vehicle inventory, production, sale, finance, provider, or payment operation. |
| Music and catalog context | `/music` and `/connected-statements-music-evidence` | Public listening handoff and source review | No rights, distribution, revenue, payout, sale, or account claim. |
| Visual and fashion context | `/protected-visual-evidence-hub`, `/creator-visual-intake-atlas`, `/fashion-empire`, and `/mein` | Source records and presentation routes | No identity, rights, inventory, production, sale, or vendor claim. |
| Digital creation context | `/digital-mirror-twin` and `/sovereign-cartoon-gallery` | Browser-local identity and visual-creation records | No mint, token, wallet, listing, sale, or transfer action. |
| Partnership and capital preparation | `/scrollverse-xlviiiblocks-readiness`, `/xlviiiblocks-proposal-canvas`, and `/capital-readiness` | Browser-local proposal and evidence preparation | No lender, partnership, contract, capital, credit, or transaction result. |
| Source continuity | `/transmissions-source-convergence` and `/connected-source-registry` | Protected source discovery | No technical, financial, provider, or external operating assertion. |

## Hub design requirements

The hub will use current-state labels and direct route handoffs. It will not show asset values, totals, ownership status, collateral eligibility, loan amounts, funding amounts, inventory totals, revenue, sales, or performance claims.
