# Transactional Exchange and Institutional Systems Atlas — Research Record

**Reference date:** August 14, 2026. This record supports a public learning and readiness route. It does not authorize ScrollVerse to offer payment, clearing, settlement, custody, brokerage, exchange, banking, lending, investment, money-transmission, or other regulated financial activity.

| Source | Public learning finding | Atlas design implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Federal Reserve — Payment Systems | The U.S. payment system includes currency, checks, ACH, and wholesale Fedwire payment systems; the Federal Reserve describes its role as promoting safe, efficient, and accessible U.S. dollar transactions and studying payment, clearing, and settlement systems. [1] | Explain system roles—payer, payee, processor, bank, operator, clearing/settlement, oversight, consumer access—without representing ScrollVerse as any of them. |
| BIS/CPMI-IOSCO — PFMI | The PFMI applies to systemically important payment, clearing, settlement, depository, counterparty, and trade-repository infrastructures and emphasizes more robust requirements and broader risk-management areas. [2] | Use governance, risk, operational resilience, disclosure, access, and accountability as learning lenses. Do not claim PFMI compliance or financial-market-infrastructure status. |
| FinCEN — Convertible Virtual Currency Guidance | FinCEN’s guidance addresses virtual currency arrangements and money-transmission questions, which depend on the exact facts and activities. [3] | Keep all token, wallet, exchange, custody, payment, prize, and transfer actions fail-closed; route any future product design to legal/compliance review before activation. |

## Atlas Rule

The route will provide a clear system map: **public learning**, **creator/readiness planning**, **professional/provider gate**, and **not offered here**. No lane will accept funds, move value, recommend an investment, quote a price, facilitate an exchange, create a wallet, connect a bank, custody assets, or select a counterparty.

## Route Verification

On August 14, 2026, `/institutional-systems-atlas` was visually verified in the ScrollVerse development portal. The established route rendered 11 source-led institutional-system practice cards, six browser-local interpretation lenses, public-source handoffs, a record-before-release sequence, and a no-hidden-action promise. It showed no deposit, custody, credit, loan, investment, securities, trade-clearing, account-opening, wallet, payment-detail, provider-form, or blockchain-action control.
