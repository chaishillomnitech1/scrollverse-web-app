# Phase 652 — Original Public Episode Slate + Non-Posting Calendar

## Purpose

This record translates the protected ScrollVerse episode archive into a public-version production slate. It does not claim that any episode is finished, uploaded, scheduled, premiered, monetized, or published. Each public version needs a confirmed master, representation decision, release metadata, caption file, channel destination, visibility, and exact owner release before video generation or a YouTube scheduling action.

## Common Production Baseline

The draft baseline is an original, all-ages public version with 16:9 landscape framing, spoken English or an owner-approved language, separate caption preparation, readable credits, original character identity, accessible visual contrast, and no unapproved third-party character, music, voice, or footage. Runtime, final narration, score, master, thumbnail, channel, visibility, and date remain unapproved until supplied in a per-episode release record.

## Non-Posting Calendar Choices

| Cadence | Creative use | What is prepared locally | What remains an owner-release action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Story cadence | One feature episode in a recurring weekly or biweekly window, with a short companion asset between releases. | Episode sequence, metadata draft, caption and thumbnail checklist, local calendar export. | Final date/time, channel destination, privacy setting, premiere selection, upload, and publication. |
| Event cadence | A limited launch arc: trailer, two public episodes, a creator dialogue, then the next chapter. | Local launch sequence, companion-asset list, and review gates. | Final media, collaborators, announcements, channel settings, and all public posts. |

## Required Record Before Video Execution or Scheduling

Each episode must have an approved record that names the source episode, target audience, total duration, 16:9 or 9:16 format, visual style, language, recurring character approvals, dialogue/narration direction, music-use authorization, final master path, caption file, thumbnail, channel destination, visibility, desired schedule or premiere setting, and same-conversation release confirmation.
