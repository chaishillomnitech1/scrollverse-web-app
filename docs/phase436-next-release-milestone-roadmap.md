# Phase 436 — Next Release Milestone Roadmap

**Milestone name:** **The Choiceable Release Frontier**  
**Purpose:** Turn the existing ScrollVerse creative world and readiness records into a connected visitor system where every visible choice has a truthful operating state, a source path, and a next step.

## Milestone thesis

ScrollVerse is most useful when a visitor can move from **meaning** to **choice** without the portal pretending a release has happened before it has. The next milestone therefore is not a vague expansion. It is a connected release spine: a visitor can explore freely, understand what is available, see what is being prepared, follow sources, and reach a live destination only when the destination is genuinely usable.

> **The boundary is not a dead end. It is an interface: it tells the visitor what exists, what is ready, and what moves the work forward.**

## Core concepts

| Concept | Working definition | Portal expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Choiceable release** | A visitor choice that has a truthful label, a usable destination, and a correction path | Payment Options Map, Product Release Pipeline, Direct-to-Fan Catalog |
| **Source trail** | The readable chain from item or claim to its source, date, context, and revision point | Source Study Library, Omni Manifesto, release packets |
| **Product as a promise** | A product page is a visitor promise before it becomes a payment surface | Item source, rights, accessibility, price, delivery, terms, support, and destination record |
| **Correction-capable interface** | A route that can return an inaccurate or incomplete record to a safer state without erasing the record | `requires owner release` rollback paths and Delivery Resilience |
| **Release signal** | The visible operating-state label that prevents a concept, research item, and public offering from being confused | `available now`, `free experience`, `research`, `requires owner release`, `proposal route` |
| **Bounded discovery** | Public-source discovery governed by visible seeds, relevance rules, deduplication, and correction rather than an unbounded feed | Synchronization Research queue design |
| **Action register** | A named record for any external action that may change visitors, funds, public availability, or provider configuration | Phase 430 Activation Register and `/activation-register` |
| **Dual delivery** | Local readiness and public delivery are related but separate states | Delivery Resilience and managed checkpoint records |

## Emerging definitions to develop carefully

The next frontier is not a claim of certainty. It is a set of testable definitions that can grow with the project.

| Emerging definition | Research question | Safe initial form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Creator operating system** | Can a creator manage sources, releases, distribution, visitor choice, and correction from one readable record? | Product Release Pipeline plus Activation Register |
| **Evidence mesh** | Can independent records remain connected without becoming one uncheckable narrative? | Source trails, operating-state labels, dated corrections, and public manifest index |
| **Choice commons** | Can visitors freely explore, support, propose, or purchase without coercion or fake availability? | Free routes, verified payment handoffs, proposal routes, and release-gated product paths |
| **Human-forward automation** | Can recurring work surface missing records and research candidates without replacing creator release judgment? | Read-only readiness audits and bounded research queue options |
| **Reversible release design** | Can a public item move safely back to review when its record changes? | Visible rollback to `requires owner release` and correction logs |

## Milestone success criteria

The milestone is complete when the following conditions can be checked directly:

1. A reader-directed Milestone Roadmap route connects the Product Release Pipeline, Premium Storefront Program, Payment Options Map, Source Study Library, Synchronization Research, Trading Readiness, Delivery Resilience, Activation Register, and public Omni Manifesto.
2. Every milestone destination displays a visible operating-state label and correction or continuation path.
3. At least one original item is prepared as a complete named launch packet, even if no provider-side product is published yet.
4. The bounded research queue is defined with source, relevance, claim-status, access, and correction fields; no schedule is activated before deployment and a named operation choice.
5. The exact provider-side action register remains visible and no live provider, payment, trade, payout, market, or schedule operation is represented as complete without its current system record.
6. TypeScript and the full regression suite pass after the roadmap route is added.
