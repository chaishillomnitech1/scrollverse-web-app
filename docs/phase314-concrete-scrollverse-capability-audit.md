## Controls That Are Deliberately Browser-Local

The portal’s active discovery controls deliberately run in the visitor’s browser. Production Continuum filters, Fashion & Artifact Atlas filters, Book/Media readiness filters, Creator Channel Hub search/sort/source controls, and Crypto Systems Atlas categorization change the visible local view. They do not publish content, alter a release state, write to an external platform, call a wallet, or change network state.

## External Gates That Still Require Their Own Record

The portal is designed to prepare and document external actions without fabricating them. An individual product, music release, book edition, creator collaboration, social post, token, wallet, payment, contract, reward program, marketplace listing, distribution handoff, or media publication requires its own source/version, contributor/rights context, accessible materials, destination, provider/account or public-network evidence where relevant, and direct owner-release decision.

| External action | Required gate before it can be represented as active |
| --- | --- |
| Social post | An active authorized account, approved original asset, caption/alt text or captions, and an actual result/permalink record. |
| Music, book, film, or visual release | Final master/edition, rights/contributor record, accessible materials, destination, and owner release. |
| Product or fashion offering | Final product evidence, mark/rights review, inventory/fulfillment record, terms/support path, and owner release. |
| Wallet, token, contract, or reward | Verified public contract/network record, defined role/action, security review, funding/gas consequence, appropriate terms, and explicit immediate confirmation before a live mutation. |
| Payment or provider integration | Verified provider account, configured secure controls, transaction/release context, and owner approval for the specific activation. |
