## 5. Tokenization and coin boundary

The collection has several **future technical options**, but none is an instruction to mint or issue now:

1. **Provenance-first record.** Start with a human-readable source ledger and final asset manifest. This is available now and does not require a chain.
2. **Integrity reference.** After rights records are complete, create a versioned checksum or metadata manifest that points to the specific authorized audio, artwork, credits, and release terms.
3. **Digital collectible.** Only after the master, composition, producer, visual, destination, purchaser terms, and owner approval are documented, a collectible can be scoped as a limited art/provenance edition.
4. **Standalone token or coin.** A separate coin is not implied by the song, cover, or view count. It would require a distinct purpose, supply/utility decision, risk and legal review, user terms, technical security review, buyer protections, and a specific owner-approved transaction decision.

No supply, price, yield, purchaser benefit, royalty percentage, donation/proceeds allocation, exchange listing, or financial return is proposed by this blueprint.
