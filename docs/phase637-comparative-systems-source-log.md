# Phase 637 — Comparative Systems Source Log

The Comparative Systems Atlas uses four public institutional sources as distinct starting points for study. Each card is a source-labeled research path, not a legal, financial, institutional, or policy conclusion for a particular visitor.

| District | Public source | Institution | Scope limit | Correction path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Financial foundations | [Global Financial Development Report](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/gfdr) | World Bank | Macroeconomic and systems analysis; not individualized financial guidance. | World Bank contact and feedback pathways. |
| Governance | [Worldwide Governance Indicators](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/worldwide-governance-indicators) | World Bank | Includes perception and expert-assessment inputs; does not directly measure every administrative output. | World Bank methodology-feedback pathways. |
| Public institutions | [Government at a Glance 2025](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2025/06/government-at-a-glance-2025_70e14c6c/full-report.html) | OECD | Focuses on OECD members and selected partners; it is not a universal country-by-country measure. | OECD publication and contact pathways. |
| Rights frameworks | [Universal Declaration of Human Rights](https://www.ohchr.org/en/universal-declaration-of-human-rights) | OHCHR | Foundational normative standard; not a self-executing treaty. | OHCHR contact pathways and source substitution in the Atlas. |

## Research boundary

The cards distinguish documented institutional sources from ScrollVerse’s browser-local design-pattern notes. They do not establish a law, enforce a right, authorize an action, create a program, move funds, or represent an external outcome as active.

## Browser verification

The browser-local `/research-atlas` route rendered all ten districts, including Financial Foundations, Governance, Public Institutions, and Rights Frameworks. The interface showed source labels, filter controls, a selected-record panel with a public-source link, a visible scope limit, a local correction field, copy/JSON controls, and direct Fair Practice and Legion Continuity handoffs. No public-source link, copy action, export, or external operation was activated during review.
