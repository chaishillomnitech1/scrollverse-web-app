## Evidence and Release Discipline

The material above is presented as creator-provided source context. Historic templates, copied status language, account or provider references, market figures, chain statements, and operational claims stay inactive unless an independent, item-specific record supports them. A physical object is not treated as a public digital reproduction; a visual concept is not treated as a token, wallet action, price, listing, or exchange event. Each selected work can move forward through the archive without changing any external state.
