## Future Attachment Rule

If a collectible is later considered, it must point to a versioned original publication record rather than implying ownership of real-world venues, destinations, editorial references, or third-party brands. The accompanying rights statement must explain exactly what a holder receives, what remains with the creator, and what no holder receives. No public destination listing becomes a commercial endorsement or a bundled offer without that organization’s written permission and an approved consumer-facing agreement.
