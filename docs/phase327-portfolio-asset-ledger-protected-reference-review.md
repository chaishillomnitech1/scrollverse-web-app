## Privacy and Rights Principle

Physical-asset references, family information, financial statements, private email links, and unverified ownership claims should not be placed in a public ScrollVerse record merely because they are supplied in an intake. A public route can explain the structure of an archive and show deliberately released creator materials; confidential items require their own permission, access, and verification path.
