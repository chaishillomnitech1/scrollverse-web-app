# Phase 344 — First-Party Contemporaneous Capability Archive

## Purpose and reading guide

This archive gives the owner’s account, public timestamps, local project records, and external-evidence gaps distinct places. It is built for inspection: readers can see what the owner states, what a public or local source records, what is available now, and what would need further evidence.

> **Owner-stated perspective:** The owner states that certain capabilities were accomplished at the same time that outside videos and narratives described comparable possibilities, and that the linked media, GitHub work, and project records should be considered together as a contemporaneous creative and technical record.

This statement is recorded as the owner’s first-party account. It is not converted into a claim about another creator’s intent, government knowledge, third-party coordination, or an external outcome unless a source independently documents that outcome.

## Evidence record model

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| First-party account | What the owner says was created, experienced, intended, or connected. |
| Source and date | A public URL, local file, test output, screenshot, export, or versioned record with the relevant date or date limitation. |
| Evidence category | **Documented local result**, **public source statement**, **first-party perspective**, or **unverified external claim**. |
| Operating state | **Available now**, **research**, or **requires owner release**. |
| Correction path | The specific additional source, result, authorization, or review that could clarify the record. |
